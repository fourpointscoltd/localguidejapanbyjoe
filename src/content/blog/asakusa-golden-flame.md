> "I've never been been able to figure out what this is since I saw it..."

**you're talking about the Asahi Flame!** It’s definitely one of the quirky sights in Tokyo. Originally, it's meant to represent the burning heart of Asahi beer, but lots of locals and visitors fancy it looks more like a giant golden poop! Because of that, it’s humorously nicknamed the "Poop Building." But if you want to sound a bit more posh calling it, you might use "Golden Nimbus Cloud" instead. It’s right near Asakusa, so you can check it out when you visit Senso-ji Temple. It’s quite a sight, really!
