> "Where can I buy a katana sword in Tokyo?"

The most famous shop is **SEIYUDO**. But before you buy, please check some important things!

---

## Where to Buy

> "What's the best shop?"

**SEIYUDO**
- Most famous sword shop in Japan
- Real and replica swords available
- Check their website for details

**Other options:**
- Specialty shops in Asakusa
- Antique stores
- Some souvenir shops sell replicas

---

## Important Legal Information

> "Can I take it home?"

**Before buying, please check:**

1. **Airplane rules** – Can you bring it on your flight?
2. **Your country's laws** – Is it legal to import?
3. **Type of sword** – Real vs replica makes a difference

**In Japan:**
- Carrying a sword without a reason can lead to arrest
- Real swords require registration
- Replica/decorative swords are easier to handle

---

## Real vs Replica

> "What's the difference?"

**Real Japanese swords:**
- Very expensive (¥100,000 - millions)
- Require proper documentation
- Strict export rules
- True collector's items

**Replica/decorative swords:**
- Much cheaper (¥5,000 - ¥50,000)
- Easier to transport
- Still look beautiful
- Good for display

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Don't own real swords (very rare)
- See them in museums
- Buy decorative ones for display
- Understand the legal restrictions

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Research before buying** – Know your country's import laws

2. **Ask the shop** – They can advise on export

3. **Shipping might be easier** – Than carrying on a plane

4. **Museums are great** – See real swords without buying

5. **Samurai experiences** – Try sword-cutting experiences instead
