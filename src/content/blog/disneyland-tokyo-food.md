> "Hi, if we cannot reserve any restaurants inside Disneyland, do we have to wait long to buy food at any counters? We are planning to go in the end of July. All restaurants are fully booked. Thanks"

**Disneyland Tokyo is such a blast!** Yes, at the park, there are spots where you can book a table and others where you'll need to reserve for sure. But for many of the eateries, it’s first come, first served. If you time your meals a bit off the usual lunch or dinner hours, you might just breeze through with about a ten-minute wait. Hitting those off-peak times really makes a difference in cutting down your wait time. Enjoy all the yummy treats!
