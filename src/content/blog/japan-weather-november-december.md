 Im planning to visit Japan end of November to first week of December. What are the average temperatures around this time? What clothes should I take? Planning to visit Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka.
 TIA"

**Japan's temperatures in late November to early December really vary by region because of its diverse geography.** In Hokkaido, you'll find it quite snowy, signaling winter's start, while Okinawa enjoys weather that feels more like a cool summer. This makes packing a bit tricky! It's best to check the forecast for each area you plan to visit. But don’t stress about packing everything perfect—Japan has plenty of shops like Uniqlo where you can grab affordable clothes, gloves, and other essentials. Even convenience stores might surprise you with their range of useful items! So, pack light and adapt as you go.
