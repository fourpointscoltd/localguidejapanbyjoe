> "After eating at a restaurant, is it okay to take home what I didn't eat?"

**It's not very common in Japan.** Let me explain why.

---

## Why Takeout Is Rare

> "Why can't I take leftovers home?"

**In Japan, there is a culture of eating fresh food.**

Many restaurants do not allow takeout because:
- Liability issues if food goes bad
- Risk of food poisoning
- Quality concerns

**You should order with the understanding that you won't take it home.**

---

## What You Can Do

> "Is there any way to take food home?"

**Some options:**
- Ask before ordering (some places allow it)
- Some places that sell boxed lunches may give you a container
- If you really want to, put it in your own bag

But honestly, it's better to order less than you think!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Order carefully to avoid waste
- Eat everything on the plate
- Understand takeout is not common
- Know which restaurants allow it

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Order less** – You can always order more

2. **Ask first** – Some places will allow takeout

3. **Bento shops are different** – They expect takeout

4. **Don't be offended** – It's about food safety

5. **Convenience stores** – Great for small portions
