> "Internet /SIM card, which is the best way to have unlimited internet and be able to use my phone whilst there?"

**When you're in Japan, staying connected is important.** Free Wi-Fi isn't that common outside cafes and hotels, so it's good to plan ahead. I recommend getting a SIM card for your phone. This option usually works well, but keep in mind that “unlimited” plans may have speed limits after certain usage. It's smart to check the details before you choose.

---

## Details

For reliable options, I often use Airalo, but you can also check out Sakura Mobile. They have different unlimited plans that you might find useful. Just visit their website at https://www.sakuramobile.jp/ to see what fits your needs. Make sure your phone is unlocked before you get a SIM card, so you can easily switch over!
