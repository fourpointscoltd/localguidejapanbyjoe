 Just curious if it's possible to rent drones in Tokyo."

**Sure, you can rent drones in Tokyo!** You'll find a few shops that offer this, but remember, whether foreigners can rent them might depend on the shop, so it's a good idea to check ahead. Prices usually range from about 4,500 to 25,000 yen for a night or two. Also, Japan's pretty strict about where and how you can fly drones, requiring a license and special permissions. Before you plan on flying one, definitely brush up on the rules—you can find more info on the regulations and rental details at https://naka4.com/drone/english/. Super important to stay informed!
