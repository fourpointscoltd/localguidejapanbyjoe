> "I'm looking for fresh yuzu fruit in Tokyo. Any recommendations?"

Yuzu can be tricky to find because it's **seasonal**. But I know some good spots!

---

## Where to Buy Yuzu

> "Which stores have it?"

**Supermarkets in touristy areas:**

- **LIFE** in Asakusa – Popular supermarket, good selection
- **OK Store** in Ginza – Locals love this discount supermarket

Both are regular Japanese supermarkets where you can buy fresh food at local prices.

---

## Seasonal Availability

> "When is yuzu in season?"

**Winter is the best time** (November - January)

During winter, yuzu is much easier to find because Japanese people use it for **yuzu bath** (yuzu-yu). This is a tradition where you put whole yuzu fruits in your bath – it smells amazing and is said to ward off colds.

Outside of winter, fresh yuzu is harder to find.

---

## Alternatives

> "What if I can't find fresh yuzu?"

If fresh yuzu isn't available:
- **Yuzu juice** – Bottled, available year-round
- **Yuzu kosho** – Yuzu pepper paste, great souvenir
- **Yuzu products** – Snacks, drinks, skincare

These are available at most supermarkets and souvenir shops.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Buy yuzu in winter for yuzu bath
- Use yuzu juice for cooking year-round
- Don't eat fresh yuzu as a fruit (it's too sour!)
- Add yuzu zest to dishes for flavor

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Check the season** – Winter = easy to find

2. **Department store basements** – High-quality produce

3. **Supermarkets are fun** – Great for seeing local food

4. **Yuzu bath sets** – Sold at bath product shops

5. **Yuzu souvenirs** – Kit Kat, snacks, and more
