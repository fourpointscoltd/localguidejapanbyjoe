> "What Are the Best Stationery Stores in Tokyo for Kids?"

### Conclusion

If you love stationery, **Tokyo is a dream come true**.
For famous shops, go to **Itoya in Ginza**. For unique or artistic finds, explore **Kuramae** near Asakusa, where you’ll find shops like **Kakimori** and **mt lab.** ✏️📓

---
### General Information

Japanese stationery is known worldwide for its **high quality, attention to detail, and beautiful design**.
Whether your daughter loves pens, notebooks, washi tape, or paper crafts, Tokyo offers countless stores to explore.

Even everyday bookstores and variety shops carry amazing stationery — so you don’t need to go far to find something cute or practical.

---
### 🏆 Top Stationery Shops in Tokyo

#### ✨ Itoya (Ginza)

- Tokyo’s most **iconic stationery store**
- Founded in 1904, with 12 floors of paper goods, pens, art supplies, and gifts
- Great for both school supplies and high-end fountain pens
- **Location**: 5-minute walk from Ginza Station
- Website: [https://www.ito-ya.co.jp/](https://www.ito-ya.co.jp/)

💡 *Highly recommended if you have time — it’s like a stationery theme park!*

#### 🖋️ Kakimori (Kuramae, near Asakusa)

- Boutique-style shop known for its **custom notebooks and fountain pens**
- Design your own cover and binding
- Great selection of inks and writing tools
- **Location**: Kuramae Station (Toei Asakusa Line or Oedo Line)
- Website: [https://kakimori.com](https://kakimori.com)

[kakimori · 1 Chome-6-2 Misuji, Taito City, Tokyo 111-0055, Japan★★★★★ · Kantoorboekhandel![](https://www.localguide-japan.com/content/images/icon/maps15_bnuw3a_32dp-28.ico)kakimori · 1 Chome-6-2 Misuji, Taito City, Tokyo 111-0055, Japan![](https://www.localguide-japan.com/content/images/thumbnail/AF1QipP_oUw8_nJwtVMlKktBN3fI-dRG0bOUMLfS_DC4-w900-h900-p-k-no)](https://t.co/tDxareVmS5)

💡 *If your daughter loves journaling or calligraphy, this is a must-visit.*

#### 🎨 mt lab. (Kuramae)

- **Specialty shop for washi tape** by the mt brand
- Limited-edition designs and fun displays
- **Location**: Close to Kakimori in Kuramae
- Website: https://www.masking-tape.jp/lab/

💡 *A paradise for washi tape lovers — some designs are exclusive to the store!*

#### 📄 TAKEO Paper Shops (Jimbocho & Omotesando)

- High-end paper store offering beautiful textures and colors
- Fun to browse even if you’re not buying
- **Jimbocho location** is great for book and paper lovers
- **Omotesando store** is more modern and stylish
- Website: [https://www.takeo.co.jp](https://www.takeo.co.jp)

### 🛒 Other Easy-to-Find Stores

If you don’t have time to explore specialized shops, don’t worry — these chains also carry excellent stationery:

- **Loft**: Trendy department store with lots of notebooks, pens, and Japanese kawaii goods
- **Tokyu Hands**: Huge variety store with great stationery sections
- **Bookstores (like Tsutaya, Kinokuniya)**: Often have stationery corners near the entrance

💡 *Most big stations like Shibuya, Shinjuku, and Ikebukuro have Loft or Tokyu Hands nearby.*

---
### My Personal Tip

Japanese stationery is not just cute — it’s **amazingly functional**.
Even if you buy a simple pen, it often feels smoother and lasts longer than expected.

If you have time for only one store, **go to Itoya in Ginza**.
But if you want something unique or handmade, **head to Kuramae** — it’s quieter, local, and full of hidden gems.

Kuramae also has cute cafés and design stores, so you can **make it a relaxing afternoon trip** ☕🛍️

---
### Travel Tips

- **Tax-free available** at most larger shops (bring your passport)
- **IC cards like Suica** can be used to pay at most stores
- **Check store hours** — many close by 8 PM
- **Some stores offer gift wrapping** — perfect for presents
- **Bring a small bag** — you might end up buying more than expected!
