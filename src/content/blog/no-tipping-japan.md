> "Do I need to tip waiters and waitresses in Japan?"

**No! In Japan, there is no custom of giving tips.**

---

## Is Tipping Rude?

> "I heard tipping is rude in Japan?"

**It's NOT rude, just unnecessary!**

Some information says tipping is rude, but that's not quite right:
- Japanese people know about tipping culture
- They would be happy if you said "it's a tip"
- It's just not expected or required

---

## Why No Tipping?

> "How does it work without tips?"

**Service is included in the price!**

- Staff get regular salaries
- Service quality is high anyway
- No need to calculate tips

**You can receive the best service even without giving tips.**

---

## Special Cases

> "Are there any exceptions?"

**Some restaurants may have:**
- Tip boxes (rare)
- Service charges already added

**Note:** Some restaurants don't accept tips because it creates accounting problems.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Never tip
- Know service is excellent without tips
- Understand tourists might try to tip
- Don't think it's rude

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Don't worry about tips** – Not expected

2. **Service is great** – Without any tips

3. **Prices include service** – Already factored in

4. **Say thank you** – That's appreciated more!

5. **Relax and enjoy** – One less thing to think about
