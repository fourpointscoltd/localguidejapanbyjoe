> "Got few 2k yen bills. Still valid in Japan? I heard this 2k bill has been discontinued."

**Yes, 2000 yen bills are still valid in Japan, but they are quite rare!** They were first issued in the year 2000 to celebrate the new millennium. You might find using them a bit tricky, though. While you can definitely use them at places like convenience stores or when paying someone directly, many vending machines and automated ticket machines may not accept them. If you happen to have a 2000 yen bill and find it inconvenient, no worries! Just take it to any bank, and they can easily exchange it for you or give you smaller denominations that are more commonly used. Remember, all types of legal currency, including special edition bills like the 2000 yen note, are still technically valid for transactions.
