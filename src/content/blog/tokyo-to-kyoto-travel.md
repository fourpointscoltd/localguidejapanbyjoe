> "Have you flown from Tokyo to Kyoto and if so, how was it?"

I've done it both ways — flying and Shinkansen. **The Shinkansen is better in almost every case.** Here's why:

## Shinkansen vs Flying: The Comparison

| | Shinkansen | Flying |
|---|---|---|
| **Travel time** | 2 hours 15 min (Nozomi) | 4-5 hours total (airport + flight + transfer) |
| **Price** | ~¥13,320 one way | ~¥5,000-15,000 (varies wildly) |
| **Station → City center** | Tokyo Station → Kyoto Station (direct) | Haneda → Itami/Kansai → bus/train to Kyoto |
| **Frequency** | Every 10-15 minutes | A few flights per day |
| **Luggage** | No restrictions, no check-in | Standard airline rules |
| **Experience** | Mt. Fuji views, ekiben bento | Generic flight |

## My Recommendation

**Take the Shinkansen.** It's door-to-door faster because:

1. **No airport security** — just walk on the train
2. **Tokyo Station and Kyoto Station are in the city center** — no 1-hour airport transfers
3. **Trains leave every 10-15 minutes** — miss one? Catch the next
4. **Mt. Fuji view** — sit on the right side (seat E) going to Kyoto for the best view

## When Flying Makes Sense

- You have a **JR Pass** that doesn't cover Nozomi (use Hikari instead — 2h40m)
- You're flying **from another city** and connecting through Tokyo
- You found an extremely cheap flight on **Peach, Jetstar, or ZipAir** (sometimes under ¥5,000)

## How to Book Shinkansen Tickets

- **At the station:** JR ticket counter or ticket machines (English available)
- **Online:** [SmartEX app](https://smart-ex.jp/en/) — book and use your IC card or phone as ticket
- **JR Pass holders:** Reserve seats at any JR ticket counter (free with pass)

## Pro Tips

- **Reserved seats** (指定席) are worth the extra ¥500-1,000 during peak season
- **Ekiben** (station bento boxes) are a Shinkansen tradition — buy one at Tokyo Station before boarding. Tokyo Station has over 200 varieties!
- **Green Car** (first class) is luxurious but not necessary for a 2-hour ride
