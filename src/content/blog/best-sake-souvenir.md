> "What's a good sake to bring home as a gift?"

**Great question!** Here are my top recommendations.

---

## The Best 3 Sake in Japan

> "Which brands should I buy?"

**Top recommendations:**
1. **Dassai** – Very famous, smooth taste
2. **Hakkaisan** – Clean and refreshing
3. **Juyondai** – Premium and rare

These are known even among people who don't drink much sake!

---

## Want to Try Many Types?

> "How can I taste different sake?"

**Go to a sake-genka bar!**

At these bars:
- Taste many types with small cups
- Learn about different flavors
- Find your favorite style

Some sake are sweet, some are savory. Try many while you're in Japan!

---

## Other Alcohol Options

> "What else is good?"

**Other recommendations:**
- **Local beer** – Each region has specialties
- **Hibiki** – Famous Japanese whisky
- **Yamanashi wine** – From the Mt. Fuji area

Note: If you're from Europe, Japanese wine might not impress you as much.

---

## Fun Sake Products

> "Anything unique?"

**These days, there's even sake ice cream!**

Sake lovers will have a great time exploring all the varieties.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Have favorite sake brands
- Buy local sake when traveling
- Know the best regional varieties
- Give sake as gifts

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Dassai is everywhere** – Easy to find

2. **Try at bars first** – Then buy your favorite

3. **Check airline rules** – For bringing liquids home

4. **Airport has good selection** – Last-minute shopping

5. **Ask locals** – We love recommending sake
