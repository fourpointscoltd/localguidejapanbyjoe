Airport limousine buses in Japan are a convenient and comfortable way to travel between airports and major city areas. Here's everything you need to know!

## How They Work

1. **Purchase tickets** at the airport bus counter or online
2. **Check the schedule** for your destination
3. **Load your luggage** - staff will help store it under the bus
4. **Sit back and relax** - direct service to major hotels and stations

## Reservations

**For departures from airports:** Generally **no reservation needed**. Buses run frequently, and you can just buy a ticket and hop on the next available bus.

**For departures to airports:** **Reservations recommended**, especially during:
- Rush hours (morning departures)
- Holiday periods
- Popular routes

## Advantages

- **Comfortable seats** with legroom
- **Direct routes** to major hotels
- **Luggage storage** included
- **No transfers** required
- **Wi-Fi** on most buses

## How to Book

1. **Online**: Airport Limousine website
2. **At the airport**: Bus ticket counters
3. **At hotels**: Some hotels sell tickets

The limousine bus is my go-to recommendation for travelers with heavy luggage who want a stress-free journey!
