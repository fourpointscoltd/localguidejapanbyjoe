#### **Conclusion**

Finding fresh yuzu in Tokyo can be difficult outside of its peak season (November–December). However, you can still enjoy yuzu through bottled juice, sauces, or even a **traditional yuzu bath (yuzuburo)**.

#### **General Information**

Yuzu is a fragrant Japanese citrus fruit with a tart, slightly floral aroma. It’s mainly used in cooking, but it also plays a role in traditional customs. Fresh yuzu is typically harvested in late autumn and early winter, making it hard to find during other seasons.

#### **Best Option Based on Availability**

- **If you want fresh yuzu:** Try visiting supermarkets like **LIFE (Asakusa)** or **OK Store (Ginza)**, where fresh produce is available at reasonable prices.
- **If fresh yuzu is unavailable:** Look for **bottled yuzu juice, yuzu kosho (spicy yuzu paste), or yuzu-flavored products** at supermarkets and specialty stores.
- **If you want a unique experience:** Try a **yuzu bath (yuzuburo)** at an onsen or buy yuzu to use in your own bath.

#### **What Is a Yuzu Bath (Yuzuburo)?**

In Japan, **yuzu baths** are a traditional custom during the **winter solstice (Toji, around December 21st)**. People place whole yuzu fruits into hot baths, releasing their refreshing citrus aroma. This is believed to **warm the body, improve circulation, and ward off colds**. Many **onsen (hot springs) and sento (public baths) offer yuzu baths** during this time.

**Where to Try a Yuzu Bath in Tokyo:**

- **Public baths & onsen:** Some Tokyo bathhouses, like **Thermae-Yu in Shinjuku**, offer seasonal yuzu baths.
- **At home:** If you find fresh yuzu, simply float them in your bathtub and enjoy the soothing scent.

#### **Best Options by Season**

- **Winter (best time for fresh yuzu & yuzu baths!)** – Enjoy fresh yuzu and traditional **yuzu baths on the winter solstice**.
- **Spring–Summer** – Fresh yuzu is rare, but bottled juice and yuzu kosho are available year-round.
- **Autumn** – Yuzu starts appearing in markets from November.

#### **Unexpected Factors & Disadvantages**

- **Fresh yuzu is seasonal.** If visiting outside winter, you may not find it easily.
- **Not all supermarkets stock fresh yuzu.** Visit larger stores or specialty markets for a better chance.

#### **Extra Travel Tips**

- **Want fresh yuzu?** Try **LIFE Asakusa** or **OK Store Ginza**.
- **Love Japanese citrus flavors?** Consider trying **sudachi** or **kabosu** as alternatives.
