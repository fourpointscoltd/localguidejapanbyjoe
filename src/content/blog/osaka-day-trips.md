> "Hi evervone
 I'm planning to take day trips from Osaka have around 7 days in Osaka to do day trips
 Planning to go to
 Nara
 Hiroshima
 Kobe
 Any other suggestions?
 Also is it better to stay near Shin Osaka station or Namba station to go to those day trips
 TIA"

**Absolutely, heading out from Osaka gives you some really cool options depending on what you fancy!** Kyoto is super close and absolutely packed with culture—think stunning temples and old-timey streets. If nature's more your thing, hop over to Awaji Island for beautiful scenery and parks. And if you're looking to unwind, you've got to check out the hot springs near Kobe. Arima Onsen and Kinosaki Onsen are like the hotspots for a relaxing dip. Each place has its own charm, so you really can't go wrong with any choice!
