#### **Conclusion**

Yes! If you're looking for **kid-friendly arcades and easier claw machines** in Tokyo, we recommend visiting **Molly Fantasy**, a game center located inside **Aeon malls**. These places are designed for children, making the claw machines more fun and approachable for families—much easier than the ones in Akihabara or Shibuya, which are geared more toward adults.

#### **General Information**

In Japan, claw machines are commonly called **“UFO catchers” (UFOキャッチャー)**. They're extremely popular and found in nearly every arcade. However, **many of the arcades in places like Akihabara or Shinjuku are aimed at teenagers and adults**, with complex machines and prizes like anime figures, electronics, or collectibles.

For kids, these machines can be **too hard** or the prizes might not be very exciting for them. That’s why **Molly Fantasy** is a great option—it’s a **child-focused arcade** with colorful interiors, easy games, and adorable, age-appropriate prizes.

#### **Why Molly Fantasy Is Great for Kids**

- Located inside **Aeon Shopping Malls**, often next to food courts and family services
- **UFO catchers are easier** and designed to be winnable for children
- Prizes include **plushies, character toys, and sweets**—perfect for young kids
- Safe, clean, and quieter compared to arcades in Akihabara or Shibuya
- Often have **coin rides and mini-attractions** for toddlers

#### **Claw Machine Tips for Beginners**

- **Yes, there’s a trick to it!** Even adults need to practice.
- Many prizes are intentionally **positioned to look easy but are hard to grab.**
- Watch a few **YouTube videos** from experienced players—you’ll be surprised how useful they are!
- Sometimes, if you ask staff politely, they may help adjust the prize’s position!
- Don’t forget: **UFO catchers are about timing and angles**, not just grabbing power.
