> "Your fayourite thing to eat/drink from 7/11..."

**you have to try the sandwiches and onigiri at 7/11 in Japan; they're so fresh and tasty!** If you’re in the mood for something a bit different, grab the Nana Chicken. It’s super flavorful. And don't forget to try the Seven Coffee – perfect for a quick pick-me-up. Here's a fun tip: if you spot Iida Shouten's soy sauce ramen cup noodles, definitely give them a try. They might not always be around, but they’re absolutely worth trying when you find them. Simple, but oh so delicious!
