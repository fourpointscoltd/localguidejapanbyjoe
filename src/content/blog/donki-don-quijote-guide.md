> "What is Don Quijote? Everyone says I should go."

**It's a discount variety store!** Cramped, chaotic, and surprisingly addictive.

---

## What Is Donki?

> "What do they sell?"

**Everything:**
- Snacks and food
- Cosmetics and beauty
- Electronics
- Costumes and party goods
- Souvenirs
- Luggage
- Household items
- Even luxury brands!

**24 hours** – Many locations never close!

---

## Best Things to Buy

> "What should I get?"

**For souvenirs:**
- Japanese snacks
- Kit Kat varieties
- Beauty products
- Character goods
- Japanese candy

**For yourself:**
- Electronics (check voltage)
- Cosmetics
- Unique Japanese items

---

## Navigating the Chaos

> "It's so overwhelming!"

**Tips:**
- Take your time
- Look up and down – items everywhere
- Ask staff if you can't find something
- Check prices – not everything is cheap
- Multiple floors have different items

---

## Tax-Free Shopping

> "Can I get tax free?"

**Yes!**
- Spend over ¥5,500
- Bring passport
- Look for tax-free counter
- Items sealed in bag

---

## Famous Locations

> "Which store should I visit?"

**Popular locations:**
- **Shibuya Mega Donki** – Huge!
- **Shinjuku** – Multiple stores
- **Akihabara** – Good for electronics
- **Osaka Dotonbori** – Giant ferris wheel on building

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Shop late night for convenience
- Know it's not always cheapest
- Buy specific items they know are good deals
- Find it fun to browse

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Go late** – Less crowded at night

2. **Compare prices** – Not always the best deal

3. **The song** – Get ready for the jingle stuck in your head!

4. **Staff help** – Ask them to find items

5. **Budget time** – You'll spend longer than expected
