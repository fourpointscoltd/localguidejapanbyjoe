> "Hey!
 need some help deciding if it's a good idea to invite my boyfriend to Japan. He is has lots of tattoos but I heard it's not really accepted in Japan. Is it a good idea to still invite him? Will he still be able to participate in lots of things, hot springs, entertainment, food, etc? What is the worst case downside, I don't want him to feel rejected from restaurants and fun things to do Thanks!"

**This is a common question!** In short, yes, you should definitely bring your tattooed boyfriend to Japan. Many people with tattoos visit without any problems. The main places that can be a concern are public baths, known as onsen. Tattoos are often banned there because of some cultural views. But don’t worry; you can still enjoy the trip together!

---

## Details

If you really want to experience an onsen, consider booking a private onsen. These are usually reserved just for you and your group, so your boyfriend can enjoy it without issues. The prices are generally not very high, and it’s a great way to relax together. So, go ahead and plan that trip! Japan has so much to offer for both of you.
