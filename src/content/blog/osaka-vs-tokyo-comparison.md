> "What's the difference between Osaka and Tokyo?"

**They're like different countries!** Both are great but have very different vibes.

---

## Personality

> "How are the people different?"

**Tokyo:**
- More reserved
- Formal and polite
- Fast-paced
- Fashion-conscious
- Business-focused

**Osaka:**
- Outgoing and friendly
- Humor is important
- More relaxed
- Direct communication
- Food and fun focused

---

## Food Culture

> "What about the food?"

**Tokyo:**
- High-end dining capital
- Sushi is king
- Refined and elegant
- Most Michelin stars

**Osaka:**
- "Kitchen of Japan"
- Street food paradise
- Takoyaki and okonomiyaki
- Eat until you drop!

**Famous saying:** In Tokyo, people spend on fashion. In Osaka, people spend on food.

---

## Cost

> "Which is cheaper?"

**Osaka is generally cheaper:**
- Hotels
- Food
- Entertainment
- Shopping

**Tokyo:**
- More expensive overall
- But has budget options too

---

## Things to Do

> "What's unique to each?"

**Only in Tokyo:**
- Shibuya Crossing
- Akihabara anime culture
- Harajuku fashion
- Government area

**Only in Osaka:**
- Dotonbori neon
- Osaka Castle
- Universal Studios Japan
- Comedy culture

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Have friendly rivalry between cities
- Know the stereotypes
- Appreciate both cities
- Choose based on preference

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Visit both** – Shinkansen is only 2.5 hours

2. **Osaka accent** – Sounds different from Tokyo

3. **Osaka for food** – Street food is amazing

4. **Tokyo for variety** – More neighborhoods to explore

5. **Base yourself** – Stay in one, day trip to the other
