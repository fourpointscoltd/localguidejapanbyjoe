## Why Visit Ramen Street at Tokyo Station?

If you're traveling through Tokyo Station and craving authentic Japanese ramen, **Ramen Street** is the perfect place to visit. Located in **Tokyo Station First Avenue**, this underground ramen hub offers some of the best ramen experiences in Japan. The taste is just as good as at the original locations, making it a must-visit spot before catching the **Shinkansen** or when you're in the area for business.

## Types of Ramen You Can Try

Ramen comes in a variety of flavors and styles. Some of the most popular ramen types include:

- **Shoyu (Soy Sauce) Ramen** – A classic soy-based broth with a rich umami taste.
- **Shio (Salt) Ramen** – A lighter broth with a clean and delicate flavor.
- **Miso Ramen** – A deep, flavorful broth made from fermented soybean paste.
- **Tonkotsu Ramen** – A creamy, pork bone-based broth popular in southern Japan.
- **Tsukemen (Dipping Noodles)** – Thick noodles served separately from the broth for dipping.
- **Abura Soba (Oil Noodles)** – Ramen without broth, coated in a flavorful sauce.
- **Taiwan Mazesoba (Taiwan-style Mixed Noodles)** – Spicy, dry-style ramen topped with minced meat.

For foreigners, **shoyu or shio ramen** might be the easiest to try, as **miso and tonkotsu ramen** can be a bit rich in flavor. However, if you're a ramen lover, it's worth trying different types to find your favorite.

## Best Ramen Shops at Tokyo Ramen Street

If you’re unsure where to start, here are two top ramen shops you should try:

### 1. **Soranoiro Nippon** (ソラノイロ・NIPPON)

- **Location:** Tokyo Station First Avenue B1F, Ramen Street
- **Google Maps:** [Soranoiro Nippon](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Mg19zZCFjjch6RfM8)
- **Why Try It?** Soranoiro offers unique ramen options, including **vegetable-based ramen** that is perfect for health-conscious diners. Their signature dish features a colorful vegetable broth that is both light and flavorful.

### 2. **Rokurinsha** (六厘舎)

- **Location:** Tokyo Station First Avenue B1F, Ramen Street
- **Google Maps:** [Rokurinsha](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VxvJURVCwKZb1F8v5)
