Great question! Japanese convenience stores like 7-11 have a slightly different system for their frozen fruit smoothies than you might expect.

## How It Works

1. **Grab the frozen smoothie pack** from the freezer
2. **Take it to the register** and pay first
3. **Staff will hand you a cup** with the machine
4. **Go to the smoothie machine** and blend it yourself
5. **Enjoy!**

## Step-by-Step

### At the Register
- Pay for the smoothie (usually ¥200-300)
- Receive a special cup
- Staff may point you to the machine

### At the Machine
- Place cup under dispenser
- Insert the frozen pack
- Press the button
- Wait about 30-60 seconds
- Done!

## Important Notes

- **Pay BEFORE opening** - Unlike drinks you might open and pay for later in some countries
- **One cup per purchase** - The cup is part of what you're buying
- **Flavors rotate** - Check what's available in the freezer
- **Popular flavors** - Mango, mixed berry, banana

## Why This System?

Japanese convenience stores are designed for efficiency. By paying first:
- Staff can focus on other customers
- No confusion about unpaid items
- Machine area stays organized

## Pro Tip

These smoothies are **delicious** and perfect for hot summer days! The frozen fruit pack system means they're always fresh and not watered down.

Looking for my favorite? The **mango smoothie** is amazing!
