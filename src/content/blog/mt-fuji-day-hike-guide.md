Yes, I've done the classic day hike starting from the 5th Station on Mt. Fuji, and honestly, it's quite the challenge but totally doable if you prepare well!

## Time Estimates

The time it takes really depends on which route you pick:

| Route | Ascent | Descent | Total |
|-------|--------|---------|-------|
| Yoshida | 5-7 hrs | 3-4 hrs | 8-11 hrs |
| Subashiri | 5-8 hrs | 3-4 hrs | 8-12 hrs |
| Gotemba | 7-10 hrs | 3-5 hrs | 10-15 hrs |
| Fujinomiya | 4-7 hrs | 2-4 hrs | 6-11 hrs |

## My Recommendation

The **Yoshida Trail** is the most popular and well-maintained. For a day hike:

1. **Start EARLY** - Leave 5th Station by 4-5 AM
2. **Pace yourself** - The altitude affects everyone
3. **Take breaks** - Mountain huts sell snacks and drinks
4. **Watch the time** - Last buses leave around 7-8 PM

## Essential Preparation

- **Physical fitness** - Train beforehand with hill walks
- **Proper gear** - Layers, rain jacket, headlamp
- **Food & water** - Bring snacks; hut prices are high
- **Cash** - Mountain huts don't take cards
- **Altitude awareness** - Ascend slowly to avoid sickness

## Climbing Season

Mt. Fuji is only open for climbing from **early July to early September**. Outside this period, trails are closed and extremely dangerous.

## Is It Worth It?

Absolutely! Reaching the summit and watching the sunrise (goraiko) is unforgettable. Just respect the mountain and prepare properly!
