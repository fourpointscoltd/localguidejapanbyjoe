> "I'm a hairstylist. Where can I buy Hatori Hanzo shears in Tokyo?"

**Great news - you can buy professional Japanese shears in Tokyo without special membership!**

---

## Where to Buy

> "Do I need a Member ID or special access?"

**Mizutani Scissors Showroom**
- Walk-in welcome, no appointment needed
- No Member ID required
- Staff speaks some English
- You can try before you buy
- Located in central Tokyo

**Hatori Hanzo shears** specifically may require ordering through their official channels, but Mizutani is one of Japan's top professional scissor makers and sells directly.

---

## Other Options for Professional Shears

> "Where else can I look?"

**Kappabashi Street (合羽橋)**
- Tokyo's famous kitchen/tool district
- Several shops carry professional scissors
- Can find various Japanese steel brands
- Near Asakusa

**Tokyu Hands / Loft**
- Carry some professional-grade scissors
- Easier to navigate in English
- Tax-free shopping available

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most Japanese hairstylists:
- Buy directly from manufacturer showrooms
- Attend professional beauty trade shows
- Order through their salon's supplier
- Consider scissors a lifetime investment

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Bring your budget** - Professional Japanese shears range ¥30,000-200,000+

2. **Tax-free shopping** - Show your passport for tax exemption

3. **Ask about maintenance** - Some shops offer sharpening services

4. **Check airline rules** - Pack shears in checked luggage, not carry-on

5. **Get a warranty card** - Important for future maintenance
