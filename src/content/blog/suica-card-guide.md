> "Hello Travelling to Tokyo in December fo 7 people. Wondering for Suica Card (For Japan Train) do we need to have suica card per person? Or can we just have one or two and tap it per person? Thanks a lot for answering my question"

**Yes, each person needs their own Suica card when traveling on trains in Japan.** It’s super convenient because you just tap it at the gate and go. You can buy a Welcome Suica card right at the airport when you land. This card is especially designed for visitors, so it's perfect if you're only staying for a short time. Plus, you don’t have to worry about a deposit! Just grab one for each person, and you’ll be all set to zip around exploring.
