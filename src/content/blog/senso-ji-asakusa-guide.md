> "How do I visit Senso-ji? What should I see?"

**Senso-ji is Tokyo's most famous temple!** It's beautiful, historic, and full of things to explore.

---

## Basic Information

> "What is Senso-ji?"

**About:**
- Tokyo's oldest temple (founded 645 AD!)
- Buddhist temple
- Free to enter
- Very popular with tourists and locals

**Location:** Asakusa – easy to reach by subway.

---

## What to See

> "What are the highlights?"

**Must-see spots:**
- **Kaminarimon Gate** – Famous red lantern
- **Nakamise Street** – Shopping street to temple
- **Hozomon Gate** – Inner gate
- **Main Hall** – The temple itself
- **Five-Story Pagoda** – Beautiful landmark

---

## Nakamise Shopping Street

> "What can I buy?"

**Traditional souvenirs:**
- Ningyo-yaki (small cakes)
- Sensu (folding fans)
- Tenugui (cotton towels)
- Snacks and sweets
- Chopsticks
- Japanese crafts

**Great for gifts!**

---

## Temple Etiquette

> "What should I do at the temple?"

**At the main hall:**
1. Throw a coin in the offering box
2. Bow and pray
3. No clapping (it's Buddhist, not Shinto)

**Incense:** Waft the smoke over yourself for good health!

---

## Best Time to Visit

> "When should I go?"

**Recommended:**
- Early morning – Less crowded, peaceful
- Evening – Beautiful when lit up
- Weekdays – Fewer people

**Avoid:**
- Weekend afternoons
- Holidays

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit during New Year (very crowded!)
- Buy omamori (protective charms)
- Draw omikuji (fortune papers)
- Enjoy Nakamise shopping

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Go early** – Much better experience

2. **Omikuji** – Draw your fortune (¥100)

3. **Nearby attractions** – Tokyo Skytree is visible

4. **Night illumination** – Beautiful after dark

5. **Combine with area** – Explore Asakusa streets too
