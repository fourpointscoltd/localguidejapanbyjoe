> "Are there bugs and mosquitoes in Tokyo in August? Should I be worried?"

Yes, there are insects in Tokyo, but **not as many as in Southeast Asia**. And in clean city areas, you'll rarely see them. Let me give you the real picture.

---

## Insects You Might See

> "What kinds of bugs are in Tokyo?"

Here's what exists:
- **Mosquitoes** – But rare in urban areas
- **Cockroaches** – Mostly inside buildings, not outside
- **Flies** – Near food areas
- **Cicadas** – Very loud in summer, but harmless!

I've lived in Ginza for years and haven't been bitten by a single mosquito. Urban areas are very clean.

---

## What Tourists Usually Experience

> "Will I actually encounter them?"

**In city centers (Shibuya, Shinjuku, Ginza):**
- Mosquitoes are almost unheard of
- You might hear cicadas but won't see many bugs
- Hotels and restaurants are very clean

**In parks or nature areas:**
- More chance of mosquitoes
- Bring bug spray if you're hiking

**Near water or old buildings:**
- Slightly higher chance of insects

---

## What About Cockroaches?

> "I'm really scared of cockroaches..."

Cockroaches exist but:
- They're mostly **inside old buildings**
- Hotels and restaurants deal with them professionally
- Tourists rarely see them
- They're not outside on the streets

If you stay in modern hotels, you probably won't see one.

---

## The Only Things to Watch For

> "Is anything actually dangerous?"

The only things to be careful about (mostly in rural areas):
- **Bees** – Especially in mountains
- **Snakes** – Very rare, mostly in countryside

But even these almost never cause serious problems. Japan is very safe!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Don't worry much about insects in cities
- Use bug spray for camping or hiking
- Keep windows closed with AC in summer
- Consider cicada noise just "summer sounds"

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **City centers are fine** – Don't stress about bugs in Tokyo

2. **Bring bug spray for nature trips** – Hiking, camping, rural areas

3. **AC is everywhere** – Hotels, trains, and shops are all air-conditioned

4. **Cicadas are loud but harmless** – They're just singing!

5. **Night markets may have flies** – Normal for outdoor food areas

6. **Konbini sells everything** – Bug spray, cooling sprays, all available
