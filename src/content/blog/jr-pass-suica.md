> "Why do I need both a JR Pass and Suica card?"

Great question — these two cards serve completely different purposes. Think of it this way: **JR Pass is for long-distance travel, Suica is for daily convenience.**

## What Each Card Does

### JR Pass (Japan Rail Pass)
- **What it covers:** Unlimited rides on JR trains nationwide, including most Shinkansen (bullet trains)
- **Best for:** Multi-city trips (Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka → Hiroshima)
- **Duration:** 7, 14, or 21 consecutive days
- **Cost:** ¥50,000 / ¥80,000 / ¥100,000 (7/14/21 days)
- **Does NOT cover:** Tokyo Metro, private railways, buses, convenience stores

### Suica / PASMO (IC Card)
- **What it covers:** All trains (JR, Metro, private lines), buses, and payments at stores
- **Best for:** Daily getting around within a city + small purchases
- **Cost:** ¥500 deposit + whatever you charge (rechargeable)
- **Bonus:** Tap to pay at convenience stores, vending machines, restaurants, coin lockers

## Why You Need Both

| Situation | Which to Use |
|---|---|
| Tokyo → Kyoto (Shinkansen) | **JR Pass** |
| Kyoto Station → Fushimi Inari (JR local) | **JR Pass** |
| Shibuya → Asakusa (Tokyo Metro) | **Suica** |
| Buying a drink at 7-Eleven | **Suica** |
| Tokyo → Hakone (private railway) | **Suica** |
| Tokyo → Osaka (Shinkansen) | **JR Pass** |

## Do I Even Need a JR Pass?

**Not always.** The JR Pass is only worth it if you're making **long-distance trips**:

- **Tokyo + Kyoto + Osaka** (7-day pass) — Yes, worth it
- **Staying in Tokyo only** — No, just use Suica
- **Tokyo + one day trip** — Probably not worth it, buy individual tickets

**Quick math:** A round-trip Tokyo ↔ Kyoto Shinkansen costs **¥26,640**. A 7-day JR Pass is **¥50,000**. So you need at least one more long trip to break even.

## How to Get Each

### Suica
- **At the airport:** Look for "Welcome Suica" machines at Narita or Haneda — takes 2 minutes
- **On iPhone:** Add Suica directly to Apple Wallet (no physical card needed!)
- **Charge:** Any train station machine or convenience store

### JR Pass
- **Online:** [JR Pass official site](https://japanrailpass.net/) — order before your trip
- **At the airport:** JR ticket counters at Narita or Haneda
- **Activate:** Choose your start date when you pick it up

## My Setup

I always carry a **Suica on my iPhone** for daily use. When I'm taking visitors around Japan on multi-city trips, we get a **JR Pass** for the Shinkansen portions. The two cards complement each other perfectly.
