> "How do I visit Meiji Shrine? What should I know?"

**Meiji Shrine is Tokyo's most famous shrine!** It's peaceful, beautiful, and easy to visit from Harajuku.

---

## Basic Information

> "What is Meiji Shrine?"

**About:**
- Dedicated to Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken
- Built in 1920
- Surrounded by 170 acres of forest
- Free to enter

**Location:** Next to Harajuku Station – very convenient!

---

## What to See

> "What's inside?"

**Highlights:**
- Giant torii gates (wooden)
- Main shrine building
- Forest walking path
- Sake barrel display
- Wine barrel display (gift from France)
- Inner garden (fee required)

---

## Proper Etiquette

> "How should I behave?"

**At the torii gate:**
- Bow slightly before entering
- Walk on the sides, not center (reserved for gods)

**At the purification fountain:**
1. Take ladle with right hand, pour on left hand
2. Switch to left hand, pour on right hand
3. Pour water into left hand, rinse mouth
4. Clean ladle handle

**At the main shrine:**
1. Bow twice
2. Clap twice
3. Bow once
4. (Optional: toss coin, make a wish)

---

## Best Time to Visit

> "When should I go?"

**Recommended:**
- Early morning – Peaceful, fewer crowds
- Weekdays – Less busy
- New Year – Very crowded but festive

**Avoid:**
- Weekend afternoons
- Holidays

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit for New Year (Hatsumode)
- Have weddings at Meiji Shrine
- Walk through for the peaceful atmosphere
- Know the etiquette naturally

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Combine with Harajuku** – Right next door

2. **Free entry** – Inner garden costs extra

3. **Walking time** – 10-15 minutes from station to main shrine

4. **Ema boards** – Write wishes on wooden plaques

5. **Omamori** – Buy protective charms as souvenirs
