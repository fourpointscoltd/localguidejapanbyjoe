## Details

Japan’s summer humidity is no joke. Luckily, a cool escape is always nearby. Step into any konbini for instant air conditioning and essential supplies. They have tons of cold drinks, ice cream, and special cooling items. Locals love the "Gari Gari Kun" ice pop. Always drink plenty of water and try to avoid being in the direct sun for too long. Heatstroke is a serious risk; it causes headaches and dizziness that will ruin the next day of your trip. Grab some cooling body wipes or a spray to stay comfortable while you explore.
