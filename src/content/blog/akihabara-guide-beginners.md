> "What is Akihabara? What can I do there?"

**Akihabara is otaku paradise!** Anime, manga, games, and electronics all in one place.

---

## What Is Akihabara?

> "What's the area known for?"

**Famous for:**
- Anime and manga shops
- Video games
- Electronics
- Figure shops
- Maid cafes
- Arcade games

**Also called:** "Akiba" for short!

---

## Must-Visit Shops

> "Where should I go?"

**Anime/Manga:**
- **Mandarake** – Used goods, great prices
- **Animate** – New merchandise
- **Radio Kaikan** – Multiple floors of shops
- **Kotobukiya** – Figures

**Electronics:**
- **Yodobashi Camera** – Huge electronics store
- **Small shops** – For specific components

---

## Maid Cafes

> "What are maid cafes?"

**Unique experience:**
- Waitresses dressed as maids
- Call you "master" or "princess"
- Cute performances
- Themed food and drinks
- Definitely unique to Japan!

**Try it once** for the experience!

---

## Arcade Games

> "What about game centers?"

**Popular activities:**
- UFO catchers (claw machines)
- Rhythm games
- Fighting games
- Photo booths (purikura)

**Many floors** of different games!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit for specific shops
- Know the best stores
- Check prices before buying
- Enjoy the atmosphere

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Weekends are crowded** – Weekdays better

2. **Tax-free available** – Bring passport

3. **Compare prices** – Not all shops are cheap

4. **Small shops** – Often have rare finds

5. **Explore side streets** – Hidden gems everywhere
