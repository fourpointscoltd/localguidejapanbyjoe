> "Is Golden Week a good time to visit Japan?"

**Honestly... it's challenging!** Here's what you need to know.

---

## What Is Golden Week?

> "When is it?"

**A series of holidays:**
- Late April to early May
- April 29: Showa Day
- May 3: Constitution Day
- May 4: Greenery Day
- May 5: Children's Day

**Everyone travels** during this period!

---

## The Problems

> "Why is it difficult?"

**Challenges:**
- Everything is crowded
- Hotels are expensive
- Trains are packed
- Attractions have long lines
- Some businesses closed

**Locals joke:** "Golden Week is when you stay home"

---

## The Benefits

> "Are there any good things?"

**Possible advantages:**
- Beautiful weather usually
- Many events and festivals
- Cherry blossoms in northern areas
- Festive atmosphere

---

## Survival Tips

> "How do I manage if I go?"

**Tips:**
- Book everything months ahead
- Expect higher prices
- Reserve shinkansen seats
- Have backup plans
- Consider less popular destinations

**Alternative:** Visit right before or after Golden Week!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Travel domestically
- Go back to hometown
- Stay home to avoid crowds
- Book very far in advance

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book 3-6 months ahead** – Hotels sell out

2. **Avoid airports** – Extremely crowded

3. **Consider rural areas** – Less impacted

4. **Flights expensive** – Expect premium prices

5. **After GW** – Early/mid May is much better
