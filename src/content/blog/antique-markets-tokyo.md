> "Where can I find antique markets in Tokyo?"

Tokyo has great antique markets! Here are my favorites.

---

## Regular Antique Markets

> "Which markets happen often?"

**Oedo Antique Market (Tokyo Forum)**
- 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month
- Huge selection – 200+ vendors
- Free entry
- My top recommendation!

**Nogi Shrine Market**
- 2nd Sunday of each month
- Smaller but quality items
- Near Nogizaka Station

**Hanazono Shrine Market**
- Every Sunday
- In Shinjuku
- Good for beginners

---

## What Can You Find?

> "What do they sell?"

**Common items:**
- Kimono and obi belts
- Ceramics and pottery
- Old coins and stamps
- Vintage toys
- Woodblock prints
- Lacquerware
- Old photographs
- Furniture

---

## Tips for Shopping

> "Any advice for first-timers?"

**My tips:**
- Bring cash – Most vendors don't take cards
- Go early – Best items sell fast
- Bargaining is OK – But be polite
- Check condition carefully – No returns usually
- Bring a bag – For your purchases

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Hunt for specific items they collect
- Go early morning for best selection
- Enjoy the treasure-hunting experience
- Bring reusable bags

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Check schedules** – Markets cancel for rain sometimes

2. **Oedo is the biggest** – Start there if you only have one day

3. **Prices vary** – Compare before buying

4. **Ask questions** – Vendors love sharing stories

5. **Shipping available** – Some vendors can ship overseas
