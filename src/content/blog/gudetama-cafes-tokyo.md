> "My kids want to go to some Gudetama-themed cafes and buy Gudetama items. Does anyone have any idea where I can take them in Tokyo?"

**you’re in for a treat!** If you’re hunting for Gudetama-themed goodies, head over to Sanrio World in Ginza. It’s not too far from Tokyo Station and is like a paradise for Sanrio fans. But guess what? If you're really into Sanrio characters, you should definitely check out Sanrio Puroland. It’s a bit of a trek from central Tokyo, but totally worth it, especially for a Sanrio enthusiast. They've got an incredible range of merchandise and even some themed rides and shows. You’ll find heaps of Gudetama stuff there!
