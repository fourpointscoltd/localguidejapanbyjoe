> "Is it okay to visit Japan during Golden Week?"

**To put it bluntly: It's better NOT to come.**

---

## What Is Golden Week?

> "When is it?"

**Golden Week is April 29th to May 5th.**

This week has many national holidays in Japan.

---

## Why You Should Avoid It

> "Why is it so bad?"

**In Japan, there are only two one-week holidays:**
- Golden Week (May)
- New Year's holiday (January)

**That's why almost ALL Japanese people travel during this time!**

---

## The Problems

> "What happens during Golden Week?"

**Everything is difficult:**
- Shinkansen (bullet train) is fully booked
- Airplanes are fully booked
- Accommodation prices increase a lot
- Roads are the most congested all year
- Popular spots are extremely crowded

---

## The Only Reason to Come

> "Is there any good reason to visit?"

**Only one:** To meet friends who live in Japan.

If there's no other reason, I'll say it again:

**Absolutely do not come during Golden Week.**

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Travel domestically during Golden Week
- Book everything months in advance
- Know it's the busiest time
- Sometimes stay home to avoid crowds

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book 3+ months ahead** – If you must come

2. **Expect high prices** – Hotels cost much more

3. **Consider other dates** – May after Golden Week is nice

4. **New Year is similar** – Also very busy

5. **Ask me for alternative dates** – Happy to help!
