> "Are there free things to do in Tokyo?"

**Yes, plenty!** Tokyo has many amazing free experiences.

---

## Free Observation Decks

> "Can I see views for free?"

**Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building**
- 45th floor, amazing views
- Open until 10 PM usually
- Shinjuku location
- My top recommendation!

**Caretta Shiodome**
- 46th floor
- Nice Tokyo Tower view
- Less known by tourists

---

## Free Temples and Shrines

> "Which are free to enter?"

**All temples/shrines are free outside:**
- Meiji Shrine (Harajuku)
- Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa)
- Nezu Shrine (beautiful torii gates)
- Zojo-ji Temple (Tokyo Tower view)

**Only inner gardens may charge fees.**

---

## Free Parks

> "What about parks?"

**Great free parks:**
- Ueno Park – Zoo costs money, park is free
- Yoyogi Park – Near Harajuku
- Shinjuku Gyoen – Only ¥500 but worth it
- Imperial Palace East Gardens – Free!

---

## Free Activities

> "What else can I do?"

**Free experiences:**
- Tsukiji Outer Market (looking is free!)
- Window shopping in Ginza
- Shibuya Crossing (just cross it!)
- Harajuku street fashion watching
- Akihabara exploring

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use free observation decks
- Walk through parks on weekends
- Visit shrines regularly
- Know the free gems

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Government Building** – Best free views in Tokyo

2. **Walk around** – Many areas are fun just to explore

3. **Free samples** – Depachika (department store food floors)

4. **Festival days** – Sometimes free events

5. **Walking tours** – Some free volunteer-led tours exist
