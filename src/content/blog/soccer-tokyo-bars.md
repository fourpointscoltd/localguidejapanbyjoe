> "do you know where we could watch women's' World Cup games in Tokyo on July 20,21,22,23 and 24? We would love at least once to go to a group viewing or sports bar. And do you know of it will likely to be available on a hotel TV? Thanks so much!!!"

**Guess what?** Watching soccer in Tokyo can be a total blast, especially at sports bars! Since TV stations here often skip broadcasting games due to high costs, locals head to bars like "Los Cabos" and "HUB" to catch the action. These spots are super popular and easy to find around major train stations in the city. Aside from that, keep an eye out for public viewing events, especially during big tournaments. They’re a fun way to experience the game with a lively crowd!
