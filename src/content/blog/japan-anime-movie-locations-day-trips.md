> "Is there a day trip to visit filming locations or real-life spots that inspired famous anime or movies？"

**Yes, Japan offers many day trips to visiting spots that have inspired or appeared in famous anime and movies.** For example, if you're a fan of Studio Ghibli films, you might want to visit the city of Tokorozawa, which inspired the setting in "My Neighbor Totoro". Another popular destination is the town of Oarai in Ibaraki Prefecture, featured in the anime "Girls und Panzer".

---

## Details

Kyoto also has multiple sites from "The Tatami Galaxy" and "Sound! Euphonium". And for a slice of Tokyo mixed with a touch of cinematic magic, you can explore the real-life locations seen in "Your Name" and "Weathering with You". These trips provide not only a closer look at scenes from your favorite movies and shows but also a deeper understanding of Japanese culture.
