> "How do I book restaurants in Japan if I don't speak Japanese?"

**There are several ways!** Here's how to get reservations even without Japanese.

---

## Easy Options

> "What's the easiest method?"

**For tourists:**
- **Hotel concierge** – Ask them to book for you
- **TableCheck** – English booking site
- **Pocket Concierge** – For high-end restaurants
- **Google Maps** – Some restaurants have booking links

**Many restaurants** now accept English reservations online!

---

## Using Your Hotel

> "Can my hotel help?"

**Yes! Most hotels will:**
- Call restaurants for you
- Make reservations in Japanese
- Confirm details
- Often have recommendations

**Tip:** Give them the restaurant name and desired time!

---

## Walk-Ins

> "Can I just show up?"

**Often yes!**
- Casual restaurants usually OK
- Lunch is easier than dinner
- Arrive early or late to avoid peak times
- Some places are walk-in only

**When reservations are needed:**
- Popular restaurants
- Weekend dinners
- Special occasions
- Omakase/kaiseki

---

## Reservation Apps

> "Are there apps I can use?"

**Useful apps:**
- **Tabelog** – Japanese but has English (major site)
- **Hot Pepper Gourmet** – Some English
- **TableCheck** – English friendly
- **OpenTable** – Limited but available

---

## Important Etiquette

> "What should I know about reservations?"

**Rules:**
- Show up on time (very important!)
- Cancel if you can't make it
- Don't no-show – it's very rude
- Some places require credit card guarantee

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Book via phone or apps
- Use Hot Pepper for deals
- Know no-shows are serious
- Arrive on time

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book early** – Popular places fill up fast

2. **Credit card holds** – High-end places may require

3. **Group size** – Small groups easier to book

4. **Allergy info** – Mention when booking

5. **Late arrival** – Call if you'll be late
