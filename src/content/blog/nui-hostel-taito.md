> "Is Nui. Hostel near Asakusa good, and is it far from the center?"

**Nui. is one of Tokyo's best hostels!** And Kuramae/Asakusa is a fantastic area to stay.

---

## About Nui. Hostel & Bar Lounge

> "What's it like?"

**The good:**
- Stylish converted warehouse building
- Amazing bar/lounge area on the ground floor
- Great for meeting other travelers
- Mix of dorm beds and private rooms
- Clean and well-maintained
- Friendly staff

**The vibe:**
- Social but not a party hostel
- Good mix of Japanese and international guests
- Bar attracts locals too
- Creative, artsy atmosphere

---

## Location

> "Is it far from the center?"

**It's in Kuramae, which is actually a great location:**
- 10-minute walk to Asakusa/Sensoji Temple
- 5-minute walk to Kuramae Station (Oedo Line, Asakusa Line)
- Easy access to Akihabara, Ueno, and Skytree
- Quiet neighborhood but close to everything

**Kuramae itself is trendy** - lots of coffee shops, craft shops, and small galleries. It's becoming one of Tokyo's coolest neighborhoods.

---

## Nearby Highlights

> "What's around the hostel?"

- Asakusa/Sensoji Temple (walking distance)
- Tokyo Skytree (visible from the area)
- Kappabashi kitchen street (nearby)
- Great local restaurants and cafes
- Sumida River walks

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Know Kuramae as an up-and-coming artisan neighborhood
- Visit for craft coffee and small galleries
- Appreciate that it's less touristy than Asakusa proper
- Consider it one of Tokyo's hidden gems

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book early** - Nui. is popular and fills up fast

2. **The bar is worth visiting even if you don't stay** - Great atmosphere

3. **Kuramae is walkable** - Explore the side streets for hidden cafes

4. **Asakusa Line goes to the airport** - Direct access to both Haneda and Narita

5. **Budget-friendly area** - Cheaper dining than Shinjuku or Shibuya
