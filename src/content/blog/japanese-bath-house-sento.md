> "What's a sento? Is it different from an onsen?"

**Sento are local public bath houses!** They use heated tap water, while onsen use natural hot spring water.

---

## Sento vs Onsen

> "What's the difference?"

| Feature | Sento | Onsen |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Water | Heated tap water | Natural hot spring |
| Price | ¥500-1000 | ¥500-3000+ |
| Location | Cities, neighborhoods | Resort areas |
| Experience | Local, casual | Often luxury |
| Tattoo rules | Varies | Often strict |

---

## How to Use a Sento

> "What's the process?"

**Step by step:**
1. Pay at entrance (often a coin machine)
2. Go to locker room (gender separated)
3. Undress completely
4. Take small towel to washing area
5. Wash thoroughly before entering bath
6. Enter bath and relax
7. Rinse off, dry, dress

**Important:** Wash completely before getting in the bath!

---

## What to Bring

> "Do I need anything?"

**Usually available:**
- Towel rental
- Shampoo/soap (sometimes)
- Lockers

**Bring if possible:**
- Small towel
- Your own toiletries
- Change of underwear

---

## Tattoo Policy

> "Can I go with tattoos?"

**Sento are often more relaxed:**
- Small tattoos may be OK
- Cover with bandages sometimes works
- Ask before entering
- Local sentos are often more accepting than tourist onsen

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit sento near their homes
- Go regularly for relaxation
- Know the proper etiquette
- Consider it normal daily life

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Very affordable** – Great cultural experience for ¥500

2. **Evening is popular** – Go before 9 PM

3. **Clean yourself first** – Most important rule!

4. **No swimming** – Just soak and relax

5. **Try a super sento** – Modern version with many baths
