> "I heard Japanese stationery is amazing. What should I buy?"

**Japan takes stationery seriously!** Here's why it's special and what to get.

---

## Why It's Different

> "What makes it special?"

**Japanese stationery is:**
- Incredibly precise
- Beautifully designed
- Innovative
- High quality
- Affordable for the quality

**Attention to detail** in everything from erasers to notebooks!

---

## Best Brands

> "Which brands are good?"

**Pens:**
- **Pilot** – Famous for quality
- **Uni (Mitsubishi)** – Great variety
- **Pentel** – Energel series popular
- **Zebra** – Sarasa pens loved worldwide

**Other items:**
- **Kokuyo** – Notebooks, Campus series
- **MUJI** – Minimalist design
- **Midori** – Traveler's notebooks
- **Plus** – Correction tape, scissors

---

## Must-Buy Items

> "What should I get?"

**Popular items:**
- **Friction pens** – Erasable ink
- **Jetstream pens** – Smoothest writing
- **Campus notebooks** – Student favorite
- **Correction tape** – Much better than liquid
- **Mechanical pencils** – Many varieties
- **Cute erasers** – Great gifts

---

## Where to Shop

> "Where should I go?"

**Best stores:**
- **Loft** – Huge stationery section
- **Tokyu Hands** – Variety store
- **Itoya** (Ginza) – 12-floor stationery paradise
- **Kinokuniya** – Bookstore with stationery
- **100 yen shops** – Budget finds

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Care about writing instruments
- Have favorite pens
- Use specific notebooks
- Consider stationery important

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Itoya in Ginza** – Must-visit for stationery lovers

2. **Test before buying** – Most stores have testers

3. **Tax-free** – Available at larger stores

4. **Limited editions** – Japan-only colors and designs

5. **Light and packable** – Great souvenirs!
