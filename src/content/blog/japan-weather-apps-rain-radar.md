### Conclusion

If you're traveling in Japan — especially in summer — **sudden rain showers (like mini downpours)** can happen even on a sunny day.
To stay prepared, **Yahoo! Japan’s Rain Cloud Radar (雨雲レーダー)** is one of the most reliable tools for real-time weather updates. 🌦️📱

---
### General Information

Japanese summers are **hot, humid, and often unpredictable**.
It’s common for the weather to suddenly change — clear skies can quickly turn into a short, heavy shower and go back to sunny in 10 minutes.

While the default weather app on your phone might be fine in your home country, in Japan, it often **misses short-term changes**.
That’s why **local Japanese apps and services are better** for reliable, detailed forecasts.

---
### 🧭 Best Weather Apps and Sites for Japan

#### 1. **Yahoo! Japan Weather App / Website (Yahoo!天気)**

- Best for: **Real-time rain tracking**
- Language: Japanese only, but very visual and easy to understand
- Features:
Detailed rain cloud radar by the minute
- Push notifications for approaching rain
- Hour-by-hour and weekly forecasts

- App (iOS/Android): Search for “Yahoo!天気” in the App Store
- Website: weather.yahoo.co.jp

💡 *Even if you don’t read Japanese, the radar map is intuitive and very accurate.*

#### 2. **Tenki.jp**

- Japan’s official weather forecast service (run by the Japan Weather Association)
- More detailed than global apps
- Hourly, daily, and weekly forecasts
- Website: [tenki.jp](https://tenki.jp)
- App: Available in Japanese only

💡 *Great for planning multi-day trips or checking pollen, UV, or typhoon info.*

#### 3. **Windy**

- Language: English-friendly
- Excellent for visualizing **wind, rain, cloud movements**
- Popular among hikers, pilots, and travelers
- Website: [windy.com](https://www.windy.com)
- App: iOS/Android

💡 *Beautiful map interface and global coverage. Not Japan-specific, but very useful.*

#### 4. **Google Weather (via Google Search)**

- Not as accurate for sudden showers, but simple for daily weather
- Just search “Tokyo weather” or “Kyoto weather” in Google
- Reliable for basic planning

💡 *Good backup for general info, but not great for surprise summer rain.*

---
### My Personal Tip

I often travel around Japan in the summer, and I’ve learned that **even on sunny days, a 10-minute downpour can come out of nowhere**.
To avoid getting soaked, I check **Yahoo!天気’s rain cloud radar** whenever I go out.
It’s especially helpful for:

- Deciding if you need an umbrella ☔
- Avoiding getting caught between train stations
- Planning short walks or photo shoots

Even if the app is in Japanese, you can still understand the map and rain cloud animation — it’s super visual.

---
### Travel Tips

- **Carry a small foldable umbrella** in summer, even if the forecast says sunny
- **Check the rain radar** before heading out — many sudden rains last only 10–15 minutes
- **Use hotel Wi-Fi to download the app** if you have limited mobile data
- **Many stations have free umbrellas** you can borrow or buy for cheap at convenience stores
- **Don’t trust only Apple/Google default apps** — they often miss short, local rain
