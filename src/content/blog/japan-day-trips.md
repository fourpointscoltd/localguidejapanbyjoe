> "Which of these \"day trips\" will you recommend for a 1st timer?
 Yokohama, Nikko, kawaguchiko, Hakone, Nara, Kobe, Himeji, Nagoya, +"

**If it's your first time in Japan and you want a fun day trip, Kamakura and Nikko are great options.**

---

## Details

Kamakura is perfect if you like beaches and surfing. It has a mix of old temples and a nice seaside feel, which makes it a relaxing place to spend the day.

Nikko is better if you enjoy nature, hot springs, and waterfalls. The area is full of beautiful forests and famous sites like Toshogu Shrine.

Both places are full of things to see, and even though one day might not be enough to see everything, you’ll still enjoy the trip. Nagoya might not be as exciting for a short visit, so you can skip it.
