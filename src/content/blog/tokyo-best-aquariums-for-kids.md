### Conclusion

If you're visiting Tokyo with kids, **Aqua Park Shinagawa** is the best for a full-day experience 🐬.
For a short and casual visit, **Sumida Aquarium** at Tokyo Skytree is a great option.
However, the **best overall aquarium near Tokyo is in Kanagawa — Hakkeijima Sea Paradise!**

---
### General Information

Japanese aquariums are not always as impressive as those in other countries.
But in the **Tokyo and Kanto area**, there are still several enjoyable spots — some are stylish and compact, others are large and exciting with animal shows.

### My Personal Opinion

You might think these places are only for Pokémon-aged kids, but honestly, I’ve visited many aquariums in Japan and:

- **Hakkeijima Sea Paradise** was the most exciting — with high-quality shows that were better than some theme parks!
- **Aqua Park Shinagawa** impressed me with its modern design and easy access.
- **Sumida Aquarium** is small, but nice for a short break while sightseeing in Skytree.
- **If you want something more artistic**, **DMM Kariyushi Aquarium** in Okinawa is truly unique — though far from Tokyo.

Even though I love Okinawa, **Churaumi Aquarium** (while large) didn’t feel as fun to me personally.

---
### Best Aquarium Depending on Your Plan

- **Half-day casual visit in Tokyo** → Sumida Aquarium or Sunshine Aquarium
- **Full-day in Tokyo** → Aqua Park Shinagawa
- **Best overall near Tokyo** → Hakkeijima Sea Paradise
- **Want seaside & travel combined** → Enoshima Aquarium + beach walk

---
### Budget Guide

- **General admission**: ¥1,000–¥3,000 per person (~$7–$20 USD)
- **Kids under 4**: Usually free
- **Hakkeijima**: Some areas are free, but full access (rides + aquarium) requires a pass (~¥5,000)

---
### Seasonal Tips

- **Weekdays** are less crowded
- **Summer** → bring water, hat, and cooling items. Some aquariums like Enoshima & Hakkeijima are outdoors.
- **Rainy days** → Aqua Park or Sumida Aquarium are perfect indoor options
