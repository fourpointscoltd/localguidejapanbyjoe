> "Can I wear jewelry in a public onsen?"

**I recommend removing all accessories** – rings, necklaces, earrings, everything. Here's why.

---

## Why Remove Jewelry?

> "What's the problem?"

**Hot spring water can damage your jewelry:**
- Most onsen water is **acidic**
- Different springs have different minerals
- Your accessories may **rust or discolor**
- Some metals react badly with sulfur

Even gold and silver can be affected by certain hot springs!

---

## What About My Valuables?

> "Where do I put them?"

**Don't worry – there are lockers:**
- Changing rooms have lockers for your belongings
- Most lockers have keys you wear on your wrist
- Your valuables are safe

It's normal to wear the locker key into the bath on your arm.

---

## What to Remove

> "Specifically what should I take off?"

**Remove:**
- Rings (including wedding rings)
- Necklaces and bracelets
- Earrings
- Watches
- Hair accessories with metal

**Keep:**
- The small towel for modesty (don't put in water)
- Locker key on wrist

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Remove all accessories before bathing
- Store everything in lockers
- Don't worry about theft (it's very rare)
- Know that hot spring minerals can damage jewelry

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Leave expensive jewelry at home** – Or in your hotel safe

2. **Lockers are safe** – Japan has very low theft

3. **Check the water type** – Sulfur springs are especially harsh on metal

4. **Rinse after bathing** – If you accidentally wore something

5. **Ask staff** – They can advise about specific springs
