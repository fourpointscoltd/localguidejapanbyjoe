Yes! Your Suica card can be used for much more than just trains in Japan. It's basically an all-purpose payment card!

## Where You Can Use Suica

### Transportation
- **Trains** (JR, Metro, private lines)
- **Buses**
- **Some taxis**
- **Monorails**

### Shopping
- **Convenience stores** (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart)
- **Vending machines**
- **Kiosks** at train stations
- **Some supermarkets**
- **Drugstores**

### Food & Drinks
- **Fast food chains** (McDonald's, etc.)
- **Coffee shops** (Starbucks, Doutor)
- **Station restaurants**
- **Some izakayas**

### Others
- **Coin lockers**
- **Some tourist attractions**
- **Airport shops**

## Why Use Suica for Shopping?

1. **Speed** - Just tap and go, no fumbling with coins
2. **Convenience** - One card for everything
3. **Small purchases** - Perfect for vending machines
4. **No language barrier** - Just tap!

## How to Spot Suica-Accepted Places

Look for these symbols:
- **Suica** logo
- **IC** symbol
- **交通系IC** sign

## Balance Tips

- Keep at least **¥1,000** on your card
- **Reload at stations** or convenience stores
- Check balance at any **IC-enabled machine**

It's honestly one of the most useful things you'll have in Japan!
