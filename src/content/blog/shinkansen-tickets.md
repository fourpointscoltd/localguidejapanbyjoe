**when it comes to buying Shinkansen tickets from Tokyo to Osaka, you’re mostly in the clear without needing a month's advance booking.** Shinkansen trains are super frequent, running every 3 minutes during peak times, and each train has more than 1300 seats! You usually won't have trouble finding a seat except during super busy seasons like New Year, Golden Week in May, and Obon in mid-August. During these popular travel times, grabbing a reservation might be wise. On regular days, you might need to wait for a couple of trains, but you'll definitely get a seat. No worries!
