> "My son is in japan and managed to get a suica card but apart from trains, can they be used elsewhere?"

**Yes, your Suica card can be used for more than just trains in Japan!** Besides riding trains, you can use it to pay for buses and some taxis. It's really handy for shopping too. Many convenience stores, vending machines, and some restaurants accept Suica cards. You can even use it to enter some tourist spots like Japanese gardens.

---

## Details

Think of your Suica card as a wallet you don’t have to open every time. It's super easy to use. Just tap it on the card reader, and you're good to go! It's a great way to avoid carrying around a lot of cash and dealing with change. Also, refilling it is easy; you can do it at train stations or convenience stores. So, make sure to carry it with you—it’ll make your trip smoother.
