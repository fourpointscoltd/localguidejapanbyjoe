> "I love to go to Pokemon go event at Yokohama! any advice?"

**if you're a Pokémon fan, you'll absolutely love the week-long celebration in Yokohama!** It's definitely one of those must-go events. Tons of fun activities and loads of Pokémon stuff to see and do. Just a little tip: it happens in the summer, so it can get pretty hot. Make sure to stay hydrated and slap on some sunscreen to keep the heatstroke at bay. If you have the chance to go, definitely don’t miss out—it’s a blast!
