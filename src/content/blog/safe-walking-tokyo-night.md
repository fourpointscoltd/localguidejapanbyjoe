> "Is it safe to walk late at night in Tokyo? Which streets should I avoid?"

**Japan is very safe!** But there are a few places to be careful.

---

## Generally Very Safe

> "How safe is Tokyo?"

**Very rare to have problems:**
- Low crime rate
- Police are everywhere
- People are helpful
- Well-lit streets

It's rare to be injured or robbed by strangers in Japan.

---

## Areas to Be Careful

> "Where should I avoid?"

**If there is danger, it's where drunk people gather:**
- Shinjuku (especially Kabukicho)
- Shibuya
- Ikebukuro

**Better not to walk there late at night.**

---

## Why These Areas?

> "What's the problem?"

**Late night in entertainment areas:**
- Many drunk people
- Some pushy touts
- Can be rowdy
- Not dangerous, but uncomfortable

During the day and early evening, these areas are fine!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Feel safe walking at night
- Avoid drunk crowds
- Know which areas get rowdy
- Use common sense

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Most areas are safe** – Don't worry too much

2. **Avoid drunk crowds** – Late night entertainment areas

3. **Women are safe** – Japan is very safe for solo women

4. **Keep valuables secure** – Basic precaution anywhere

5. **Ask for help** – Japanese people are kind and helpful
