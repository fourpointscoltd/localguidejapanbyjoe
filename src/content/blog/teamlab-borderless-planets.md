> "TeamLab Borderless or Planets? Which is better?"

**Both are amazing!** But they're very different experiences. Here's my comparison.

---

## TeamLab Borderless

> "What's Borderless like?"

**Location:** Azabudai Hills (moved from Odaiba)

**Features:**
- Huge maze-like space
- Art moves between rooms
- Get lost exploring
- 2-3 hours recommended
- Air-conditioned throughout

**Best for:** Longer exploration, photography, varied experiences

---

## TeamLab Planets

> "What about Planets?"

**Location:** Toyosu

**Features:**
- Walk through water
- Barefoot experience
- Linear path (not a maze)
- 1-2 hours usually enough
- Some outdoor sections

**Best for:** Physical/immersive experience, shorter visits

---

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Borderless | Planets |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Time needed | 2-3 hours | 1-2 hours |
| Experience | Explore freely | Linear path |
| Physical | Walking | Wading in water |
| Photography | Great everywhere | Some restrictions |
| Price | ¥3,800-4,800 | ¥3,200-3,800 |

---

## Tickets

> "How do I get tickets?"

**Both require advance booking:**
- Book online before visiting
- Weekends sell out fast
- Choose time slot carefully
- Arrive on time!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit one or both
- Book weeks in advance
- Go on weekdays if possible
- Wear comfortable clothes

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book early** – Especially for weekends

2. **Wear shorts** – For Planets (you wade through water)

3. **No tripods** – Not allowed at either

4. **Lockers available** – For bags and belongings

5. **Combine with nearby areas** – Planets is near Toyosu fish market
