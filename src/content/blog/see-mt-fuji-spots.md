> "Where can I see Mt. Fuji without climbing it?"

**Great question!** Here are the best viewing spots.

---

## Lake Kawaguchi

> "What's the best lake view?"

**Lake Kawaguchi is one of the Fuji Five Lakes!**

- Beautiful views on clear days
- Various viewpoints around the lake
- Oishi Park is popular
- Kawaguchiko Natural Living Center

---

## Arakurayama Sengen Park

> "Where do photographers go?"

**Located in Fujiyoshida City!**

This park offers:
- Mt. Fuji with a pagoda in foreground
- Very popular photo spot
- Beautiful in any season

---

## The Famous Lawson Photo Spot

> "What about that convenience store photo?"

**Yes! Mt. Fuji sitting on top of a Lawson building!**

Address: 1395-1 Funatsu, Fujikawaguchiko, Minamitsuru District, Yamanashi

This became very famous on social media!

---

## Hakone

> "Any hot spring areas?"

**Hakone is a hot spring resort town!**

View Mt. Fuji from:
- Lake Ashi
- Owakudani Valley

Plus enjoy hot springs and beautiful nature!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Know the best photo spots
- Visit on clear days
- Check weather before going
- Combine with onsen trips

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Check weather** – Mt. Fuji is often hidden by clouds

2. **Morning is best** – Clearer skies usually

3. **Winter months** – Better visibility

4. **Combine with onsen** – Hakone is perfect

5. **Rent a car** – Easier to reach viewing spots
