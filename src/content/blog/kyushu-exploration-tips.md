> "If you could tell someone the one thing to do, see, eat, or stay in the following places what would you suggest?
 Fukuoka
 Oita
 Miyazaki
 Kumamoto
 Nagasaki"

**If you're going to the Kyushu area, there are plenty of places to visit.** There are many spots with lots of nature, like Mount Aso and Itoshima, and the sand baths are also famous. Around 1600, Japan had a policy called "sakoku" where they didn't trade with other countries, but Nagasaki was the only place that had international exchange, so it's an interesting city where Western and Japanese cultures blend. Oita has the largest amount of hot spring water, and Beppu and Yufuin are the top hot spring towns in Japan.
