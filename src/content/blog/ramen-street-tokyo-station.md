---

## Understanding Ramen Flavors

> "What types are there?"

**Main flavors:**
- **Miso** – Strong, savory
- **Shio (salt)** – Light, clear
- **Shoyu (soy sauce)** – Classic, rich
- **Tonkotsu** – Pork bone, creamy

**Also try:**
- Abura soba (oil noodles, no soup)
- Taiwan mazesoba (mixed noodles)
- Tsukemen (dipping noodles)

---

## My Recommendations

> "Which should I try at Ramen Street?"

**I recommend:**
- **SORANOIRO** – Unique and delicious
- **ROKURINSHA** – Famous for tsukemen

Both are excellent choices!

## Flavor Notes

> "Which flavor is best for foreigners?"

**Personally, I like shoyu or shio.**

But note:
- Shoyu and miso might be strong for some foreigners
- Try different types while in Japan
- Everyone has different taste
