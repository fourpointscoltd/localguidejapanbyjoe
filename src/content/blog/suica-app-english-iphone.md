Great question! A lot of visitors wonder how to use Suica on their iPhones, especially if they don't speak Japanese.

## The Simple Answer

You **don't actually need to download a separate Suica app** at all. Instead, you just add a Suica card to your **Apple Wallet** directly!

## How to Set Up Suica on iPhone

1. Open **Apple Wallet**
2. Tap the **+** button
3. Select **Transit Card**
4. Choose **Suica**
5. Add money (minimum ¥1,000)
6. Done!

The entire interface is in **English** if your iPhone is set to English.

## Requirements

- **iPhone 8 or later** (purchased worldwide)
- **Apple Pay** enabled
- **Japanese payment method** OR international credit card (Visa/Mastercard)

## Using Your iPhone Suica

- Just **tap your phone** at train gates
- Works even when phone is **locked**
- **Express Transit** mode means no Face ID needed
- Reload anytime through Apple Wallet

## Tips

1. **Set Suica as Express Card** for fastest entry
2. **Keep some balance** - reload before it gets too low
3. **Works everywhere** IC cards are accepted
4. **Check balance** in Apple Wallet anytime

This is honestly the most convenient way to travel in Japan - no physical card needed!
