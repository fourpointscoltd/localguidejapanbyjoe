> "Which bullet train provides the best experience solely for the train itself?"

### **Conclusion**

If you're riding the Shinkansen not just to travel—but to **experience the train itself**—then the **N700S “Supreme”** and **E5 Series Hayabusa** are the top picks. Whether you're into **cutting-edge tech, luxury interiors, or retro charm**, there's a bullet train for every type of traveler.

---
### 🏆 **Best Shinkansen Trains for Train Enthusiasts & Tourists**

---
### 🚄 1. **N700S “Supreme” (Tokaido/Sanyo Shinkansen)**

**Best for: Modern design, comfort, and tech**

💡 The “S” stands for **Supreme**—Japan’s most advanced Shinkansen currently in service.

#### ✅ Why it’s the best?

- **Smoother ride** with upgraded suspension & reduced noise
- **All seats have power outlets**
- **Free, high-speed Wi-Fi** onboard
- **Can operate during power failures** (thanks to onboard battery system)
- **Gran Class (luxury)**: Reclining leather seats, fine dining, and a personal attendant

#### 🚆 Where it runs:

- **Tokyo ⇄ Osaka** (Nozomi, Hikari, Kodama)
- **Osaka ⇄ Hakata** (Mizuho, Sakura, Kodama)

---
### 🚄 2. **E5 Series Hayabusa (Tohoku Shinkansen)**

**Best for: Sleek design & speed**

💡 One of the fastest trains in Japan (up to 320 km/h / 199 mph)

#### ✅ Why it’s special?

- Iconic **green + pink color scheme**
- **Futuristic cockpit** loved by train fans
- **Gran Class available**: Top-tier seats, premium food, relaxing atmosphere

#### 🚆 Where it runs:

- **Tokyo ⇄ Aomori ⇄ Hokkaido**
- Your fastest route to Sapporo (with transfers)

---
### 🚄 3. **E7/W7 Series (Hokuriku Shinkansen)**

**Best for: Cultural elegance + luxury**

💡 Design inspired by Kanazawa’s gold crafts and indigo blue dye

#### ✅ Why it’s special?

- Beautiful **gold and blue color scheme**
- **Quietest ride** among Shinkansen models
- **Gran Class offers gourmet meals** using local ingredients from Hokuriku

#### 🚆 Where it runs:

- **Tokyo ⇄ Kanazawa** (Nagano & Hokuriku Shinkansen)

---
### 🚄 4. **800 Series (Kyushu Shinkansen)**

**Best for: Traditional Japanese atmosphere**

💡 The most "Japanese" bullet train in interior design

#### ✅ Why it’s unique?

- **Wooden panels and washi-paper lighting**
- **Tatami-like flooring** for a Ryokan-like feel
- Scenic views of **Kyushu's landscapes**

#### 🚆 Where it runs:

- **Fukuoka ⇄ Kagoshima** (Mizuho & Sakura routes)

---
### 🚄 5. **500 Series (Sanyo Shinkansen – Kodama Only)**

**Best for: Retro-futuristic design lovers**

💡 Once Japan’s fastest, now a nostalgic favorite

#### ✅ Why it’s nostalgic?

- **Iconic long-nose design**
- Now runs slower, but train fans love its **vintage charm**
- Great for spotting on platforms or riding for old-school thrills

#### 🚆 Where it runs:

- **Osaka ⇄ Hakata** (Kodama service only)

---
### 🚄 6. **E956 “ALFA-X” (Tohoku Shinkansen – Testing Only)**

**Best for: The future of high-speed travel**

💡 A test train aiming for 400 km/h (250 mph)!

#### ✅ Why it's groundbreaking?

- **22-meter-long nose** to reduce tunnel noise
- **Emergency brakes + air resistance control**
- Expected to have **ultra-luxury interiors**

#### 🚆 Where it runs:

- Currently in testing between **Tokyo ⇄ Aomori**
- Expected to debut around **2030**, eventually connecting Tokyo ⇄ Sapporo

### 🚄 Final Thoughts

If your goal is **to ride the Shinkansen not just for transport, but as an experience**, then:

- Choose the **N700S “Supreme”** for the smoothest, most high-tech ride
- Ride the **E5 Hayabusa** if you’re heading north and want speed + comfort
- Try the **E7 or 800 Series** for unique design and cultural detail
