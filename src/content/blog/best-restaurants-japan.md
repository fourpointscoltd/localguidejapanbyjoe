Paris and New York have about 50,000 restaurants, but Tokyo alone has **over 150,000** - making it the world's largest restaurant city! So I'd need more specifics, but here are some highlights.

## My Top Recommendations by Type

### Seafood
**Yakiuo Ishikawa** - My absolute favorite for grilled fish
- Locations in Roppongi and Tsukiji
- Price: ¥5,000-10,000 per person

### Sushi
**Tsukiji Outer Market** - Great for breakfast sushi
- Multiple shops with varying prices
- Price: ¥2,000-5,000 for quality meal

### Ramen
**Fuunji (Shinjuku)** - Famous tsukemen
- Expect to queue
- Price: ¥1,000-1,500

### Yakitori
**Torikizoku** - Budget-friendly chain
- All items ¥350
- Great for groups

## By Budget

### Budget (~¥1,500)
- Convenience store bento
- Standing sushi bars
- Chain restaurants (Yoshinoya, Matsuya)

### Mid-range (¥3,000-8,000)
- Local izakayas
- Department store restaurants
- Specialty shops

### High-end (¥10,000+)
- Kaiseki restaurants
- High-end sushi counters
- Teppanyaki
