This question pops up quite often, and I get why—it's a common concern among visitors. From my experience living and traveling all over Japan, my honest answer is: **absolutely bring your boyfriend!**

## The Real Situation

Having tattoos is **not going to ruin your trip** or cause major hassles. Here's the reality:

### Where Tattoos ARE an Issue
- **Traditional onsen** (hot springs) - Many ban tattoos
- **Public pools**
- **Some gyms**

### Where Tattoos Are Fine
- **Everywhere else!** - Streets, restaurants, shops, temples, trains
- **Private onsen** - Book a room with private bath
- **Tattoo-friendly onsen** - More appearing each year
- **Modern hotels** - No issues

## Solutions for Onsen Lovers

1. **Private baths** (kashikiri buro) - Available at most ryokans
2. **Tattoo cover patches** - Sold at pharmacies
3. **Tattoo-friendly onsen** - Check websites like tattoo-spot.jp
4. **Hotel room baths** - Many ryokans have in-room baths

## The Changing Attitude

Japan's attitude toward tattoos is slowly evolving:
- **2019 tourism push** led many places to relax rules
- **Younger Japanese** are more accepting
- **International hotels** rarely care

## My Advice

1. **Don't let tattoos stop you** from visiting Japan
2. **Research onsen policies** beforehand
3. **Book private baths** for the onsen experience
4. **Cover up** when visiting traditional temples (out of respect)

Japan has so much to offer for both of you. Don't miss out over this concern!
