> "Kamakura or Nikko?"

**choosing between Kamakura and Nikko is like picking between ice cream flavors—they're both great in their own way!** If you love the beach vibe, something kind of like Miami, you’ll enjoy Kamakura. It's got a laid-back summer feel, plus there's the big Buddha statue which is pretty cool. On the other hand, if you’re more into nature with a splash of history, Nikko is your spot. Think beautiful waterfalls, lush forests, and some seriously impressive temples. Guess what? If you can, why not plan to visit both? Each place offers a unique slice of Japanese beauty!
