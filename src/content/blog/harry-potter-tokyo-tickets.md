> "How do I book Harry Potter Studio Tour in Tokyo? Are tickets hard to get?"

**Tickets sell out fast!** Here's how to book successfully.

---

## How to Book

> "Where do I buy tickets?"

**Official website only:**
- Visit the official Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo website
- Select your date and time slot
- Book as early as possible
- Tickets release about 2-3 months ahead

**No walk-ins!** You must have a ticket before visiting.

---

## Booking Tips

> "How can I get tickets?"

**My advice:**
- Check the website at midnight Japan time when new dates release
- Weekdays are easier than weekends
- Morning slots often sell first
- Have your payment ready
- Try different dates if sold out

---

## What's Included?

> "What do I get with the ticket?"

**Your ticket includes:**
- Entry to the studio tour
- All exhibits and sets
- Usually 3-4 hours to explore
- Photo opportunities

**Not included:**
- Food and drinks
- Souvenirs
- Butterbeer (sold separately)

---

## Getting There

> "How do I get to the studio?"

**Location: Toshimaen (Nerima)**
- Take Seibu Toshima Line to Toshimaen Station
- Or Toei Oedo Line to Toshimaen Station
- About 30 minutes from Shinjuku
- Free shuttle from station

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Book months in advance
- Go on weekdays if possible
- Spend the whole day there
- Buy Butterbeer and merchandise

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book early** – 2-3 months ahead is best

2. **Arrive on time** – Your time slot is fixed

3. **Bring yen** – For food and souvenirs

4. **Wear comfortable shoes** – Lots of walking

5. **Check weather** – Some areas are outdoors
