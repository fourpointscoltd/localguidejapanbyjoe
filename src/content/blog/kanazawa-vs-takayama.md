 Tokyo -> Kanazawa (1 night) ->Shirakawa-Go (1night) -> Kyoto or.
 Tokyo -> Takayama (1 night) -> Shirakwa-Go (1 night) -> Kyoto
 ?"

**If you're visiting Shirakawago and figuring out your next stop, here's a quick tip: Takayama might be your best bet for a one-night stay.** It's only about an hour by bus from Shirakawago and offers a quaint, Edo-period atmosphere. Don't forget to try the famous Hida Beef while you're there! On the other hand, Kanazawa, with its beautiful gardens and vibrant market, is fantastic if you're planning a longer stay. But for a quick, cozy overnight, Takayama really hits the spot.
