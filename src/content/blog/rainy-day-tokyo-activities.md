> "What should I do in Tokyo on a rainy day?"

Here's a secret: **grab your umbrella and go anyway!** Rain in Tokyo isn't always bad.

---

## Why Rainy Days Can Be Good

> "Really? How?"

**Benefits of rainy days:**
- **Fewer crowds** at popular tourist spots
- **Cooler temperatures** (especially nice in summer)
- **Beautiful atmosphere** – Japan looks great in rain
- **No waiting in lines** – Attractions are less busy

If the weather isn't too cold, just bring an umbrella and explore!

---

## Best Rainy Day Activities

> "Okay, what should I do?"

**Indoor attractions:**
- **Museums** – Tokyo has hundreds
- **Aquariums** – Sumida Aquarium, teamLab Planets
- **Shopping malls** – Shibuya, Odaiba, Ikebukuro
- **Department stores** – Great for browsing
- **Arcades** – Game centers everywhere

**Still fun in light rain:**
- **Temples and shrines** – Beautiful and less crowded
- **Covered shopping streets** – Nakano Broadway, Ameyoko
- **Underground areas** – Tokyo Station, Shinjuku

---

## My Recommendation

> "What would you do?"

**Go to Tokyo Disneyland or DisneySea!**

Yes, seriously! Why?
- Most attractions have roofs
- Much shorter waiting times
- You might have the parks almost to yourself
- Still magical even in rain

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Check the weather forecast and plan accordingly
- Always carry a folding umbrella
- Don't cancel plans for light rain
- Use underground passages to stay dry

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Buy an umbrella** – Convenience stores sell them for ¥500-800

2. **Clear umbrellas** – Very popular in Japan, easy to see through

3. **Underground Tokyo** – Many stations connect to malls underground

4. **Rain gear** – Konbini sells ponchos too

5. **Enjoy the mood** – Rainy Japan is photogenic!
