## Conclusion

You can buy official Mario merchandise at the **Nintendo TOKYO store in Shibuya**.
For fans, it’s a must-visit — full of exclusive goods, figures, clothes, and more 🍄🎮

---
### General Information

Mario is one of Japan’s most beloved and recognizable game characters, created by Nintendo.
Luckily for travelers, **Nintendo opened an official store in Tokyo in 2019**, and it's the **best place to find Mario items**.
You won’t find much Mario merch in regular toy stores or arcades — official shops are your best bet.

---
### Best Places to Buy Mario Goods in Tokyo

#### 🏬 Nintendo TOKYO (Shibuya PARCO)

- Location: 6th floor of **Shibuya PARCO**, a trendy shopping complex
- Huge selection of **Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach, Bowser**, and other Nintendo character goods
- Exclusive items: apparel, stationery, plush toys, kitchen goods, figures, bags, and more
- Shares the floor with **Pokémon Center Shibuya** and **CAPCOM Store**
- Closest station: **Shibuya Station** (8-minute walk)

💡 *This is the **main official Nintendo store** in Japan (before Nintendo OSAKA and Nintendo KYOTO opened). Lines can be long on weekends.*

---
### Other Locations (Limited Selection)

#### 🎢 Super Nintendo World (Universal Studios Japan – Osaka)

- Has exclusive Mario-themed goods and snacks
- However, it’s located in **Osaka**, not Tokyo

#### 🧸 Toy Stores (like Bic Camera, Yodobashi Camera, Kiddy Land)

- You may find **some Mario toys and games**, but the variety is limited compared to the official store

---
### My Personal Tip

I visited the Nintendo TOKYO store and was amazed by the variety and quality.
Even the **shopping bags and store layout are themed**, which makes the visit fun even if you don’t buy much.

If you’re a Mario fan — or traveling with kids — it’s totally worth the visit.
Don’t miss the nearby **Shibuya Sky observation deck** for a great city view after shopping 🏙️

---
### Travel Tips

- **Visit on weekday mornings** to avoid long lines
- **Check stock online** if you’re looking for something specific
- **Bring a backpack** — the store bags are cute but not the most durable for travel
- **No ticket needed** to enter, but it can get crowded during holidays
- **Combine with Pokémon Center or Shibuya sightseeing** for a full afternoon!
