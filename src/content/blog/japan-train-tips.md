> "How difficult is the train system? It looks really confusing. Any tips for first timers?? Thank you!!"

**You don't need to understand the train system.** Just buy a SUICA or PASMO card or register it on your smartphone. Then, search on Google Maps and tap on the train button. That's all you need to do.
