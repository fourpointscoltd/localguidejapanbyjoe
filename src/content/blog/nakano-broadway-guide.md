> "Hi, have you visited Nakano broadway? Are there any shops thats not anime related? And was it worth going there?"

**Absolutely, Nakano Broadway is definitely worth a visit even if you're not into anime!** Alongside the anime and manga shops, you'll find a bunch of stores selling a quirky mix of items like vintage clothing, rare records, and unique collectibles. It’s like a treasure trove for retro and unusual finds. It’s less crowded compared to Akihabara, so you can explore at a more relaxed pace. Plus, the area has a really cool local vibe that’s quite different from the usual tourist spots. So, even if anime isn’t your thing, you’ll likely find something that catches your eye!
