> "When do stores in Harajuku and Shibuya typically open?"

The short answer: **most shops open at 10am-11am** and close around **8pm-9pm**. But it varies a lot by type. Here's the detailed breakdown:

## Harajuku

| Type | Opens | Closes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Takeshita Street shops** | 10:00-11:00 | 19:00-20:00 | Some crepe shops open earlier |
| **Omotesando boutiques** | 11:00 | 20:00 | High-end brands |
| **Cat Street shops** | 11:00-12:00 | 19:00-20:00 | Indie/vintage stores |
| **Cafes** | 8:00-9:00 | 18:00-20:00 | Great for early arrivals |
| **Restaurants** | 11:00-11:30 | 22:00-23:00 | Lunch from 11:30 |

## Shibuya

| Type | Opens | Closes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shibuya 109** | 10:00 | 21:00 | Fashion department store |
| **Shibuya Scramble Square** | 10:00 | 21:00 | Shibuya Sky until 23:00 |
| **Center-gai shops** | 10:00-11:00 | 21:00-22:00 | Youth fashion area |
| **Don Quijote (Mega)** | **24 hours** | **24 hours** | Never closes! |
| **Restaurants/Izakaya** | 11:30 (lunch) | 23:00-02:00 | Many open late |
| **Bars** | 18:00-19:00 | 02:00-05:00 | Varies widely |

## My Local Tips

- **Arrive at 10am** for Harajuku — Takeshita Street gets insanely crowded by noon
- **Shibuya is better in the evening** — the neon lights and energy peak after dark
- **Sunday in Harajuku** is the most crowded day — if possible, visit on a weekday
- **Lunch time (11:30-13:00)** means queues at popular restaurants — eat early or late
- **Don Quijote in Shibuya** is open 24 hours and offers tax-free shopping — perfect for late-night souvenir runs

## What If You Arrive Early?

If you're in the area before shops open:
- **Morning coffee** at a kissaten (old-school coffee shop) — many open at 7-8am
- **Meiji Shrine** — right next to Harajuku, open from sunrise. Beautiful morning walk
- **Yoyogi Park** — perfect for a morning stroll, adjacent to Meiji Shrine
- **Convenience store breakfast** — 7-Eleven, Lawson, and FamilyMart are always open
