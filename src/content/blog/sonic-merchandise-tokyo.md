### Conclusion

**Tokyo Joypolis in Odaiba** is the main place where you can buy official Sonic the Hedgehog merchandise in Tokyo.
However, note that you need to **pay admission even if you're just shopping** 🌀

---
### General Information

Sonic the Hedgehog is a global icon from SEGA, but **Sonic merchandise is surprisingly hard to find in Tokyo** compared to other big franchises like Pokémon or Nintendo.

There’s no dedicated Sonic store in Akihabara or Shibuya, and SEGA arcades usually don’t sell official merchandise.

If you're specifically looking for Sonic items, **Joypolis** is your best bet.

---
### Where to Buy Sonic Goods in Tokyo

#### 🎢 Tokyo Joypolis – SEGA’s Indoor Theme Park

- Location: **Odaiba, DECKS Tokyo Beach**
- Has an official **"JP Store"** that sells Sonic goods
- Includes keychains, plush toys, T-shirts, and more
- **Admission is required**, even if you only want to shop
Admission Fee (as of 2025): ¥1,200 for entry only
- Full passport (rides included): ¥4,500+

- Closest station: **Odaiba-Kaihinkoen Station** (Yurikamome Line)
- More info: [Tokyo Joypolis Official Page (EN)](https://tokyo-joypolis.com/language/english/shop/jpstore.html)

💡 *Check the official website for up-to-date opening hours and item availability.*

### Travel Tips

- **Buy your Joypolis ticket online** in advance to save time
- **Check the shop section ("JP Store") of the Joypolis website** to see if Sonic items are still available
- **Look for online options** like Amazon Japan or Rakuten if you want to avoid the entry fee
- **Don’t expect to find Sonic goods in most game centers or anime stores** — it’s a bit niche in Japan
