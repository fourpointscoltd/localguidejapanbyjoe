> "Christmas in Tokyo!
What are your top 5 to not miss?"

**Guess what, Tokyo turns into a winter wonderland during Christmas!** Definitely check out the Christmas market in Hibiya where the festive vibe is just amazing. Tokyo is famous for its stunning illuminations, and places like Tokyo Midtown and Omotesando are must-sees. While wandering, you’ll see tons of shops dressed up in holiday style—all perfect for some Christmas shopping. And don’t forget to indulge in some local traditions; grab some KFC or a delightful Christmas shortcake, which is a real treat here. Just walking around Tokyo this time of year, you can really feel the Christmas spirit everywhere!
