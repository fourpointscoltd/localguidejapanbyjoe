> "Hi, can someone suggest vegan restaurants in tokyo, minato city area, kyoto, Shin Osaka ?"

**Absolutely, Happy Cow is a fantastic resource for finding vegan spots in Japan!** For Tokyo, definitely try Soranoiro NIPPON for their unique soy sauce-flavored ramen—it's a must-try if you're okay with soy sauce. In Minato, there's T's Tantan, known for their delicious vegan noodles. Heading over to Kyoto, check out Ain Soph Journey, it's a cozy spot with creative dishes. And in Shin-Osaka, Veg Out is right by the station and offers great views with your meal. Each place has its own charm, so you're in for a treat wherever you go!
