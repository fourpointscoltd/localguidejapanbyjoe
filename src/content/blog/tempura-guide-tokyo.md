> "What exactly is tempura? Is it just fried food?"

**Tempura is an art form!** Light, crispy batter coating fresh ingredients. Very different from regular fried food.

---

## What Makes Tempura Special

> "How is it different?"

**Tempura characteristics:**
- Very light, crispy batter
- Not heavy or greasy
- Ingredients stay fresh inside
- Served immediately after frying
- Portuguese-influenced originally!

---

## Common Tempura Items

> "What can be tempura?"

**Vegetables:**
- Shiso leaf (perilla)
- Sweet potato
- Lotus root
- Eggplant
- Shishito peppers
- Kabocha squash

**Seafood:**
- Shrimp (most popular!)
- Squid
- Whitefish
- Small fish

---

## How to Eat

> "What's the right way?"

**Dipping options:**
- Tentsuyu (dipping sauce)
- Salt only
- With grated daikon radish

**Tips:**
- Eat immediately when served
- Don't let it sit – gets soggy
- Dip lightly, don't soak

---

## Where to Try

> "Best places for tempura?"

**Options by budget:**

**Casual:**
- Tenya (chain, budget-friendly)
- Department store restaurants
- Set meals at family restaurants

**High-end:**
- Counter seating
- Watch chef fry each piece
- Served one at a time
- Much more expensive

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat tempura at home sometimes
- Know good tempura is light
- Save high-end tempura for special occasions
- Eat quickly while hot

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Tenya chain** – Great quality for the price

2. **Tempura don** – Over rice, filling meal

3. **Tempura soba/udon** – Popular lunch combo

4. **Tendon** – Rice bowl with tempura and sweet sauce

5. **Counter seats** – Best experience at high-end places
