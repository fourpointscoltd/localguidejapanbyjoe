> "Is upgrading to the green pass on the JR worth the extra money? We are looking at a 14 day pass and it is about $120usd more for the green pass."

**about the JR Green Pass—honestly, it really depends on what you value.** The Green Car is indeed more spacious and quieter, which can be a nice perk. However, if you're traveling on regular days and not during busy holiday periods like Golden Week, you might find that the standard cars are just fine. They are well-maintained and comfortable too. So, if you're looking to save some money, sticking with the regular JR Pass could be the way to go. Save that extra cash for some amazing sushi or a few extra souvenirs!
