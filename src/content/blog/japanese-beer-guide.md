> "What's the beer situation in Japan?"

**Japan has great beer!** From major brands to craft breweries.

---

## Major Beer Brands

> "What are the main beers?"

**Big Four:**
- **Asahi Super Dry** – Crisp, clean, most popular
- **Kirin Ichiban** – Smooth, first press
- **Sapporo** – Oldest brand, balanced
- **Suntory Premium Malts** – Rich, premium

**All are good** – try them all and find your favorite!

---

## Beer vs Happoshu

> "What's the difference?"

**In Japan:**
- **Beer** – Made with malt, higher tax
- **Happoshu** – Less malt, cheaper
- **Third beer** – Even less malt, cheapest

**Check labels** – "Beer" (ビール) vs other categories.

---

## Craft Beer Scene

> "Is there craft beer?"

**Yes! Growing scene:**
- Yona Yona Ale – Popular craft brand
- Coedo – Saitama brewery
- Many local microbreweries
- Craft beer bars in cities

**More expensive** than major brands but worth trying.

---

## Where to Drink

> "Best places for beer?"

**Options:**
- Izakaya – Casual, food included
- Beer halls – Large, social
- Convenience stores – Cheap!
- Craft beer bars – Specialty
- Yatai (food stalls) – Outdoor drinking

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Drink Asahi or Kirin usually
- Start with "toriaezu beer" (beer first!)
- Share pitchers at izakaya
- Try seasonal limited editions

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Convenience store** – ¥200 for a can, great value

2. **Draft vs canned** – Try both

3. **Beer gardens** – Summer rooftop drinking

4. **Happoshu** – Fine if you're on a budget

5. **Kanpai!** – Say cheers before drinking!
