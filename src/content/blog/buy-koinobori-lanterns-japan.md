> "Any idea where I can buy some Koinobori in Tokyo? Kyoto or Osaka?
 Or carp koi paper lanterns? Popular in May for the children's festival"

**you're in for a treat with those colorful koinobori!** If you're around during the right season, finding them is pretty easy and fun. In Tokyo, Kyoto, or Osaka, pop into any 100-yen shop like Daiso—you'll be surprised what you can find for just a buck! Also, check out larger stores like Cainz Home, Kohnan, Tokyu Hands, and Loft. Don't miss out on Don Quijote, affectionately known as "Donki"; it's a treasure trove for all sorts of items, including koinobori. It’s like a festival in a store! Have fun hunting for those vibrant carp lanterns!
