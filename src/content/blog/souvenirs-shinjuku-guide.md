Shinjuku is actually a great place for souvenir shopping! Here are my local recommendations.

## Top Souvenir Spots

### 1. Don Quijote (Donki)
- **Location:** Multiple in Shinjuku (main one near Kabukicho)
- **Open:** 24 hours!
- **Best for:** Everything - snacks, cosmetics, electronics, weird stuff
- **Tax-free:** Yes
- **Tip:** Can be overwhelming - make a list first

### 2. NEWoMan Shinjuku
- **Location:** Connected to Shinjuku Station (South side)
- **Best for:** High-quality gifts, food souvenirs
- **Style:** Modern, curated selection
- **Tax-free:** Yes

### 3. Takashimaya Times Square
- **Location:** South exit of Shinjuku Station
- **Best for:** Premium gifts, department store quality
- **Food floor:** Excellent for food souvenirs
- **Tax-free:** Yes

### 4. Tokyu Hands
- **Location:** Takashimaya Times Square building
- **Best for:** Stationery, crafts, unique gadgets
- **Style:** Quality over quantity

### 5. Bic Camera
- **Location:** East exit
- **Best for:** Electronics, cosmetics (surprisingly!)
- **Tax-free:** Yes

## Souvenir Ideas by Type

### Food
- **Tokyo Banana** - Classic, found everywhere
- **Kit Kat flavors** - Don Quijote has best selection
- **Green tea sweets** - Any department store

### Non-Food
- **Japanese stationery** - Tokyu Hands
- **Cosmetics** - Don Quijote or Matsumoto Kiyoshi
- **Character goods** - Don Quijote

## Pro Tips

1. **Prices are comparable to airports** - Don't stress about "better deals"
2. **Tax-free** - Bring your passport everywhere
3. **Evening shopping** - Less crowded after 8 PM
4. **Shinjuku Station basement** - Food souvenir paradise!

Grab your souvenirs in Shinjuku and save airport time for relaxing!
