Booking a trip to Japan can feel overwhelming at first, but honestly, the Japanese system is pretty user-friendly once you get the hang of it!

## What You MUST Book in Advance

### 1. Flights
- Book 2-4 months ahead for best prices
- Compare Haneda vs Narita arrival times

### 2. First & Last Night Hotels
- Especially during cherry blossom (late March-early April)
- Golden Week (late April-early May)
- Autumn leaves (mid-November)

### 3. JR Pass (if using)
- Order before arriving in Japan
- Activate when you need it, not on arrival day

## What You CAN Book in Advance

### Hotels (Middle of Trip)
- Nice to have but not always necessary
- Booking.com offers free cancellation options

### Popular Restaurants
- High-end sushi places: 1-3 months ahead
- TeamLab and similar attractions: 1-2 weeks ahead

### Day Tours
- Helpful for Mt. Fuji, Kyoto temples
- Klook and Viator have good options

## What You DON'T Need to Book

- **Regular restaurants** - Walk in almost anywhere
- **Train tickets** - Buy at stations (except peak Shinkansen)
- **Convenience store visits** - Obviously!
- **Most attractions** - Pay at the door

## My Booking Timeline

| When | What to Book |
|------|--------------|
| 3+ months | Flights |
| 2 months | Key hotels, JR Pass |
| 1 month | Popular attractions |
| 1-2 weeks | Day tours, special restaurants |
| On arrival | The rest! |

## Helpful Websites

- **Booking.com** - Hotels
- **Klook** - Attractions & tours
- **Tabelog** - Restaurant reservations
- **Google Maps** - Everything navigation

Japan is very walkable and spontaneous-trip friendly. Don't over-plan!
