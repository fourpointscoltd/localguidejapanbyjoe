> "I'm arriving in Shinjuku with my son 12 year old on Wednesday, near the Tokyo Dome. Any suggestions for what to visit within walking distance?"

**Shinjuku is such a vibrant spot!** While it's not packed with kid-specific attractions, you'll find the peaceful Shinjuku Gyoen, perfect for a stroll or a picnic. But guess what? Just a short train ride away, Tokyo Dome City awaits! It's a hit with teens, offering mini amusement parks and cool shops, especially if he loves anime. Plus, there are tons of unique eateries from noodle shops to sweet shops. It’s a great mix of fun and culture, sure to keep both of you entertained!
