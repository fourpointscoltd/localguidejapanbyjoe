#### **Conclusion**

Tokyo has many incredible **rooftop bars** where you can enjoy amazing cocktails and breathtaking city views. While **Privado** near Asakusa was once my top recommendation, it has unfortunately closed. However, I personally love **The Gate Hotel Asakusa’s rooftop bar**, which offers **great views, reasonable prices, and fewer tourists**. Other excellent options include **Didot in Ueno, The Roof Shibuya Sky, and Bvlgari Bar near Tokyo Station**.

#### **General Information**

Rooftop bars in Tokyo range from **luxurious high-rise lounges** to **casual, scenic spots**. Many are located in **hotels and commercial buildings**, providing stunning panoramic views of landmarks like the **Tokyo Skytree, Shibuya, and the Rainbow Bridge**. Some bars specialize in **craft cocktails**, while others focus on **wine, champagne, or seasonal drinks**.

#### **Unexpected Factors & Disadvantages**

- **Reservations are often required** – Some high-end bars (like Andaz Tokyo & Bvlgari Bar) require advance booking.
- **Dress codes may apply** – Luxury bars often require **smart casual or formal attire**.
- **Some bars are weather-dependent** – Outdoor seating may close on **rainy or windy days**.

#### **Extra Travel Tips**

- **For a budget-friendly rooftop experience:** → Try **The Gate Hotel Asakusa** or **Didot in Ueno**.
- **For the best photos:** → Go to **The Roof Shibuya Sky** at sunset.
- **For special occasions:** → **Andaz Tokyo or Bvlgari Bar** offer a luxury setting.
