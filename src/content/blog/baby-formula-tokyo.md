> "Hell! Where can I buy baby formula in Tokyo? Thank you"

**shopping for baby formula in Tokyo is pretty easy!** You should definitely check out Akachanhonpo and Nishimatsuya—they're like treasure troves for all things baby-related. Babies R Us is another great spot, and you'll find lots of choices there. Don’t forget about the supermarkets and pharmacies around town; many also stock various brands of baby formula. So, no worries, you’ll have plenty of options to choose from!
