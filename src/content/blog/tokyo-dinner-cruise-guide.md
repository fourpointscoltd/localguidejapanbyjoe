Here's what I've learned from living in Tokyo and actually trying a few dinner cruises!

## The Honest Truth

In Japan, we don't really have a big tradition of formal dinner cruises on large ships like you might find in some Western countries. However, there are some nice options!

## Top Recommendations

### 1. Yakatabune (Traditional Boat)
- **Experience:** Most authentically Japanese
- **Food:** Traditional Japanese course meal
- **Price:** ¥10,000-15,000 per person
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Best for:** Cultural experience

### 2. Symphony Tokyo Bay Cruise
- **Experience:** Modern cruise ship
- **Food:** French/Western cuisine
- **Price:** ¥8,000-20,000 per person
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Best for:** Special occasions

### 3. Jicoo The Floating Bar
- **Experience:** Stylish modern boat
- **Food:** Light fare and drinks
- **Price:** ¥5,000-8,000 per person
- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Best for:** Casual evening out

## What to Expect

- Views of **Rainbow Bridge** and **Tokyo Bay**
- **Night skyline** is spectacular
- **Seasonal decorations** on some boats
- **English menus** available at most

## Booking Tips

1. **Reserve 1-2 weeks ahead** for weekends
2. **Check departure points** - Some leave from Odaiba
3. **Dress code** - Smart casual for most
4. **Motion sickness** - Tokyo Bay is calm, but bring medicine if sensitive

## My Personal Pick

For tourists, I recommend **Yakatabune** for the unique Japanese experience. It's something you can't easily find elsewhere!
