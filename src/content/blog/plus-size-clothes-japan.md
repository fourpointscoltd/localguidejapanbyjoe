Here's what you should know from a local's perspective about finding larger sizes in Japan.

## The Honest Truth

Yes, it can be challenging. Japanese sizes tend to run smaller than Western sizes, and many clothing stores don't stock larger sizes regularly. 

For a US women's 12, you're looking at roughly:
- Japanese XL or 2L
- Sometimes labeled as "LL"

## Where to Shop

### Uniqlo (Best Bet)
- Carries up to 3XL in many items
- **Online store** has larger selection than physical stores
- Ships to hotels
- Consistent sizing

### Shimamura
- Budget-friendly
- Larger size sections in many locations
- Less trendy but practical

### GU (Uniqlo's Sister Brand)
- Similar sizing to Uniqlo
- More fashion-forward
- Good basics

### Department Stores
- **Isetan, Takashimaya** - Look for "Queen Size" floors
- More expensive but better selection
- Professional staff can help

### Online Options
- **Zozotown** - Japan's largest fashion site
- Filter by size easily
- Ships to hotels/convenience stores

## What to Bring from Home

- **Shoes** - Sizes above US 8.5 are hard to find
- **Bras** - Sizing is completely different
- **Jeans** - If you have a preferred fit

## Tips

1. **Try on before buying** - Sizing can be inconsistent
2. **Check size charts** - Don't assume
3. **Don't feel discouraged** - Options exist!
4. **Order online** - Ship to your hotel

## The Positive Side

- Japan has excellent quality at reasonable prices
- Uniqlo's basics are genuinely good
- You might find unique pieces at vintage shops (sizing varies)

Enjoy your trip - don't let clothing concerns overshadow it!
