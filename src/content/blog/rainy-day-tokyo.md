> "What should I do in Tokyo on rainy days?"

**Grab your umbrella and let's go!** Rain can be great for sightseeing.

---

## Why Rainy Days Are Good

> "Should I stay inside?"

**No! Go outside!**

The weather isn't even chilly usually, so it's perfect for:
- **Popular tourist spots** – Fewer people!
- **Taking photos** – Beautiful rain scenery
- **Exploring without crowds**

**Less people around means better experience!**

---

## My Top Recommendation

> "Where should I go?"

**If I were you, I'd go to Disney!**

Rainy days at Disney:
- Shorter wait times
- Less crowded
- Indoor attractions available
- Still lots of fun

---

## Indoor Options

> "What about indoor activities?"

**If you want to stay dry:**
- Shopping malls
- Museums
- Aquariums
- Department stores
- Underground shopping areas

Tokyo has many covered areas!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Go out with umbrellas
- Know rainy days mean fewer crowds
- Visit indoor attractions
- Don't let rain stop them

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Buy a clear umbrella** – From any convenience store

2. **Crowds are smaller** – Great opportunity!

3. **Indoor Tokyo** – Many underground passages

4. **Rain gear available** – Convenience stores have everything

5. **Embrace the weather** – Part of the experience!
