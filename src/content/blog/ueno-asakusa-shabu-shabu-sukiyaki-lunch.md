> "We're going to be in Ueno & Asakusa today. Can you recommend a good and reasonable place for shabu shabu or sukiyaki for lunch? thanks!"

**In Ueno and Asakusa, eating at lunchtime is a great way to enjoy fancy foods like shabu shabu and sukiyaki without spending too much money.** For shabu shabu, I recommend a place called Hachinoki. It's very popular and you can get a delicious meal for under 2000 yen. If you prefer sukiyaki, go to a restaurant named Yansando. It's also budget-friendly and highly recommended for its quality.

---

## Details

Both places offer a cozy setting and give you a taste of authentic Japanese cuisine at a reasonable price. Just remember, lunchtime is usually cheaper than dinner, so it's the best time to enjoy these dishes. Enjoy your meal and your time in Ueno and Asakusa!
