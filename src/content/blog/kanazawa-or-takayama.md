> "Which is better for 1 night after Shirakawago: Kanazawa or Takayama?"

**This is a tough question!** Both are amazing. Here's my breakdown.

---

## Quick Answer

> "Which should I choose?"

**For 1 night:** Takayama is the easy win

**Staying longer (2-3 nights):** Kanazawa has more to offer

---

## Why Takayama for 1 Night

> "Tell me about Takayama"

**Takayama is:**
- Small and quiet
- Much closer to Shirakawago (about 1 hour by bus)
- Traditional Edo-period feel
- Perfect for short stay

**Don't miss:** Hida Beef – It's delicious!

---

## Why Kanazawa for Longer Stays

> "What about Kanazawa?"

**Kanazawa offers:**
- Bigger city with more to do
- Beautiful gardens and museums
- Great food scene

**Must-see spots:**
- Kenrokuen Garden
- Kanazawa Castle
- Omicho Market

If you have 2-3 nights, Kanazawa is better!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Choose based on travel time
- Know Takayama is closer
- Visit Kanazawa for longer trips
- Enjoy both destinations

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **1 night = Takayama** – Easier logistics

2. **2-3 nights = Kanazawa** – More things to see

3. **Try Hida Beef** – Takayama specialty

4. **Omicho Market** – Great seafood in Kanazawa

5. **Book accommodation early** – Both are popular
