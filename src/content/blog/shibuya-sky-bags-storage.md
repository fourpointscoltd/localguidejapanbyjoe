> "Can I store my luggage at Shibuya Sky? I have bags with me."

**Yes!** There are lockers at Shibuya Sky and many options nearby.

---

## Shibuya Sky Lockers

> "Are there lockers at Shibuya Sky itself?"

**Yes, in Shibuya Scramble Square:**
- Lockers on B2 floor
- Different sizes available
- ¥400-700 depending on size
- Can fill up on busy days

---

## Nearby Options

> "What if those are full?"

**Shibuya Station lockers:**
- Many locker areas in the station
- Multiple sizes including large suitcase size
- Check near ticket gates
- Use IC card or coins

**Ecbo Cloak service:**
- App-based luggage storage
- Leave bags at shops and cafes
- Very convenient, book in advance
- Around ¥400-800 per bag

---

## Tips for Shibuya Sky Visit

> "Any advice for visiting?"

**My tips:**
- Store bags before going up
- Evening visits are popular – book tickets ahead
- Sunset time is most crowded
- Rooftop can be windy – secure loose items

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use station lockers frequently
- Travel light when sightseeing
- Store bags at hotels if possible
- Use Ecbo Cloak app for convenience

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Hotel storage** – Most hotels keep bags before/after check-in

2. **Shibuya Station is huge** – Give yourself time to find lockers

3. **Peak hours** – Lockers fill up afternoons and weekends

4. **Coin lockers** – Bring ¥100 coins or use IC card

5. **Large bags** – Fewer lockers available, use luggage services instead
