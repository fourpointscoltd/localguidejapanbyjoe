### Conclusion

If you're visiting Tokyo in July and looking for a **resort-style pool (not an onsen)**, your best options are:

- **Tokyo Summerland** for the biggest pool & theme park combo
- **Yomiuriland Pool WAI** for a family-friendly water park
- **Keio Plaza Hotel Sky Pool** for a convenient city escape 🏙️💦

---
### General Information

Unlike many countries where hotels have year-round pools, **pools in Japan are often seasonal** and open only during the summer (usually **July to early September**).
Also, most “resorts” in Japan focus on **onsen (hot springs)**, so it’s important to look specifically for summer pools.

Here are the top resort-style pool experiences around Tokyo in July — no hot springs involved!

---
### 🏄‍♂️ Best for Big Pools & Adventure

#### Tokyo Summerland)

- **Japan’s largest pool park**, combined with a theme park
- Includes:
Huge wave pool 🌊
- Lazy river
- Indoor & outdoor areas
- Multiple water slides
- Unlimited rides with a 1-day pass

- **Location:** Akigawa (1 hour from Shinjuku via Keio Line to Keio-Hachioji)
- Great for: **Teens, adventure seekers, group fun**
- Website: [https://www.summerland.co.jp/](https://www.summerland.co.jp/)

💡 *Come early — it gets crowded during weekends in July.*

---
### 🛝 Best for Kids & Families

#### Yomiuriland Pool WAI

- Water park located **next to Yomiuriland amusement park**
- Includes:
Kids’ pool
- Lazy river
- Slides
- Amusement rides next door 🎡

- **Location:** Near Keio Yomiuriland Station (30 min from Shinjuku)
- Less crowded than Summerland
- Website: https://www.yomiuriland.com/wai/

💡 *Perfect for families with small children.*

---
### 🏙️ Best for Quick Access & Relaxation

#### Keio Plaza Hotel Sky Pool 

- Rooftop outdoor pool with a city view
- Small but stylish, **no need to leave Tokyo center**
- **Location:** Shinjuku, a 5-minute walk from the station
- For guests: Entry is discounted; non-guests may enter for a fee
- Great for: **Adults, travelers with limited time, or couples**
- Website: https://www.keioplaza.com/

💡 *A calm escape without leaving Tokyo — often overlooked by tourists.*

---
### Optional: Night Pool Experiences 🌙

Some pools offer **night pool access** during summer — with lights, music, and a resort atmosphere.
It’s more about the vibe than swimming — **great for Instagram or relaxing with friends**.

Check for availability at:

- Keio Plaza Hotel
- Other luxury hotels in Tokyo (e.g., Grand Nikko Tokyo Daiba, Hotel New Otani)

---
### My Personal Tip

If you want the **biggest, most exciting pool day**, go to **Tokyo Summerland**.
If you're traveling with kids or want to avoid huge crowds, **Yomiuriland Pool WAI** is a better fit.
And if you're short on time or staying in the city center, **Keio Plaza Hotel’s Sky Pool** is the most convenient.

All three options are worth considering depending on your goals.
Just remember: **bring a towel, sunscreen, and check the entry rules — tattoos are often not allowed at Japanese pools.**

---
### Travel Tips

- **Open season:** Early July to early September
- **Tattoos:** Many pools do not allow visible tattoos. Use cover stickers or rash guards
- **Footwear:** Bring flip-flops — shoes are not allowed near poolside
- **Book in advance** during busy weekends or holidays
- **Bring swimwear** — rentals are rare or expensive
