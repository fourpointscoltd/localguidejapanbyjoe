> "Where can I find unique breakfast in Japan?"

Japan has many breakfast options! Let me share my favorites.

---

## Budget-Friendly Options

> "What's affordable?"

**Family restaurants:**
- Denny's, Gusto, Jonathan's
- Open early, cheap sets
- Coffee refills often free

**Beef bowl chains:**
- Yoshinoya, Matsuya, Sukiya
- Breakfast sets from ¥350
- Fast and filling

---

## Traditional Japanese Breakfast

> "Where can I get a real Japanese breakfast?"

**MISOJYU in Asakusa**
- Delicious miso soup specialty
- Traditional Japanese style
- Beautiful presentation

**Tsukiji Honganji Cafe Tsumugi**
- Famous breakfast set
- No reservations – line up in the morning
- Worth the wait!

---

## Unique Experiences

> "Something really special?"

**World Breakfast in Ginza**
- Different country's breakfast each day
- Interesting and educational
- Great for food lovers

**Benizuru in Asakusa**
- Famous for fluffy pancakes
- Popular with locals and tourists

---

## Cafe Morning Sets

> "What about cafes?"

Japanese cafes often have "morning service":
- Toast, coffee, and salad
- Sometimes eggs
- Very affordable (¥400-600)
- Local favorite!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat breakfast at home
- Grab convenience store onigiri
- Use cafe morning sets
- Save restaurants for weekends

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Early start?** – Beef bowl chains open 24 hours

2. **Convenience stores** – Great quick breakfast

3. **Hotel breakfast** – Often included, try it once

4. **Coffee shops** – Morning sets are great value

5. **Weekend brunch** – Trendy spots get crowded
