> "Hey,
 I'm travelling to tokyo with my boyfriend for 3 days next month.
 One of the days we're planning to go to mount fuji but not sure what to do for the others.
 Seems so many different things to do and not sure what to pick!?"

**When packing for Japan, don't worry too much as buying locally is often cheaper thanks to the weak yen.** Just be sure to pack a few essentials: a power adapter (Type A or B), essential medications—especially motion sickness pills if you're sensitive to winding roads—and comfortable shoes, since you’ll be walking a lot. Larger shoe sizes can be hard to find in Japan, so bring those if needed. Otherwise, pack light and pick up anything else you might discover you need once you're there. It’s all about traveling smart and light!
