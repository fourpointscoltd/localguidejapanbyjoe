> "What is okonomiyaki exactly?"

**It's a savory Japanese pancake!** Made with batter, cabbage, and your choice of toppings.

---

## Two Main Styles

> "What's the difference between styles?"

**Osaka style:**
- Everything mixed together
- Ingredients in batter
- Flipped like a pancake
- Topped with sauce, mayo, bonito

**Hiroshima style:**
- Layered, not mixed
- Noodles included
- Much taller/thicker
- More complex to make

Both are delicious!

---

## Common Ingredients

> "What's in it?"

**Base ingredients:**
- Cabbage (lots of it!)
- Flour batter
- Egg
- Green onion

**Popular additions:**
- Pork belly
- Shrimp
- Squid
- Cheese
- Mochi

---

## How to Eat

> "Do I cook it myself?"

**Depends on the restaurant:**

**Self-cook:** You make it on the table griddle
**Staff-cook:** They make it, you eat

**Toppings:**
- Okonomiyaki sauce (sweet and savory)
- Japanese mayo
- Bonito flakes (they dance!)
- Aonori (seaweed powder)

---

## Where to Try

> "Best places?"

**Osaka:** Dotonbori area has many shops
**Hiroshima:** Okonomimura building (multiple restaurants)
**Tokyo:** Monjayaki is Tokyo's version (runnier)

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Have regional pride in their style
- Know how to cook at home
- Use the small spatula to eat
- Cut pieces and share at the table

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Osaka for beginners** – Easier to make yourself

2. **Hiroshima is filling** – Noodles make it hearty

3. **Don't press down** – Let it cook naturally

4. **Use the spatula** – Cut and eat with it

5. **Lunch special** – Often cheaper than dinner
