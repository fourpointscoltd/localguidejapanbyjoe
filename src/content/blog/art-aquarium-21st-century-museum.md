> "Can you recommend unique art and design places in Japan?"

Japan has some truly one-of-a-kind art experiences that you won't find anywhere else. Let me share my favorites!

---

## In Tokyo

> "What's special in Tokyo?"

### Art Aquarium (Nihonbashi)

This is unlike any museum you've seen:
- **Living goldfish** displayed as art
- Beautiful lighting and traditional Japanese design
- Feels magical and dreamlike
- Perfect for photos

The space blends traditional Japanese beauty with living creatures. It's really something special.

**Details:**
- Location: Nihonbashi, Tokyo
- Takes about 1-2 hours
- Can get crowded – weekdays are better

### teamLab Borderless / Planets

If you haven't heard of teamLab:
- **Digital art** that responds to your movement
- Immersive rooms you walk through
- Very popular – book tickets in advance!
- Planets has water areas (wear shorts)

These are probably Japan's most famous modern art experiences.

---

## Beyond Tokyo

> "What about outside Tokyo?"

### 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art (Kanazawa)

This is worth a special trip:
- **Circular building** – the architecture itself is art
- Interactive exhibits you can touch and enter
- Famous "Swimming Pool" installation
- Blurs the line between art and environment

Located in Kanazawa (about 2.5 hours from Tokyo by Shinkansen). Combine with Kenrokuen Garden!

### Naoshima Art Island

For serious art lovers:
- **Entire island** dedicated to art
- Yayoi Kusama's yellow pumpkin sculpture
- Chichu Art Museum (underground!)
- Takes a full day or overnight

Located in the Seto Inland Sea, between Okayama and Takamatsu.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit art museums on rainy days
- Book teamLab tickets in advance
- Make day trips to Kanazawa for the museum
- Combine art with other sightseeing

Art museums are popular date spots too!

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book teamLab ahead** – Tickets sell out, especially weekends

2. **Kanazawa makes a great day trip** – Or combine with Shirakawa-go

3. **Art Aquarium is small** – 1-2 hours is enough

4. **Naoshima needs planning** – Check ferry schedules

5. **Weekdays are best** – All these places get crowded on weekends

6. **Combine activities** – Nihonbashi has great food near Art Aquarium
