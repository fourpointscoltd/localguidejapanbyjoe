> "Is the water in Japan safe to drink, or should I buy bottled water?"

**Yes, tap water in Japan is safe!** You can drink it anywhere in the country.

---

## Why Japan's Water Is Safe

> "What makes it so clean?"

Japan has:
- **Lots of rain** – Fresh water is abundant
- **Many mountains** – Natural filtration
- **Strict standards** – Government regulations
- **Modern infrastructure** – Clean pipes and treatment

Tap water here is cleaner than bottled water in many countries!

---

## Should You Still Buy Bottled Water?

> "Is there any reason to buy bottles?"

**Maybe, just in case:**
- Your stomach might need time to adjust to different water
- Some people are sensitive to mineral differences
- Hotel room tap water is always fine

**My advice:** Try the tap water first. If you feel okay, you don't need to buy bottles.

---

## Where to Get Free Water

> "Can I refill my bottle?"

- **Tap anywhere** – Restaurants, hotels, public restrooms
- **Water fountains** – In parks and stations
- **Ask at restaurants** – They'll give you tap water for free

Bring a reusable bottle and save money!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Drink tap water every day
- Only buy bottled water for convenience (cold, portable)
- Never worry about water safety
- Use tap water for cooking, tea, everything

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Carry a bottle** – Refill anywhere for free

2. **Restaurant water** – Always free tap water with meals

3. **Vending machines** – For cold water on hot days

4. **Mountain springs** – Some areas have famous spring water

5. **Hotels** – Tap water in your room is perfectly safe
