> "Where can I get amazing matcha desserts in Tokyo?"

**Tokyo has incredible matcha options!** From traditional tea houses to trendy cafes.

---

## What to Try

> "What matcha desserts exist?"

**Popular options:**
- Matcha parfait
- Matcha soft serve
- Matcha tiramisu
- Matcha cheesecake
- Matcha latte
- Matcha shaved ice (kakigori)
- Matcha warabi mochi

---

## Best Spots in Tokyo

> "Where should I go?"

**Nana's Green Tea**
- Many locations
- Good quality, reasonable prices
- Modern Japanese cafe style

**Saryo Tsujiri**
- Ginza location popular
- Traditional Kyoto brand
- High-quality matcha

**Suzukien (Asakusa)**
- World's strongest matcha ice cream
- 7 levels of matcha intensity
- Instagram famous!

**Marufuji Kafe (Shibuya)**
- Matcha tiramisu
- Cozy atmosphere
- Less crowded

---

## Matcha Quality Levels

> "What's the difference?"

**Matcha grades:**
- Ceremonial grade – Highest quality, for drinking
- Culinary grade – For cooking and desserts
- Both can be delicious in different ways!

**Look for:** Uji matcha (from Kyoto) – considered best quality

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Love matcha but don't eat it daily
- Know good matcha should be slightly bitter
- Prefer less sweet versions
- Visit Kyoto for best matcha experiences

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Sweetness varies** – Ask for less sugar if you prefer

2. **Asakusa for unique** – Try the 7-level matcha ice cream

3. **Pair with wagashi** – Traditional sweets balance bitterness

4. **Department store cafes** – Often have great matcha desserts

5. **Kyoto trip** – Even better matcha options there!
