### **Conclusion**

If you're looking for nightlife in Tokyo, **Shinjuku is one of the best places to explore**. Whether you want to experience tiny bars, late-night izakayas, or unique themed spots, Shinjuku has it all.
I personally don’t go to bars often, so I can't recommend specific places. But if you speak English, I strongly suggest visiting **Golden Gai** — it’s safe, interesting, and full of character. You can look inside each bar from the outside, and **just walk into one that feels right** for you.

---
### 🍸 **Best Bars & Izakayas in Shinjuku**

#### 🔹 **Golden Gai – Tiny, Unique Bars with Local Vibes**

Golden Gai is a must-visit for anyone who wants to bar-hop in Tokyo. It’s a **small neighborhood filled with over 200 bars**, often only 4–10 seats each. Each bar has its own theme, music, and regulars. Many places have a **small cover charge (¥500–¥1,000)**, but the intimate experience is worth it.

**Top Picks:**

- **Albatross** – Vintage decor, rooftop seating, good cocktails
- **Bar Champion** – Classic rock bar, cheap drinks, friendly atmosphere
- **Ken’s Bar** – Tourist-friendly, cozy, great conversations
- **Deathmatch in Hell** – Horror-themed with heavy metal music

📌 **Tips:**

- Some bars are for **regulars only** — look for signs that say **“Tourists OK”**
- Many bars are **cash-only**, so bring yen
- If one bar feels full or uncomfortable, just try the next — it’s part of the fun!

---
#### 🔹 [**Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) – Old-School Izakayas**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g14133673-d1173749-Reviews-Omoide_Yokocho-Nishishinjuku_Shinjuku_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)

Nicknamed **“Piss Alley”** (though it's cleaner than it sounds!), this narrow alley is full of **tiny restaurants and izakayas** serving yakitori, sake, beer, and regional specialties.

**Top Picks:**

- **Tachibana Shokudo** – Known for horse meat sashimi & grilled skewers
- **Kabuto** – Specializes in eel skewers (unagi & anago)
- **Kushiya Monogatari** – All-you-can-eat kushiage (deep-fried skewers)

📌 **Tips:**

- Very **crowded after 8 PM**, so go early for a seat
- Expect a **lively, smoky, and loud** environment

---
#### 🔹 **High-End Bars – Craft Cocktails & Whisky**

If you want a **quiet, classy drink**, Shinjuku also offers world-class cocktail bars.

**Top Picks:**

- **Bar Benfiddich** – No menu, tell the bartender what you like
- **Bar Orchard Ginza (Shinjuku branch)** – Fruit-based cocktails, calm atmosphere
- **Ishinohana** – Famous for Japanese whisky and award-winning bartenders

📌 **Tips:**

- Many of these bars **require reservations** or have **limited seating**
- **Smart casual dress code** is often expected

---
#### 🔹 **Shinjuku Ni-Chome – LGBTQ+ Friendly Nightlife**

Ni-Chome is **Japan’s biggest LGBTQ+ district**, filled with welcoming bars, drag shows, and diverse crowds.

**Top Picks:**

- **AiiRO Café** – Great for first-time visitors
- **Campy! Bar** – Run by drag queens, tourist-friendly
- **Arty Farty** – Lively dance club with mixed crowds and good music

📌 **Tips:**

- Some bars have a **cover charge**, often includes one drink
- **Very foreigner-friendly**, so feel free to explore without worry

---
### 🎤 **Other Fun Nightlife Ideas in Shinjuku**

- **Karaoke Kan** – 24/7 karaoke, all-you-can-drink plans
- **Samurai Bar Tokyo** – Try on samurai armor while enjoying drinks
- **Club WARP** – Big dance club with local & international DJs

---
### 🚦 **Nightlife Tips for Shinjuku**

🔹 **Cash is King** – Many small bars don’t accept cards
🔹 **Watch Out for Scams** – Especially around **Kabukicho**, avoid pushy promoters
🔹 **Last Train is at Midnight** – Miss it, and a taxi to central Tokyo costs ~¥4,000+
🔹 **Bars Open Late** – Many open around **7 PM and close around 4–5 AM**

---
### 📌 Final Thoughts

Even if you're not a big drinker (like me), **Shinjuku at night is full of interesting places** to explore.
You don’t need a plan — just **walk around Golden Gai**, peek into a few bars, and see what feels right. You might find an unforgettable experience in a bar with just five seats and great conversation.
