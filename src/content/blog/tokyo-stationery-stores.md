> "Can you please suggest the biggest and best stationery stores to visit in Tokyo-daughters wish list. Kia ora."

**Guess what?** You should definitely check out Itoya in Ginza—it's like the legend of stationery shops in Tokyo! If you're rushing, no worries, just pop into bookstores or stores like Loft and Tokyu Hands; they're everywhere and full of quality finds. and a hidden gem? KAKIMORI in Kuramae. It’s near Asakusa, and they’ve got awesome notebooks and fountain pens. While you're there, explore the area for other cool shops like mt lab. for fancy masking tapes. Also, don’t miss TAKEO in Jimbocho and Omotesando for some exquisite paper goods. Happy stationery shopping!
