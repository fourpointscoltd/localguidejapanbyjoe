> "Do I need to bring an umbrella to Japan?"

**You can easily buy one there!** Japan has great umbrella culture.

---

## Buying Umbrellas

> "Where can I get one?"

**Available everywhere:**
- Convenience stores (¥500-1,000)
- 100 yen shops (basic ones)
- Department stores (nice ones)
- Umbrella specialty stores

**Clear vinyl umbrellas** are iconic Japanese style!

---

## Types of Umbrellas

> "What kinds exist?"

**Common types:**
- **Vinyl umbrella** – Cheap, clear, disposable feeling
- **Folding umbrella** – Compact, carry always
- **Parasol** – For sun protection
- **Large umbrella** – Sturdier, longer lasting

---

## Umbrella Etiquette

> "Are there rules?"

**Yes!**
- Close when entering buildings
- Use umbrella stands at entrances
- Don't shake water on others
- Keep it close in crowds
- Don't steal others' umbrellas!

---

## Umbrella Stands

> "What about stores?"

**At entrances:**
- Use umbrella stands provided
- Some have plastic bags for wet umbrellas
- Take the RIGHT umbrella when leaving
- Consider bringing your own lock

**Umbrellas get "borrowed"** from stands – be careful!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Own multiple umbrellas
- Accept losing vinyl umbrellas
- Use parasols in summer
- Always carry folding umbrella

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Buy cheap** – You might lose it anyway

2. **Folding umbrella** – Keep in your bag always

3. **Rainy season** – June-July, always have one

4. **Parasols** – Women use them for sun protection

5. **Unique umbrella** – Mark yours to identify it
