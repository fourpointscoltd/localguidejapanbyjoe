Looking for skiing accessible by Shinkansen from Tokyo? Here are the best options!

## Top Recommendations

### GALA Yuzawa (My #1 Pick)
- **Travel time:** 77 minutes from Tokyo Station
- **Why it's great:** Station directly connected to slopes!
- **Best for:** Day trips, beginners to intermediate
- **Unique:** No bus transfer needed - ski from the station
- **Cost:** ~¥5,500 lift ticket

### Echigo-Yuzawa Area
- **Travel time:** 70-90 minutes
- **Resorts nearby:**
  - Naeba (large, varied terrain)
  - Kagura (powder, advanced)
  - Ishiuchi Maruyama (family-friendly)
- **Note:** Short bus/taxi from station

### Karuizawa Prince Hotel Ski Resort
- **Travel time:** 60-70 minutes
- **Best for:** Beginners, families, shopping after
- **Bonus:** Huge outlet mall adjacent
- **Snow:** Can be limited (lower altitude)

## Day Trip Tips

1. **Leave early** - First Shinkansen around 6 AM
2. **Rent equipment there** - Available at all resorts
3. **Buy combo tickets** - Shinkansen + lift discount packages
4. **Last train** - Check schedule (around 9 PM)

## JR Pass Holders

Your pass covers the Shinkansen portion! Just pay for:
- Lift tickets
- Equipment rental
- Shuttle buses (if needed)

GALA Yuzawa is perfect for JR Pass holders - truly door-to-door convenience!
