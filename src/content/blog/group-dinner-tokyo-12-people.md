If you're planning a dinner for a group of 12 in Tokyo and need a reservation on short notice, my personal recommendation is **TWO ROOMS** in Nihonbashi.

## Why TWO ROOMS?

- **Directly connected** to the station (no walking in bad weather)
- **Large group seating** available
- **English menu** and English-speaking staff
- **Reasonable prices** for the quality
- **Course menus** make ordering for groups easy

## Other Good Options for Large Groups

### Izakaya Style
- **Torikizoku** - Budget-friendly, all items ¥350
- **Uotami** - Seafood focus, private rooms available
- **Watami** - Chain with reliably good food

### Yakiniku (BBQ)
- **Gyukaku** - Fun interactive dining
- **Stamina-en** - Higher quality cuts
- Both have group seating

### Course Meal Restaurants
- **Gonpachi** (the "Kill Bill" restaurant)
- **Ninja Akasaka** - Theatrical experience
- **Zauo** - Fish-your-own-dinner

## Tips for Large Groups

1. **Book 1-2 weeks ahead** for private rooms
2. **Set a budget beforehand** - Course meals help control costs
3. **Consider nomihodai** (all-you-can-drink) deals
4. **Ask about party menus** - Often better value
5. **Confirm the total cost** including tax and service

## My Honest Advice

Chain izakayas might not be "Instagram-worthy," but they handle large groups very well and won't break the bank. For 12 people, practicality matters!

Price range: ¥3,000-5,000 per person with drinks.
