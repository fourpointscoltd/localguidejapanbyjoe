> "How do izakayas work? I'm confused about ordering."

**Izakayas are Japanese pubs!** They're social places for food and drinks. Here's how they work.

---

## How Izakayas Work

> "What's the basic system?"

**When you arrive:**
- You'll be seated at a table
- Get an "otoshi" (small appetizer) – this is a cover charge
- Order drinks first
- Then order food throughout the night

**Style:**
- Order many small dishes to share
- Keep ordering as you drink
- Stay as long as you like

---

## What to Order

> "What should I try?"

**Popular izakaya foods:**
- **Yakitori** – Grilled chicken skewers
- **Edamame** – Salted soybeans
- **Karaage** – Fried chicken
- **Gyoza** – Pan-fried dumplings
- **Tamagoyaki** – Sweet omelet
- **Sashimi** – Fresh raw fish
- **Fried potatoes** – Japanese style

**Drinks:**
- **Beer** – Most popular choice
- **Highball** – Whisky and soda
- **Sake** – Traditional rice wine
- **Shochu** – Japanese spirit
- **Chuhai** – Fruity cocktails

---

## Ordering Tips

> "How do I actually order?"

**Easy methods:**
- Picture menus are common
- Point at what you want
- Use Google Translate on menus
- Say item name + "kudasai" (please)

**Useful phrases:**
- "Sumimasen" – Excuse me (to call staff)
- "Onegaishimasu" – Please
- "Okaikei" – Check please

---

## Otoshi Explained

> "Why did I get food I didn't order?"

**This is normal!**
- Otoshi is a small appetizer
- Acts as a cover charge (¥300-500)
- You can't refuse it usually
- Just enjoy it!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Go with coworkers or friends
- Order drinks first, food later
- Share everything on the table
- Stay for 2-3 hours
- End with "Kanpai!" (Cheers!)

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Seating charge** – Expect otoshi on your bill, it's not a scam

2. **All-you-can-drink** – Called "nomihodai," good value

3. **Last order** – Usually 30 minutes before closing

4. **Smoking** – Some izakayas allow it, check before entering

5. **Reservations** – Helpful on weekends
