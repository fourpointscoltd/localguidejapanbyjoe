> "Is expensive sushi worth it compared to normal sushi places?"

**If it's within budget, try both!** Here's why.

---

## Why Expensive Sushi Is Different

> "What makes it special?"

**High-end sushi offers:**
- Best quality fish
- Skilled chef techniques
- Personal attention
- Unique taste experience

**For example:** I ate sea urchin at a high-end restaurant. It was amazing to compare different types of sea urchin!

---

## My Recommendations

> "Where should I go?"

**For high-end sushi:**
- Make a reservation
- Expect omakase (chef's choice)
- Budget more money

**For reasonable options:**
- **Sushiro** – Great conveyor belt sushi
- **Kura Sushi** – Also very popular

Both are delicious for the price!

---

## The Real Difference

> "What do I actually get?"

**Expensive sushi:**
- Fresh, premium ingredients
- Chef prepares in front of you
- Each piece is special
- Learn about the fish

**Conveyor belt sushi:**
- Good quality for price
- Fun atmosphere
- Many choices
- No reservation needed

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat conveyor belt sushi regularly
- Go to expensive sushi for special occasions
- Know both have their place
- Enjoy trying different styles

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Try both** – Different experiences

2. **Reserve for high-end** – Popular places book fast

3. **Lunch is cheaper** – Even at expensive places

4. **Sushiro and Kura Sushi** – Easy to find everywhere

5. **Watch the chef** – Part of the experience at high-end places
