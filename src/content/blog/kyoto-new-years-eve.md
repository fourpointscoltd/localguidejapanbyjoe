> "Hello I am planning a trip in Japan around New year eve. What is the best place to spend it? Thank you so much for your advices."

**Guess what?** If you're up for a traditional Japanese New Year, Kyoto is the place to be! Imagine ringing in the new year by visiting ancient shrines and temples—truly magical. Join the locals in end-of-year cleaning, then slurp some delicious soba noodles to welcome good luck. Don't miss the countdown events at shrines or lively outdoor concerts. And on New Year's Day, treat yourself to special dishes and try drawing an omikuji fortune slip for fun. It's a wonderfully unique way to start the year surrounded by rich culture and friendly faces.
