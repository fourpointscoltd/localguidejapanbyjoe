> "I love fresh quality coffee any suggestions for Tokyo.
 ??"

**you're in for a treat in Tokyo if you're after some quality coffee!** If you've enjoyed Kaikado Cafe in Kyoto, you've got a fine taste! In Tokyo, make sure to check out CAFE DE L'AMBRE right near Ginza for a classic vibe, or ignis in Sendagi if you're exploring that area. But guess what? For a real special cup, head over to GRAND CRU CAFÉ GINZA in the Ginza SIX shopping complex. It's fantastic! And for a more budget-friendly but equally delightful option, drop by BUNDOZA CAFE in Ginza. Their drip coffee? Simply amazing! Enjoy your coffee adventure!
