> "How much time should I plan for changing platform from subway to trains or buses, in big stations like Shinjuku (but even smaller one)? Is 30 minutes enough? I can't rely on google maps as they suggest few minutes for the change, but I've read that Shinjuku is huge and can be hard to navigate.
 just want to make sure I won't miss my connections"

**Shinjuku Station can be quite the adventure!** It's one of the busiest stations in the world, so changing platforms might take you about 10 to 20 minutes. It can feel like a maze especially if it's your first time, and yep, you might take a bit longer if you get turned around. A little tip - if you can, try to plan a route that avoids having to change at Shinjuku. It might save you some hassle and keep your trip smooth. But don't worry, once you get the hang of it, you'll navigate like a local!
