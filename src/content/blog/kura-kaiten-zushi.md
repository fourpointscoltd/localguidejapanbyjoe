> "Has anybody been to the Kura kaiten zushi at Tokyo Skytree? Is booking recommended? If so, how do I book? Thanks!"

**the Kura kaiten zushi at Tokyo Skytree is a popular spot!** You're right, you don't absolutely have to book ahead, but it's a good idea to secure a spot using the Kurashushi app, especially during busy times. Just a heads up—the app might be in Japanese only. If you find it tricky, no worries. A little tip: sometimes just popping in to check for a free table works out too, though weekends and holidays can get pretty crowded. Enjoy the sushi experience—it’s so much fun watching the little plates zoom by on the conveyor belt!
