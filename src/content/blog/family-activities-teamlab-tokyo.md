### Conclusion

Although TeamLab Tokyo is a great experience, the surrounding area is mostly residential. **So I think you should skip Toyosu after going to Teamlab.**
For better family fun, it’s a good idea to explore nearby spots like **Ariake Garden**, **Lalaport Toyosu**, or **Tokyo Big Sight**.

---
### General Information

TeamLab Planets is located in the **Toyosu/Ariake** area of Tokyo, which is mostly a modern residential district. While it’s peaceful and clean, it’s not the best area for sightseeing after your TeamLab visit.

If you're visiting with family on a Friday, there are a few good options nearby, but you might want to hop over to another area depending on your schedule.

![](https://www.localguide-japan.com/content/images/2025/03/30690j_short_rinji_002_pixta.jpg)*Tokyo big sight*

---
### Recommended Nearby Spots

#### Ariake Garden

A new, family-friendly shopping and entertainment complex.

- Kids' play areas
- Food court and restaurants
- Large public bath facility (onsen-style) 🛁
- Hotel Villa Fontaine for those who want to stay nearby

📍 Just one train stop from TeamLab (Ariake Station)

#### Lalaport Toyosu

One of Tokyo’s most popular bay-side shopping malls.

- Indoor playgrounds and kid zones
- Seaside park and walking area 🌿
- Wide selection of restaurants and cafes
- Beautiful sunset views over the Tokyo Bay

💡 *Perfect for a relaxed afternoon with kids*

#### Tokyo Big Sight

While mainly a convention center, there are often **family-friendly exhibitions or pop-up events** on Fridays.

- Check event calendars beforehand
- Usually quiet and spacious
- Nearby promenade for walks 🚶

---
### My Personal Opinion

I used to live near TeamLab Tokyo, and I can honestly say the area is **not ideal for sightseeing** beyond the museum itself.
It’s clean and calm, but not much happens there unless you’re visiting a specific attraction.

So, I **highly recommend taking the train** just 10–15 minutes to places like Toyosu, Odaiba, or even Ginza if you're looking for more entertainment, shopping, or food options.
But if you're short on time, **Ariake Garden** is your best nearby bet.

---
### Suggested Itinerary (Family-Friendly)

**Morning:**

- Visit TeamLab Planets (Book tickets in advance)

**Afternoon:**

- Walk or take the Yurikamome Line to **Lalaport Toyosu**
- Lunch and indoor play time 🧸

**Evening (optional):**

- Go to **Ariake Garden** for dinner and relax at the public bath
- Or take a short ride to Odaiba for a night view of Rainbow Bridge 🌉

---
### Travel Tips

- **Weekdays are less crowded**, including Friday mornings
- **Yurikamome Line** is scenic and fun for kids — it’s an automated train with views of the bay
- **Bring a stroller** for small kids — distances between spots can be wide
