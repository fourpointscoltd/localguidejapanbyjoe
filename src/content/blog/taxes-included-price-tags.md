> "hey & When it comes to price tags displayed in stores, is tax included in the displayed price?"

**In Japan, when you look at price tags in stores, the price you see already includes tax.** This started about two years ago. So, the amount shown is exactly what you will pay at the register. However, be aware that some places like restaurants might add a service charge to the bill. This extra charge is separate from the tax and should be listed on the menu. If not, feel free to ask the staff about any added charges. Generally, shopping in Japan makes it easy because you don't have to do any extra math to figure out the final price. What you see on the price tag is what you pay, so no surprises there!
