## **Top Sake Brands to Buy**

When choosing a sake gift, **quality matters**. Here are three of the most highly regarded sake brands in Japan:

### **1. Dassai (獺祭)**

One of Japan’s most famous premium sakes, Dassai is known for its smooth, refined taste. It’s made with highly polished rice, which gives it a clean and slightly fruity flavor. **Dassai 23** is particularly sought after, as it has an incredibly pure taste.

### **2. Hakkaisan (八海山)**

This Niigata-based sake is well-balanced and pairs well with food. If you like dry and crisp sake, Hakkaisan is an excellent choice. It’s often recommended for first-time sake drinkers because of its smooth taste.

### **3. Juyondai (十四代)**

For true sake connoisseurs, Juyondai is a legendary brand. It’s rare, expensive, and highly praised for its deep, complex flavors. If you can find a bottle, it makes an amazing gift for any sake lover.

## **Try Before You Buy: Sake Genka Bars**

If you’re unsure about which sake to bring home, **Sake Genka Bars** are the perfect place to sample different varieties at affordable prices. These bars let you try sake by the glass, often in **cute little cups**, so you can explore different styles without committing to a full bottle.

Most sake bars will also have **seasonal or local sakes** that you might not find elsewhere. So if you're feeling adventurous, it’s a great way to taste something unique before making your final purchase.

## **A Unique Twist: Sake-Flavored Treats**

If you want a fun and unexpected sake-related souvenir, why not try **sake-flavored ice cream**? Some specialty shops in Japan offer creamy, sake-infused ice cream that balances the richness of dairy with the subtle sweetness of sake. It’s a great way to introduce non-drinkers to the taste of sake without the alcohol content.

While you can’t take the ice cream home, it’s definitely a must-try experience while you're in Japan!

## **Exploring the Future of Sake**

Sake-making is a centuries-old tradition in Japan, but it’s also evolving. **Aoki Risa**, a 38-year-old from Mie Prefecture with a background in finance, is bringing a fresh perspective to the industry.

In **September 2024**, she will take over the management of **Takahashi Sake Brewery**, a historic sake producer in **Nagaoka, Niigata**, with over **160 years of history**. Her mission? To bring **new life** to the sake industry while preserving its deep-rooted traditions.

## **Final Thoughts**

Sake makes for an excellent gift, whether for friends, family, or even for yourself! Whether you choose **Dassai, Hakkaisan, or Juyondai**, you’re bringing back a piece of Japanese culture. And if you have the chance, visit a **Sake Genka Bar** to explore different flavors before you buy.

Japan has so many unique ways to experience sake, so enjoy the journey of discovering the perfect one for you!
