## Finding Your Size

> "How do I know my Japan size?"

**Easy method:**
- Measure your foot in centimeters
- That's roughly your Japanese size!
- Try on shoes to confirm

**Tip:** Japanese shoes can run narrow. Try before you buy!

## Common Issues

> "What problems might I face?"

**Challenges:**
- Narrow widths (especially for Western feet)
- Large sizes (28cm+) limited
- Wide sizes (EE, EEE) available at some stores
- Staff may not speak English

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Know their cm size
- Try shoes on in store
- Look for comfort over style often
- Change shoes for different occasions

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Measure in cm** – Easiest way to find your size

2. **Try before buying** – Sizes can vary by brand

3. **Wide feet?** – Look for "wide" or "EE" options

4. **Tax-free** – Available at many shoe stores over ¥5,000

5. **Train station shops** – Often have shoe stores
