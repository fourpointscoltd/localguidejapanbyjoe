> "How can I make the most of my Tokyo Disney visit?"

**Tokyo Disney is amazing but crowded!** Here are my tips for a better experience.

---

## Disneyland vs DisneySea

> "Which park should I choose?"

**Tokyo Disneyland**
- Classic Disney experience
- Best for families with kids
- Familiar rides and characters
- Similar to other Disney parks worldwide

**Tokyo DisneySea**
- Unique to Japan
- More adult-oriented
- Beautiful theming
- Better for couples/adults

**My recommendation:** DisneySea if you only have one day!

---

## Beating the Crowds

> "How do I avoid long lines?"

**Tips:**
- Go on weekdays (avoid weekends!)
- Arrive before opening (30 minutes early)
- Use the Disney Premier Access (paid FastPass)
- Download the Tokyo Disney app
- Check crowd calendars online

**Worst times:**
- Japanese holidays
- Spring break (March-April)
- Halloween and Christmas seasons

---

## Must-Do Attractions

> "What should I prioritize?"

**Disneyland:**
- Big Thunder Mountain
- Splash Mountain
- Space Mountain
- Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast (new!)

**DisneySea:**
- Tower of Terror
- Journey to the Center of the Earth
- Soaring: Fantastic Flight
- Toy Story Mania

---

## Food Tips

> "Is the food good?"

**Yes! Try:**
- Popcorn in unique flavors
- Turkey legs
- Character-themed snacks
- Seasonal limited items

**Tip:** Restaurant reservations help avoid wait times!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Buy annual passes
- Go on weekdays when possible
- Know which rides to prioritize
- Collect limited merchandise

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Buy tickets online** – Can sell out on busy days

2. **Stay at Disney hotels** – Get early entry benefit

3. **Bring portable charger** – App uses lots of battery

4. **Wear comfortable shoes** – Lots of walking

5. **Check show schedules** – Parades are amazing!
