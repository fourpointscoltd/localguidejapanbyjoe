> "Hi, so I am planning to visit Japan for a week (I know not enough but I'll fit it in ©)
 Anyway we will be in Thailand for 3 weeks & want to go to Japan. My question is... is it worth to buy JR rail card? As I'll fit Kyoto & Osaka in this 1 week trip"

**Absolutely, thinking about a JR Pass for your week in Japan?** Great idea to check if it's worth it for your trip. There's a handy calculator over at JapanStation that can help you see if the pass pays off based on your travel plans. Here's the link: [Japan Rail Pass Value Calculator](https://www.japanstation.com/japan-rail-pass-value-calculator/). Remember, if you're not hopping between cities a lot, you might not need one. Plus, depending on where you're going, buses or taxis could be a smarter pick. Definitely worth a little research to make the best choice!
