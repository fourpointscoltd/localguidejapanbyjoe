> "Hey guys we are planning to rent a vehicle in Narita or Tokyo on January. Do accept Canadian international license? This our first time driving a right hand vehicle lol"

**exciting plans!** Yes, with a Canadian international driving license, you're good to go for driving in Japan. Just a heads up, since cars here are right-hand drive, things like the turn signal and windshield wiper controls are flipped too—might take a bit to get used to! Plus, remember to watch out for those tricky no lane change signs and one-way streets. and since you'll be here in January, if you’re heading to the mountains, don't forget to gear up for the snow, like having chains ready. Drive safe and enjoy the ride!
