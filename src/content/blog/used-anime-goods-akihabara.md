> "Where can I find secondhand anime goods in Akihabara?"

Akihabara has many shops for used anime items! You can save a lot of money.

---

## Best Secondhand Shops

> "Which stores do you recommend?"

**Mandarake**
- Multiple floors of used goods
- Figures, manga, doujinshi, games
- Good prices, great selection
- My top recommendation!

**Surugaya**
- Large chain store
- Organized by series
- Good for specific items

**Lashinbang**
- Good for female-oriented goods
- Also has general anime items
- Multiple locations

**Book Off**
- Not anime-specific but has a section
- Great for manga and games
- Very cheap prices

---

## What Can You Find?

> "What kind of items?"

**Common secondhand items:**
- Figures and figurines
- Manga (Japanese and some English)
- DVDs and Blu-rays
- Video games
- Trading cards
- Posters and art books
- Keychains and small goods
- Cosplay items

---

## Tips for Shopping

> "Any advice?"

**My tips:**
- Check condition carefully – "A rank" is like new
- Compare prices between shops
- Rare items sell fast
- Bring cash – Some small shops don't take cards
- Weekday mornings are less crowded

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Hunt for specific rare items
- Sell their old items to these shops
- Compare prices online first
- Visit multiple stores in one trip

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Mandarake first** – Best selection and fair prices

2. **Tax-free available** – Bring your passport

3. **Negotiate rarely works** – Prices are usually fixed

4. **Check for defects** – Open boxes are often checked items

5. **Shipping overseas** – Some shops offer this service
