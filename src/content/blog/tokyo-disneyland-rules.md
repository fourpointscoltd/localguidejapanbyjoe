> "ディズニーランドで持ち込めないものは？"

**Heading to Disneyland in Japan?** Well, you'll need to leave a few things at home. You can't bring any cans, bottles, or alcoholic drinks. But guess what? Bringing water in pet bottles is totally fine. Just make sure not to pack more than a light snack for food—no big meals allowed! Also, leave behind any big luggage like suitcases, hard cases, or carry-ons. And drones? Definitely a no-go. So pack light and get ready for the fun!
