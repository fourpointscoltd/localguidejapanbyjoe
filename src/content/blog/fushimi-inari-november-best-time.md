If you want to avoid crowds at Fushimi Inari Taisha in Kyoto during November, I'd say aim to arrive around **8 a.m. or even earlier**.

## Why Early Morning?

From about 10 a.m. onward, tour buses start rolling in and the place gets noticeably busier. The shrine grounds, including the famous thousand torii gates, are open **24 hours**, so you can actually visit at any time!

## Best Times to Visit

| Time | Crowd Level | Experience |
|------|-------------|------------|
| 6-8 AM | Very quiet | Mystical, photo-perfect |
| 8-10 AM | Light | Good balance |
| 10 AM-4 PM | Crowded | Busy, harder to get photos |
| 4-6 PM | Moderate | Nice sunset light |
| After dark | Quiet | Atmospheric, lit paths |

## November Specifics

November is **autumn foliage season** in Kyoto, which means:
- **More tourists** overall in Kyoto
- **Beautiful colors** around the shrine
- **Pleasant weather** for hiking

## Pro Tips

1. **Weekdays are better** than weekends
2. **Walk past the first gates** - crowds thin as you go higher
3. **Go all the way up** - Takes 2-3 hours roundtrip but much quieter
4. **Sunset visits** can be magical (and less crowded)

## Getting There Early

- **First trains** from Kyoto Station arrive around 5:30 AM
- **It's a 5-minute walk** from Inari Station (JR Nara Line)
- **Bring a flashlight** if arriving before dawn

The early effort is absolutely worth it for those iconic empty-gate photos!
