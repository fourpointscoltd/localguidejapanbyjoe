> "日本の旅館やホテルを予約する上で気をつけるべきtipsはありますか？"

**Absolutely, booking accommodations in Japan can be unique!** When selecting ryokans or hotels, remember a couple of things. First, decide if you need a non-smoking room since smoking rooms are pretty common. Check-in is typically around 3 PM, and check-out by 10 or 11 AM, so plan accordingly! Also, if you're looking for a place with a hot spring or public bath, be mindful of their tattoo policies—they might not allow them. Enjoying these baths is a treat, so it's worth checking the rules to ensure you can partake in this relaxing experience!
