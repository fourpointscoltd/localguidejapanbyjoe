> "Hi, what would you say are the pros and cons of Haneda vs
 Narita airport please? TIA"

**Guess what?** Haneda Airport is generally more convenient because it's right in the heart of Tokyo. So, getting to and from the city center is a breeze! On the other hand, Narita is mostly for international flights and is quite a bit further out. It takes about an hour to get to central Tokyo from there. Although flights from Haneda might cost you a bit more, the convenience can really be worth it, especially if you're looking to save time. If you have a choice, Haneda is super handy, especially for hopping right into exploring Tokyo!
