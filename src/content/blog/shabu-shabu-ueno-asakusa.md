> "Can you recommend a good and reasonable place for shabu shabu or sukiyaki in Ueno and Asakusa?"

In Japan, lunch is super cheap, so if you eat fancy food for lunch, you can enjoy tasty stuff at a reasonable price!

---

## My Recommendations

> "What places do you suggest?"

**For shabu shabu:**
- **Hachinoki** – Casual shabu-shabu experience, great quality

**For yakiniku:**
- **Yansando** – Super tasty grilled meat

Both places offer delicious shabu-shabu and yakiniku for **less than ¥2,000** ($14) around Ueno!

---

## Lunch vs Dinner Prices

> "Why lunch?"

**The secret:**
- Same quality food
- Much lower prices
- Lunch sets include sides
- Less crowded too

This is how locals eat at nice restaurants without spending too much!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat expensive foods at lunch time
- Know the best value spots
- Look for lunch specials
- Save money this way

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Go for lunch** – Same quality, half the price

2. **Weekdays are best** – Less crowded

3. **Check lunch hours** – Usually 11 AM to 2 PM

4. **All-you-can-eat** – Some places offer it for lunch

5. **Reservations help** – Popular spots fill up
