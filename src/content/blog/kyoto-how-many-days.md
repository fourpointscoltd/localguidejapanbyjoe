> "Is 2 nights in Kyoto too much? Or is 2 days 1 night enough?"

**I used to live in Kyoto.** Now I live in Tokyo, but I still think about spending a week there!

---

## My Honest Answer

> "How long should I stay?"

**You can stay as long as you want!**

Visiting shrines and temples can be tiring, but:
- Great hotels
- Beautiful Kamogawa river
- Amazing restaurants
- Deep cultural experiences

**If you know the hidden spots, you can enjoy many days!**

---

## Minimum Time Needed

> "What if I only have limited time?"

**2 days 1 night:**
- Can see major temples
- Very rushed
- Limited exploration

**3-4 days:**
- More comfortable pace
- Time to explore neighborhoods
- Can try more food

---

## What to Do With More Time

> "What if I stay longer?"

**Beyond temples:**
- Walk along Kamogawa river
- Explore local neighborhoods
- Find hidden restaurants
- Visit less famous temples
- Enjoy traditional ryokan stay

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Take their time in Kyoto
- Know it's worth multiple visits
- Explore beyond tourist spots
- Enjoy the atmosphere

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Don't rush** – Kyoto rewards slow travel

2. **Stay near station** – Easy to get around

3. **Book ahead** – Hotels fill up quickly

4. **Evening walks** – Gion at night is beautiful

5. **Repeat visits** – Come back to see more
