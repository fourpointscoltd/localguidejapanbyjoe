> "Hello! Do I need to apply for an international driver's license if I will rent a car? Any experience about this? Thank you!"

**Absolutely, you'll need an International Driving Permit (IDP) plus your passport to rent a car in Japan.** Not all IDPs are accepted, so make sure yours is recognized in Japan before you travel. Keep an eye out for left-hand traffic and local driving rules, which are quite strict, especially in cities like Tokyo. Also, I'd skip driving in downtown Kyoto—those narrow, busy streets are a bit of a challenge! But don’t worry, with a rental, exploring Japan’s beautiful countryside becomes super convenient and enjoyable. It's definitely worth it for the freedom to roam!
