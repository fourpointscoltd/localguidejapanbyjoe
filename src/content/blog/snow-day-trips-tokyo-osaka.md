> "Where can I see beautiful snow landscapes as a day trip from Tokyo or Osaka？"

**For stunning snow landscapes accessible as a day trip from Tokyo, Nikko is a top choice.** Just a couple of hours away by train, you’ll find beautiful scenery especially around the historical shrines and Lake Chuzenji. The area is even more magical during the winter months with its snowy backdrop.

---

## Details

From Osaka, you can head to the town of Biwako Valley. It's about an hour away and offers breathtaking views of Lake Biwa, especially when covered in snow. You can enjoy activities like skiing or just take in the winter landscapes from the observation deck.

Both places not only provide spectacular snowy scenes but also the convenience of a short travel time, making them perfect for a day trip.
