If you're looking for personalized chopsticks in Japan, **Kyoto** is definitely one of the best places to start!

## Kyoto - Best Selection

### Ichihara Heibei Shoten
- **Location:** Near Kiyomizu-dera
- **Specialty:** Traditional lacquered chopsticks
- **Personalization:** Name engraving in Japanese or English
- **Price:** ¥2,000-10,000+ depending on quality

### Hashikura Ganso
- **Location:** Teramachi Shopping Arcade
- **Specialty:** Wide variety of styles
- **Service:** Can engrave while you wait

### Kyoto Handicraft Center
- **Good for:** One-stop souvenir shopping
- **Personalization:** Available on select items

## Osaka Options

### Doguyasuji (Kitchen Town)
- **Location:** Near Namba
- **Find:** Various kitchen supply shops with chopsticks
- **Note:** Engraving less common here

### Department Stores
- Daimaru, Takashimaya often have chopstick sections

## Hiroshima

Fewer dedicated shops, but check:
- **Omotesando** shopping street on Miyajima
- **Hiroshima Station** souvenir shops

## What to Know About Personalization

- **Japanese names** - Usually free or cheap
- **English/Romaji** - Most shops accommodate
- **Time needed** - 15-30 minutes typically
- **Characters available** - Some shops offer kanji selection for non-Japanese names

## Pro Tips

1. **Bring the exact spelling** you want engraved
2. **Consider wood type** - Different woods have different meanings
3. **Buy a nice case** - Makes it gift-ready
4. **Keep receipt** - Useful for customs

Personalized chopsticks make for a fantastic souvenir or gift!
