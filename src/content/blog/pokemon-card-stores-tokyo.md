Looking for Pokemon experiences beyond the cafes and Pokemon Centers? Here are my local recommendations!

## Top Pokemon Events

### Pikachu Outbreak (Yokohama)
- **When:** Usually August
- **What:** Hundreds of Pikachu dancing in the streets!
- **Where:** Minato Mirai, Yokohama
- **Cost:** Free to watch

### Pokemon Wonder (Yomiuriland)
- **What:** Outdoor nature walk with hidden Pokemon
- **When:** Seasonal (check dates)
- **Where:** Yomiuriland theme park, Tokyo

### Pokemon Halloween (USJ)
- **When:** October
- **Where:** Universal Studios Japan, Osaka
- **Special:** Exclusive merchandise

## Pokemon Card Stores

### Akihabara (Best Selection)
- **Yellow Submarine** - Multiple floors of cards
- **Trader** - Good for rare finds
- **Card Rush** - Competitive prices
- **Super Potato** - Vintage cards too

### Nakano Broadway
- **Mandarake** - Rare and vintage cards
- **Various small shops** - Dig for treasures

### Ikebukuro
- **Pokemon Center Mega Tokyo** - Official cards
- **Animate** - Trading card section

## What to Know About Card Shopping

- **Prices** - Can vary wildly between shops
- **Condition grading** - Japanese shops are strict (good thing!)
- **Language** - Cards are in Japanese
- **Receipt** - Keep for customs
- **Tax-free** - Available for tourists at larger shops

## Current Hot Items (2024-2025)

- **151 Set** - Nostalgic original Pokemon
- **Special Art Rares** - Beautiful full-art cards
- **Promo Cards** - Check what's exclusive to Japan

## Pro Tips

1. **Mornings** are less crowded in Akihabara
2. **Compare prices** at 2-3 shops before buying
3. **Check online** prices for reference (Mercari Japan)
4. **Bring card sleeves** to protect purchases

If you're curious about specific event dates or store locations, just ask - I'm always tracking what's happening around Japan!
