> "Which hotel provides shuttle bus service to Disneyland?"

**If you're planning a trip to Disneyland in Tokyo, staying at a hotel that offers a shuttle bus service is incredibly handy!** Some fantastic options include the Disney Ambassador Hotel, Hilton Tokyo Bay, and Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay. There's also the Grand Nikko Tokyo Bay Maihama and Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel. Just a heads up—due to COVID-19, some hotels have stopped their shuttle services or might require you to book a spot in advance, so it’s a good idea to check with the hotel for the latest updates on their shuttle services.
