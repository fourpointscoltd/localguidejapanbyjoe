> "What is the size of train station coin lockers? Will my suitcase fit?"

**Good question!** I actually measured them for you.

---

## Three Main Sizes

> "What sizes are available?"

**Japanese coin lockers come in three sizes:**

**Small:**
- For backpacks and small bags
- Cheapest option

**Medium:**
- For carry-on size luggage
- Most common

**Large:**
- For suitcases
- Even the smallest can fit a suitcase!

---

## Will Your Suitcase Fit?

> "Can I store my luggage?"

**Yes!** Even the smallest large locker can fit a suitcase.

But:
- Very big suitcases may need extra-large
- Popular stations have more options
- Some stations have luggage storage services

---

## Where to Find Them

> "Where are lockers located?"

**At most train stations:**
- Near ticket gates
- Basement floors
- Look for signs
- Ask station staff

Major stations like Tokyo, Shinjuku, and Kyoto have many!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use lockers for day trips
- Know which stations have big lockers
- Use luggage delivery services for large bags
- Plan ahead for popular stations

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Carry coins** – Some only take cash

2. **IC cards work** – Many accept Suica/Pasmo

3. **Book luggage delivery** – For very large bags

4. **Early morning** – Best time to find empty lockers

5. **Take a photo** – Remember your locker number!
