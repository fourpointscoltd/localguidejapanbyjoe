> "Is it easy to find a taxi from Haneda airport to Tokyo Inn Hotel in the evening?"

### Conclusion

**Yes — it’s very easy.**
Haneda Airport is one of Japan’s major international airports, and **taxis are clearly marked and easy to find**, even in the evening 🛬🚕

---
### General Information

Japan has one of the most organized transportation systems in the world — and that includes airports.
**Haneda Airport (HND)** is located close to central Tokyo (only about 30–40 minutes by car), and it’s designed to be **foreigner-friendly**.

You’ll find:

- **Clear signs in English, Chinese, and Korean**
- **Designated taxi stands** outside every terminal
- **Airport staff nearby to help** if needed
- **Safe and reliable taxis** that run 24/7

Whether you land in the early evening or late at night, it’s **not difficult to find a taxi at Haneda**.

---
### How to Find a Taxi at Haneda Airport

- **Follow the signs for “Taxi” after exiting customs**
- Head to the **ground floor (1F)** of your terminal
- You’ll see a **line of taxis waiting at designated stands**
- Just join the line — no need to call ahead or book in most cases

All taxis are metered, clean, and professionally operated.
🚖 *You don’t need to speak Japanese — most drivers will understand the name of your hotel if you show them the address or a map.*

---
### About Tokyo Inn Hotel

There are a few hotels with similar names, so make sure you have the correct one:

For example:

- **Tokyo Inn (in Oimachi)** is about 10–15 minutes by taxi from Haneda Airport
- Fare: Around ¥3,000–¥5,000 depending on traffic (approx. $20–$35 USD)
- Travel time: ~25 minutes in the evening

💡 *Show the driver your hotel confirmation or Google Maps in Japanese to make it easier.*

---
### My Personal Tip

As someone who has traveled through Haneda many times, I can say it’s one of the **easiest airports to navigate**, even for first-time visitors.

The signs are very clear, the taxi area is easy to find, and Japan’s taxi system is extremely safe — even at night.
If you’re tired from a long flight, taking a taxi to your hotel is a stress-free choice 🚕💤

---
### Travel Tips

- **Cash or credit cards?** → Most Tokyo taxis accept credit cards and IC cards like Suica
- **Luggage fees?** → No extra charge for suitcases
- **Tipping?** → No need to tip in Japan
- **Airport staff can assist** → Ask them to write down your hotel name in Japanese if needed
- **Late-night arrival?** → No problem — taxis operate all night at Haneda
