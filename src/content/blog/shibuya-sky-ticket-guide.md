Great question! Shibuya Sky offers different ticket options that can be confusing. Let me break it down.

## Standard Observation Ticket

**Price:** ~¥2,000-2,200

**Includes:**
- Access to Sky Stage (rooftop, 46th floor)
- Access to Sky Gallery (indoor, 45th floor)
- Time slot entry (usually 60-90 minutes total)

## Seat & Drink Ticket

**Price:** ~¥3,500-4,000

**Includes:**
- Everything in standard ticket PLUS
- Reserved seating area
- One drink included
- Extended time to enjoy the view

## Key Differences

| Feature | Standard | Seat & Drink |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| Rooftop access | Yes | Yes |
| Indoor gallery | Yes | Yes |
| Reserved seat | No | Yes |
| Drink included | No | Yes |
| Time | ~60-90 min | ~90+ min |

## Which Should You Choose?

**Standard ticket if:**
- You want to take photos and leave
- Budget is a concern
- Visiting during non-peak hours

**Seat & Drink if:**
- You want to relax and enjoy the view
- Visiting at sunset or night
- Want a more premium experience
- Don't want to stand the whole time

## Pro Tips

1. **Book online** - Cheaper and guarantees entry
2. **Sunset slots** sell out fast
3. **Weekday evenings** are less crowded
4. **SKY EDGE** photo spot has separate line

The seat and drink option is perfect for sunset viewing - it's a great way to watch the city transform from day to night!
