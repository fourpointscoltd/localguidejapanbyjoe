> "A couple of questions:
 - taxi vs private car to take us from Narita to Shibuya?"

**Absolutely, going for a taxi from Narita to Shibuya is a solid choice and avoids any hassle with unlicensed cars.** Plus, taxis are convenient although they can be a bit pricey. But guess what? There's an even cooler option! You can hop on the Narita Express train. It’s super efficient and brings you to Shinjuku, right next to Shibuya. From there, Shibuya is just a short train ride away or even a nice walk if you’re up for it. It’s quick, comfy, and you get to enjoy the scenery along the way!
