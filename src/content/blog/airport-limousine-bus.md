> "How do airport limousine buses work? Do I need to make reservations?"

**Good news!** There's almost nothing you need to do in advance.

---

## No Reservations Needed

> "Can I just show up?"

**Yes!**

- Buses don't require reservations
- Trains don't require reservations
- Taxis don't require reservations
- Buy tickets at the counter
- Almost no waiting time

**It's very easy and convenient!**

---

## Late Night Warning

> "What about late at night?"

**Important:** From about midnight to 5:00 AM, the only option is a taxi.

A taxi from Narita Airport to Tokyo costs about **¥50,000** (~$330 USD). That's very expensive!

**Better options for late arrivals:**
- Stay at an airport hotel
- Wait at the airport until morning
- Book a flight that arrives earlier

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Take the bus or train from airports
- Know no reservation is needed
- Avoid late-night taxi costs
- Use the convenient ticket counters

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Buy tickets at the counter** – Very easy process

2. **Trains also available** – From both Narita and Haneda

3. **Avoid midnight arrivals** – Limited transport options

4. **Airport hotels exist** – Good for very late arrivals

5. **Buses go to many hotels** – Check the route list
