> "I am very interested in Japanese gardens, do you have any recommendations on Japanese gardens?"

**if you love architecture and gardens, Katsura Rikyu in Kyoto will definitely wow you!** It's one of those places that's just mesmerizing with its traditional design and serene landscapes. And guess what? While it's not historical, the garden at the Four Seasons Hotel in Kyoto is also a must-see. It's pretty large and so beautiful. Plus, you can enjoy some fantastic afternoon tea there—it’s a perfect way to relax after strolling around. Both spots are great for soaking in the beauty and tranquility of Japanese gardens.
