#### **Conclusion**

In Japan, all restaurants serving **fugu (pufferfish)** must have a special license, ensuring **safety at every location**. If you’re looking for a **top-rated** fugu restaurant in Tokyo, some of the best options are in **Ginza**, known for its high-quality dining experiences.

#### **General Information About Fugu**

Fugu is a delicacy in Japan, famous for its delicate taste and slightly chewy texture. However, **it contains a deadly toxin** that requires expert preparation. Only certified chefs are legally allowed to prepare and serve fugu, and Japan has **strict disposal regulations for toxic parts** to ensure safety.

Because of these strict rules, **it’s safe to eat fugu at any licensed restaurant** in Japan. That said, some places are especially renowned for their exceptional quality.

#### **Best Fugu Restaurants in Tokyo**

If you’re looking for an **authentic and high-quality** fugu experience, try one of these highly-rated restaurants in **Ginza**:

- **Fugu Fukuji (ふぐ 福治)** 🏆
A well-respected fugu specialty restaurant in Ginza.
- Traditional **multi-course fugu meals (fugu kaiseki)** available.
- Reservations recommended due to high demand.

- **Soukawa (そうかわ)** 🍶
Known for its **delicate fugu sashimi** and hot pot (fugu nabe).
- Offers a great pairing with Japanese sake.
- Excellent service and elegant setting.

- **Naruto (鳴門)** 🍽️
A classic fugu restaurant with **traditional Japanese ambiance**.
- Fresh, high-quality fugu served in multiple styles.
- Located in the heart of Ginza, making it easy to access.

- **Genpin Fugu Ginza Shimbashi (玄品 ふぐ・かに料理 銀座新橋)** 🦀
A more **casual and budget-friendly** option for fugu dining.
- Also specializes in **crab dishes**.
- A great introduction to fugu for first-time eaters.

#### **Best Fugu Dishes to Try**

If it’s your first time eating fugu, try these classic dishes:

- **Fugu Sashimi (Tessa, てっさ)** – Thinly sliced raw fugu, beautifully arranged like a flower.
- **Fugu Hot Pot (Tecchiri, てっちり)** – A warming dish with fugu simmered in broth.
- **Fried Fugu (Fugu Karaage, ふぐ唐揚げ)** – Lightly battered and deep-fried for a crispy texture.
- **Fugu Shirako (白子, soft roe)** – A delicacy available in winter, creamy and rich in flavor.

#### **Best Season to Eat Fugu**

- **Winter (December–February)** ❄️ – **Best time!** Fugu is at its peak and fugu shirako is available.
- **Spring–Summer (March–August)** 🌸☀️ – Still delicious, but less common on seasonal menus.
- **Autumn (September–November)** 🍁 – A good time to enjoy fugu as temperatures drop.

#### **Unexpected Factors & Disadvantages**

- **Fugu is expensive** due to its careful preparation and regulation.
- **Not every restaurant serves fugu year-round**, so check menus in advance.
- **Reservations are recommended** at high-end fugu restaurants, especially in winter.

#### **Extra Travel Tips**

- **Want an authentic experience?** Choose **Fukuji** or **Soukawa** for top-tier dining.
- **Trying fugu for the first time?** **Genpin Fugu** is a great, affordable introduction.
- **Pair with Japanese sake** for the best flavor experience!
