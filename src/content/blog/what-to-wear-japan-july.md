July in Japan is **hot and humid**, especially in major cities like **Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka**. The temperatures often exceed **30°C (86°F)** with high humidity, so dressing comfortably is key.

#### **1. Best Clothing for Japan’s Summer**

- **Light, breathable fabrics** like **cotton, linen, or moisture-wicking materials** (Uniqlo’s [AIRism](https://www.uniqlo.com/feature/airism/au/) is a great choice).
- **Shorts, skirts, and dresses** are perfect for staying cool.
- **Loose-fitting shirts** allow better airflow.
- **Sandals** are fine, but if you’re driving, note that **open-toe footwear isn’t allowed** for safety reasons.

#### **2. Sun Protection is a Must**

Japan’s summer sun is strong, so consider bringing:

- **A hat** to protect yourself from heatstroke.
- **Sunglasses** for UV protection.
- **A portable fan or cooling wipes** to stay refreshed.

#### **3. Fashion & Cultural Considerations**

Japan is quite open when it comes to fashion—you can wear **cosplay, traditional outfits from your country, or stylish streetwear** without issue. However, remember:
✅ Swimsuits are only acceptable at **beaches or pools**, not in public areas.
✅ In Kyoto, some temples prefer modest attire, so keep a **light scarf or shawl** handy.

If you want to **save luggage space**, consider shopping at **Uniqlo or GU** after arriving. These stores offer **affordable, high-quality clothes**, including summer-friendly pieces like [**AIRism**](https://www.uniqlo.com/feature/airism/au/)** shirts and UV-protection outerwear**.
