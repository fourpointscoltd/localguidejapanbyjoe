> "What apps should I download before landing in Japan?"

**Good news!** You don't need many. Here are the essentials.

---

## The Must-Have Apps

> "What do I really need?"

**Just two essential apps:**

1. **Google Maps** – Navigation everywhere
2. **Google Translate** – Communication help

**As long as you have WiFi prepared, these two will solve most problems!**

---

## Prepare WiFi First

> "Why is WiFi so important?"

**In Japan, there is very little free WiFi!**

Make sure you have:
- Pocket WiFi rental
- Travel SIM card
- eSIM activated

Without internet, your apps won't work well.

---

## Optional Useful App

> "Any other recommendations?"

**During the rainy summer season:**
- Rain cloud radar app

With this app, you can:
- See when rain is coming
- Plan your activities to avoid getting wet
- Avoid sudden downpours

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use Google Maps for trains
- Check weather apps regularly
- Know WiFi is limited
- Prepare mobile data

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Download maps offline** – Just in case

2. **Google Translate camera** – Point at menus

3. **No need for many apps** – Keep it simple

4. **Get SIM at airport** – Easy to buy

5. **Test before leaving hotel** – Make sure it works
