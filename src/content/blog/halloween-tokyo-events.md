> "What happens in Tokyo during Halloween? Are there events?"

**Yes!** Halloween is huge in Japan, especially in Tokyo. Here's what to expect.

---

## Shibuya Halloween

> "Is Shibuya really crazy on Halloween?"

**It used to be!** Shibuya was famous for massive street parties. But recently:
- Police now restrict the area
- Drinking on streets is banned
- Less chaotic than before
- Still worth seeing, but different

---

## Best Halloween Spots

> "Where should I go?"

**Theme parks:**
- **Tokyo Disneyland** – Halloween events September-October
- **Universal Studios Japan** – Horror nights and decorations
- **Sanrio Puroland** – Cute Halloween events

**City areas:**
- **Shibuya** – Still the main gathering spot
- **Shinjuku** – Kabukicho has events
- **Ikebukuro** – Anime/cosplay Halloween

---

## When Is Halloween Celebrated?

> "Is it just October 31st?"

Halloween season lasts all of October:
- Theme park events start early September
- Shops sell Halloween goods from September
- Main parties are the weekend before/on October 31st
- Costumes are popular all month

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Dress up and take photos with friends
- Visit theme parks for Halloween events
- Go to Halloween parties at clubs
- Enjoy the decorations at shops

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book theme parks early** – Very crowded during Halloween

2. **Costumes are welcome** – Many people dress up

3. **Trains get interesting** – Costumes on trains are normal

4. **Convenience stores** – Sell Halloween snacks and drinks

5. **Weather** – Late October can be cool, plan accordingly
