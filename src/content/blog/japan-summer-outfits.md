> "Any advice on what to wear in Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka in July? I've read that you shouldn't wear shorts to temples/shrines. With the heat, humidity and rain I'm curious what folks have worn. I'm hoping to travel as light as possible."

**In July, Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka are very hot and humid, so it's best to wear light and breathable clothes.** Cotton shirts, shorts, and dresses are good choices. It's also very sunny, so don't forget to bring a hat and sunglasses to protect yourself from the sun. Sandals are fine to wear, except if you're driving, as they're not allowed for drivers here.

---

## Details

In Japan, you can really wear what you like. Cosplay, traditional clothes from your country, almost anything is welcome. Just remember, swimsuits are only okay at the beach or pool areas.

If you want to pack light, consider buying clothes at Uniqlo in Japan. They have good quality clothes at affordable prices. This can save you some luggage space and you can enjoy local fashion too!
