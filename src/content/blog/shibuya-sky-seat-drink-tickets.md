When I first looked into the "seat and drink" tickets at observation decks here in Japan, I was curious about the same things!

## Short Answer: YES

The "Seat and Drink" ticket at Shibuya Sky **includes full access** to the observation deck. You're essentially getting:
- Standard observation deck access
- PLUS reserved seating
- PLUS one drink included

## What You Get

### Standard Ticket (~¥2,000-2,200)
- 360° rooftop access (Sky Stage)
- Indoor gallery (Sky Gallery)
- Photo spots

### Seat & Drink Ticket (~¥3,500-4,000)
- Everything above, PLUS:
- Reserved lounge seating
- One alcoholic or non-alcoholic drink
- More time to enjoy the view

## Can You Enter Early?

**Not really.** Both tickets follow the designated time slot system. However:
- Seat & Drink guests may have a slightly smoother entry
- You're not rushed as much with the seating option

## When is the Drink Served?

The drink is served once you reach the designated seating area on the observation deck. Since it can take a while to get there, you might wait a bit after entry before you get your drink.

## Can I Buy Day-Of?

**Yes, but...**
- Popular sunset slots often sell out
- Weekends are busier
- Online booking guarantees your preferred time

## My Recommendation

The Seat & Drink option is worth it if you want to:
- Watch the sunset comfortably
- Not rush through the experience
- Enjoy a drink with the view

For a quick photo visit, standard tickets are fine!
