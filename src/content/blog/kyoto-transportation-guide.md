> "What's the best transportation in Kyoto to see famous sites in 2 days?"

**I highly recommend taxis or bicycles!** Here's why.

---

## The Bus Problem

> "Why not take the bus?"

**Kyoto is extremely crowded right now!**

A few months ago, I tried to take a bus and:
- Had to skip THREE buses
- They were all completely full
- Wasted a lot of time

**Buses are not reliable in busy seasons.**

---

## Best Options

> "What should I use instead?"

**For short trips (2-3 days):**
- Charter a taxi for the day
- Comfortable and efficient
- Driver knows the best routes

**For spring or fall:**
- Bicycles are great
- Weather is nice
- See more of the city

---

## The Taxi Advantage

> "Isn't taxi expensive?"

**Consider the benefits:**
- Save time (time is precious on vacation!)
- No waiting in lines
- Go directly to attractions
- Can book in advance

Taxis are also sometimes hard to find due to high demand!

---

## Train Travel

> "What about trains?"

Trains work, but:
- Takes much longer
- Many walking transfers
- Not all places near stations

**Good for getting TO Kyoto, not moving AROUND Kyoto.**

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Know buses are overcrowded
- Use taxis for convenience
- Ride bicycles in good weather
- Plan around peak times

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book taxis early** – They get fully booked

2. **Spring and fall** – Most crowded seasons

3. **Bicycle rentals** – Available near stations

4. **Kyoto is big** – There's enough for a week!

5. **Start early** – Beat the crowds
