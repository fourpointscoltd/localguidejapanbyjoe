> "Is Nikko worth visiting compared to Kyoto?"

**I love Nikko!** I visit there about every two months. Let me explain the differences.

---

## The Main Difference

> "How are they different?"

**Kyoto:**
- City feel
- Universities and modern buildings
- Easy train access
- Many tourists

**Nikko:**
- Nature-rich mountains
- Waterfalls and lakes
- Hot springs
- More peaceful

---

## What Nikko Offers

> "What can I see in Nikko?"

**Natural beauty:**
- Beautiful waterfalls
- Mountain scenery
- Lakes
- Hot springs (onsen)
- Forests

**Famous spots:**
- Toshogu Shrine
- Kegon Falls
- Lake Chuzenji

---

## Getting There

> "How do I get to Nikko?"

**Transportation options:**
- Train from Tokyo (about 2 hours)
- Bus available
- **Having a car is better** – Nikko is quite spacious

**Stay for a few days** to really enjoy it!

---

## My Recommendation

> "Which should I choose?"

**If I were to guide a foreign friend, I would definitely take them to Nikko.**

Both places have shrines and temples, but:
- Kyoto = temples + city
- Nikko = temples + nature

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit Nikko for nature escapes
- Go to Kyoto for traditional culture
- Know both are worth visiting
- Often combine with onsen trip in Nikko

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Rent a car for Nikko** – Much easier to explore

2. **Stay overnight** – See more places

3. **Fall is beautiful** – Amazing autumn colors

4. **Less crowded** – Compared to Kyoto

5. **Hot springs** – Don't miss the onsen experience
