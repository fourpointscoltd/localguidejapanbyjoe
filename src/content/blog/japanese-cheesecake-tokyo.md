> "Where can I find the best Japanese cheesecake in Tokyo?"

**Great question! I love sweet desserts.** Here are my top picks.

---

## Must-Try Cheesecakes

> "What should I try?"

**Two special cheesecakes:**

**1. Mister Cheesecake:**
- Mainly for delivery
- Sometimes available at exhibitions
- Very popular, often sold out

**2. ReTAO (LeTAO):**
- Souvenir from Hokkaido
- Available at airports and train stations
- Frozen, thaw a little before eating
- **I highly recommend this one!**

---

## Cafe Recommendation

> "Where can I eat cheesecake at a cafe?"

**From Afar (near Asakusa):**
- Amazing cheesecake
- Wonderful decor
- Excellent coffee
- Great atmosphere

Perfect for a relaxing break!

---

## Also Try Japanese Shortcake

> "What else is good?"

**If you like cheesecake, try shortcake!**
- Born in Japan
- Perfect appearance
- Amazing taste
- Very different from Western cake

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Know LeTAO is special
- Buy at airports as souvenirs
- Visit trendy cafes
- Love both cheesecake and shortcake

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **LeTAO at airports** – Easy to buy before leaving

2. **Eat it semi-frozen** – Best way to enjoy LeTAO

3. **From Afar cafe** – Worth the trip to Asakusa area

4. **Try shortcake too** – Japanese style is unique

5. **Check exhibitions** – Mister Cheesecake sometimes appears
