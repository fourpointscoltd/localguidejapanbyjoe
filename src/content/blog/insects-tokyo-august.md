> "Are there bugs and mosquitoes in Tokyo in August?"

**Insects exist in Tokyo, but not as many as in Southeast Asia!**

---

## Types of Insects

> "What insects are there?"

**You may encounter:**
- Cockroaches
- Rats
- Mosquitoes
- Flies

**But in clean city areas, the probability is very low!**

---

## Mosquitoes in Urban Areas

> "Will I get bitten by mosquitoes?"

**Mosquitoes are almost unheard of in urban areas!**

I moved to Ginza, and I have not been bitten by a single mosquito since then.

---

## About Cockroaches

> "What about cockroaches?"

**Cockroaches are basically inside rooms,** so tourists may not see them very often.

Hotels keep their rooms clean!

---

## What to Watch Out For

> "Any dangerous insects?"

**The only things to watch out for:**
- Snakes (rare)
- Bees (occasional)

**But almost none can cause serious illness!** Enjoy your trip with peace of mind.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Don't worry about insects in cities
- Know clean areas have fewer bugs
- Keep their homes clean
- Know mosquitoes are rare in urban areas

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Cities are clean** – Few insect problems

2. **Hotels are safe** – Well maintained

3. **Mosquito repellent** – Only if going to countryside

4. **Don't worry** – Enjoy your trip!

5. **Summer heat** – Bigger concern than bugs
