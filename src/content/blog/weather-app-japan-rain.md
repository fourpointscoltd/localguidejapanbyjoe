> "Is there a better weather app for Japan? Something that shows rain clouds?"

Great question! Japan's summer has **sudden rain showers** – a rain radar app is very useful.

---

## The Problem with Japan's Weather

> "Why do I need a special app?"

In Japan's summer, you can experience **sudden squalls**:
- Sunny one moment
- Pouring rain for 10 minutes
- Sunny again

Regular weather apps don't show this well. You need a **rain radar**.

---

## What I Use

> "What app do you recommend?"

**Yahoo Japan's Rain Cloud Radar**

This shows:
- Current rain clouds in real-time
- Where rain is moving
- When it will reach your location
- Predictions for the next few hours

Search for "Yahoo 雨雲レーダー" or "Yahoo rain radar Japan"

---

## How to Use It

> "How does it help?"

**Example situation:**
- You're planning to walk to a temple
- Check the radar – rain cloud approaching in 20 minutes
- Wait at a cafe for 30 minutes
- Rain passes, you walk in sunshine!

This saves you from getting soaked.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Check rain radar before going out in summer
- Always carry a folding umbrella
- Know that summer weather changes quickly
- Don't let rain ruin their plans
