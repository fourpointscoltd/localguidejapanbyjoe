> "Where can I try kaiseki in Tokyo? Is it very expensive?"

Kaiseki is traditional Japanese multi-course dining. It can be expensive, but there are options for different budgets!

---

## What Is Kaiseki?

> "What exactly is kaiseki?"

**Traditional Japanese haute cuisine:**
- Multiple small courses
- Seasonal ingredients
- Beautiful presentation
- Each dish is like art
- Usually takes 2-3 hours

---

## Price Ranges

> "How much does it cost?"

**Budget options:**
- Lunch sets: ¥3,000-8,000 ($20-55)
- Department store restaurants
- Hotel lunch specials

**Mid-range:**
- ¥10,000-20,000 ($70-140)
- Good quality restaurants
- Dinner courses

**High-end:**
- ¥30,000+ ($200+)
- Michelin-starred restaurants
- Reservation required months ahead

---

## Where to Try

> "Any recommendations?"

**For first-timers:**
- **Department store restaurants** – More affordable, still authentic
- **Ginza area** – Many kaiseki restaurants
- **Hotel restaurants** – Good quality, English menus available

**My tip:** Start with a lunch course – same quality, lower price!

---

## Reservation Tips

> "Do I need a reservation?"

**Yes, usually:**
- High-end places need weeks/months advance booking
- Lunch is easier to book
- Some places require Japanese to book
- Ask your hotel concierge for help

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat kaiseki for special occasions
- Choose lunch for better value
- Know it's expensive but worth it once
- Appreciate the seasonal aspects

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Lunch specials** – Same quality, 50% cheaper than dinner

2. **Dress nicely** – Smart casual minimum

3. **Dietary needs** – Mention when booking, hard to change on the day

4. **Take your time** – Don't rush, it's an experience

5. **Photography** – Ask first, some places don't allow it
