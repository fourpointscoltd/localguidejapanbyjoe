> "Hallo! I'm travelling solo this July. Would you know where I can go swimming in Tokyo?
 Not onsen but a resort pool. Thanks!!!"

**Absolutely, if you're in Tokyo and looking for a place to dive in, check out these cool resort-style pools!** At Shinagawa Prince Hotel, you can enjoy a swim with stunning city views, right next to Shinagawa Station. Over at Hotel New Otani, relax in their luxurious garden pool, no reservation needed. Yomiuriland Pool WAI is perfect for a family fun, just 30 minutes from Shinjuku, with incredible night-time events. Or, hit the Sky Pool at Keio Plaza Hotel in Shinjuku—super central and perfect for a quick dip. Don't forget your swimsuit, though, as rentals aren't common. Enjoy the water!
