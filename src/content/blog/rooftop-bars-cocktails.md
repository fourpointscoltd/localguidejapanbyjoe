> "Im looking for a nice rooftop bar - are there any good recommendations? Good cocktails and
 Drinks preferable"

**If you're on the hunt for some cool rooftop bars with great cocktails, you're in for a treat!** In Tokyo, check out Didot in Ueno and The Roof Shibuya Sky in Shibuya for some amazing views and drinks. Right by Tokyo Station, there’s Bulgari Bar which is super chic. And if you find yourself in Kyoto, you have to visit K36—it's got a stellar vibe. Each place offers something unique, so you can't go wrong with any of them for a relaxing evening or a fun night out. Enjoy exploring and sipping those tasty mixes!
