> "Any recommendations for art galleries and museums in Tokyo?"

**Art galleries have different themes depending on the period,** so there's no one-size-fits-all recommendation. But here's my advice!

---

## Two Best Areas

> "Where should I go?"

**For art lovers, visit:**
1. **Roppongi** – Multiple art museums
2. **Ueno** – Museums all in one park

---

## Roppongi Art Scene

> "What's in Roppongi?"

**Famous museums:**
- The National Art Center Tokyo
- Mori Art Museum
- Mori Arts Center Gallery

**Note:** These are all located in different places, a bit far from each other.

---

## Ueno Park (Best for Beginners!)

> "What about Ueno?"

**For beginners, I recommend Ueno Park!**

Why?
- All museums are located in one park
- Easy to visit multiple places
- National Museum
- Science Museum
- Various art exhibitions

**Everything is conveniently together!**

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Check current exhibitions online
- Visit based on what's showing
- Know Ueno is convenient
- Explore Roppongi for special exhibits

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Check exhibitions first** – They change regularly

2. **Ueno for convenience** – Everything in one place

3. **Roppongi for modern art** – More contemporary

4. **Weekdays less crowded** – Better experience

5. **Combine with other sightseeing** – Ueno has zoo too
