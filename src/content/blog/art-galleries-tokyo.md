> "Where are the best art museums in Tokyo?"

Tokyo has an incredible art scene. Here are my top picks by area:

## Roppongi — Art Triangle

Three world-class museums within walking distance:

| Museum | Focus | Price | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mori Art Museum** | Contemporary art, 53rd floor views | ~¥2,000 | Art + city views |
| **National Art Center** | Rotating exhibitions, stunning building | Varies (some free) | Architecture lovers |
| **Suntory Museum of Art** | Traditional Japanese art | ~¥1,500 | Japanese aesthetics |

**Tip:** The "Art Triangle Roppongi" discount ticket covers all three.

## Ueno — Museum Mile

| Museum | Focus | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Tokyo National Museum** | Japan's largest — Japanese art, samurai armor, ceramics | ¥1,000 |
| **National Museum of Western Art** | Monet, Renoir, Rodin (UNESCO building by Le Corbusier) | ¥500 |
| **Ueno Royal Museum** | Special exhibitions | Varies |

**Tip:** Ueno Park has 4+ museums — you could spend a full day here.

## Other Standouts

- **teamLab Borderless** (Azabudai Hills) — immersive digital art. Book tickets in advance
- **Nezu Museum** (Omotesando) — traditional art in a beautiful garden setting. ¥1,300
- **21_21 Design Sight** (Roppongi) — design-focused exhibitions by Tadao Ando. ¥1,200
- **Yayoi Kusama Museum** (Shinjuku) — polka dots and infinity rooms. Requires advance booking, ¥1,100

## Free Art in Tokyo

- **Ginza galleries** — dozens of free commercial galleries on Chuo-dori
- **Tokyo Station Gallery** — inside the historic station building
- **Public art** in Roppongi Hills, Omotesando, and Daikanyama
