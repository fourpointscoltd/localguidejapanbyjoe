> "I heard department store basements have amazing food. What's the deal?"

**It's called depachika!** "Depa" (department) + "chika" (basement). It's food paradise!

---

## What Is Depachika?

> "What can I find there?"

**Depachika has everything:**
- Bento boxes (lunch boxes)
- Sweets and desserts
- Fresh sushi and sashimi
- Bread and pastries
- Wagashi (Japanese sweets)
- Pickles and side dishes
- High-quality groceries

**It's like a gourmet food court** without the seating!

---

## Best Department Stores

> "Which stores should I visit?"

**In Tokyo:**
- **Isetan Shinjuku** – Famous for sweets
- **Takashimaya** – Multiple locations
- **Mitsukoshi** – Traditional and upscale
- **Tokyu Food Show** (Shibuya) – Great bento selection

**Other cities:**
- Hankyu/Hanshin (Osaka)
- Daimaru (various)

---

## What to Buy

> "What do you recommend?"

**Must-try items:**
- **Bento boxes** – Beautiful and delicious
- **Wagashi** – Traditional sweets
- **Seasonal fruits** – Expensive but amazing
- **Onigiri** – High-quality rice balls
- **Desserts** – Cakes, parfaits, and more

---

## Tips for Visiting

> "Any advice?"

**Best times:**
- Evening (6-8 PM) – Discounts on bento!
- Look for 半額 (hangaku) stickers = 50% off
- Less crowded on weekday mornings

**Budget tip:**
- Depachika bento for dinner = great value
- ¥500-1500 for quality meal

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Buy bento for dinner after work
- Get gifts from depachika
- Hunt for discount stickers in evening
- Know which stores have best items

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Free samples** – Often available, politely accept

2. **Tax-free** – Available on purchases over ¥5,000 usually

3. **Gift wrapping** – Free and beautiful

4. **Evening discounts** – Best deals after 6 PM

5. **Eat outside** – Most don't have seating, take to hotel or park
