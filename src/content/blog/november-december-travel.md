> "Is late November to early December a good time to visit Japan?"

**Yes! Winter is considered the best season for tourism!**

---

## Why Winter Is Great

> "What makes winter special?"

**Many benefits:**
- Delicious food season
- Fish and vegetables are most delicious
- All hot pot dishes are amazing
- Great for photography
- Refreshing snow experience (even if you don't ski)

---

## The Season to Avoid

> "When should I NOT come?"

**June is probably the worst time.**

Rainy season (tsuyu):
- Tends to rain a lot
- Humid and uncomfortable
- Though in 2023, it didn't rain at all!

---

## Snow Warnings

> "What about snowfall?"

**Risk of heavy snow in:**
- Kanazawa
- Hokkaido

This can cause:
- Flight delays or cancellations
- Travel disruptions

**But it only happens a few times a year, so don't worry too much.**

---

## Tokyo in Winter

> "Does it snow in Tokyo?"

**Tokyo only gets snow about 1-3 times a year!**

Most days are clear and cold, perfect for sightseeing.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Love winter travel
- Enjoy hot pot dishes
- Visit onsen (hot springs)
- Know winter food is the best

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Winter food is amazing** – Try nabe hot pot

2. **Dress warmly** – But not too heavy

3. **Check Hokkaido flights** – In case of snow

4. **Onsen is perfect** – Hot springs in cold weather

5. **Avoid June** – Rainy season
