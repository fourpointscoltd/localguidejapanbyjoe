> "Where can I see the best night view of Tokyo?"

**Tokyo has amazing night views!** Here are my top spots.

---

## Paid Observation Decks

> "Which paid spots are worth it?"

**Shibuya Sky**
- Price: ¥2,000-2,200
- 360-degree rooftop view
- Very popular at sunset
- Book tickets in advance

**Tokyo Tower**
- Price: ¥1,200-3,000
- Classic Tokyo landmark
- Night illumination beautiful
- Less crowded than Skytree

**Tokyo Skytree**
- Price: ¥2,100-3,100
- Highest observation deck
- Can be crowded
- Good for clear days

---

## Free Options

> "Can I see night views for free?"

**Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building**
- FREE!
- 45th floor, both towers
- Great views of Shinjuku
- Open until 10 PM (usually)
- North tower better for night views

**Caretta Shiodome**
- FREE
- 46th floor
- Nice view of Tokyo Tower
- Less known by tourists

---

## Hidden Gems

> "Any secret spots?"

**Mori Tower (Roppongi Hills)**
- Rooftop helipad for extra fee
- City View deck included in museum ticket

**Hotel lobbies**
- Some have free view floors
- Park Hyatt bar (Lost in Translation!)
- Expensive drinks but free to look

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use the free Tokyo Government Building
- Go to Shibuya Sky for special occasions
- Watch Tokyo Tower illumination from the street
- Prefer less crowded spots

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Sunset timing** – Arrive 30 minutes before for best views

2. **Clear days** – Check weather forecast

3. **Book online** – Shibuya Sky sells out

4. **Winter is best** – Clearest air, Mt. Fuji visible sometimes

5. **Weekdays less crowded** – Avoid weekend evenings
