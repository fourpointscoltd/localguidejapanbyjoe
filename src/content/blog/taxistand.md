**yes!** Taxis are readily available, making it easy to get around Tokyo without needing an app.

## Finding a Taxi at Shinjuku Station

### **Plenty of Taxis at Taxi Stands**

Shinjuku Station is one of the busiest transportation hubs in Tokyo, and because of that, **there are plenty of taxis waiting at the designated taxi stands**. Whether you're traveling late at night or during peak hours, you’ll almost always find an available taxi without the need for a reservation.

### **Are Taxi Apps Necessary?**

While taxi-hailing apps like GO and Uber exist in Japan, they **aren’t as widely used as in other countries**. Most people simply head to a taxi stand or hail a taxi on the street. That said, apps can still be useful during peak times or bad weather when demand is high.

## Useful Taxi Tip: Charging Your Phone

One great feature of Tokyo taxis is that **many of them have USB-A ports for charging**. If you’re running low on battery while exploring the city, you can easily charge your phone during your ride. Just remember to **bring your own charging cable**, as most taxis don’t provide one.

## Conclusion

If you're in Shinjuku and need a taxi, you don't have to worry about pre-booking. Just head to a taxi stand, and you’ll be on your way in no time. With taxis being so accessible and even offering charging ports, getting around Tokyo is incredibly convenient!
