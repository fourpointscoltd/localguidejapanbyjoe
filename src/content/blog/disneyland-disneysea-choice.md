> "Should I visit Tokyo Disneyland or DisneySea? Which is better?"

Having been to both multiple times, my answer is clear: **DisneySea is the better choice for most visitors**, especially adults and first-timers to Japan.

## Quick Comparison

| | Tokyo Disneyland | Tokyo DisneySea |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Families with young kids | Adults, couples, teens |
| **Unique to Japan?** | Similar to US/Paris parks | **100% unique — only one in the world** |
| **Atmosphere** | Classic Disney fairy tale | Mediterranean harbor, adventure |
| **Alcohol** | Not served | Beer, cocktails, wine available |
| **Crowd feel** | More spread out | More compact, easier to navigate |
| **Top rides** | Space Mountain, Big Thunder | Journey to the Center of the Earth, Tower of Terror, Soaring |
| **Food quality** | Standard theme park | **Significantly better** |

## Why I Recommend DisneySea

1. **It exists nowhere else in the world** — Disneyland parks are in the US, Paris, Hong Kong, and Shanghai. But DisneySea is ONLY in Tokyo
2. **The theming is incredible** — it genuinely feels like you're walking through different worlds. The attention to detail is unmatched
3. **Better food and drinks** — actual restaurants, not just fast food. And you can have a beer!
4. **More adult-friendly** — the rides, atmosphere, and experiences appeal to all ages

## When to Choose Disneyland Instead

- **You have kids under 7** — more gentle rides and character meet-and-greets
- **You're a classic Disney fan** — Cinderella Castle, It's a Small World, etc.
- **You've already been to DisneySea** — both parks are worth visiting

## Practical Tips for Both Parks

- **Buy tickets in advance** — [Tokyo Disney Resort website](https://www.tokyodisneyresort.jp/en/) or the app
- **Arrive 30 minutes before opening** — the first hour has the shortest waits
- **Use the Disney Premier Access** — pay ¥1,500-2,500 per ride to skip the line. Worth it for top attractions
- **Weekdays are less crowded** — avoid Japanese holidays and school breaks
- **Bring a portable charger** — you'll use your phone for the app, photos, and wait times all day
