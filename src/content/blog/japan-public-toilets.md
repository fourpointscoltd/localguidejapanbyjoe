> "Is it true that public toilets, while clean, do not have soap for hand washing? Is this factual? If so, what are some of your work -arounds?"

**Public toilets in Japan are generally clean and well-maintained.** Most places do provide soap, but it can vary. In some areas, you might find hand sanitizer instead of soap. It's not a common problem, but it’s good to be prepared.

---

## Details

Many Japanese people eat with their hands, especially when enjoying onigiri (rice balls) or sushi. Because of this, you'll often see people carrying wet wipes or disinfecting wipes in their bags. These come in handy if you find a toilet without soap.

If you are concerned about washing your hands, it's smart to bring your own small pack of wipes or hand sanitizer. This way, you can stay clean no matter where you go.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals are familiar with this topic and handle it naturally in their daily lives.
