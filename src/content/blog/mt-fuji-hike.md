> "Has you here tried hiking Mt. Fuji via Day hike? How was the experience? How many hours would it take if you start at Station 5? Appreciate the response! thank you"

**Hiking Mt.** Fuji from the fifth station can really be an adventure! The routes range from 4.5 km to 10.5 km one way, taking about 6 hours to ascend and 3.5 hours to descend. That’s around 10 to 13 hours round trip. Remember, the altitude change can be tough, so you might feel the effects of altitude sickness. If you’re considering a day hike, start early to avoid descending in the dark—trust me, it's safer that way! The Yoshida Route is a favorite for many, offering a balanced climb with beautiful scenery. Just make sure you’re well-prepared and take it easy out there!
