> "Should I buy a rice cooker in Japan? Are they really better?"

**Yes, they're great!** But there are some things to consider.

---

## Why Japanese Rice Cookers?

> "What makes them special?"

**Benefits:**
- Perfect rice every time
- Many cooking options
- Fuzzy logic technology
- Keep warm function
- Very durable

**Japanese brands are the best** in the world for rice!

---

## The Voltage Problem

> "Will it work at home?"

**Japan uses 100V:**
- USA/Canada: 120V (close, may work)
- Europe/Australia: 220-240V (will NOT work!)

**Solutions:**
- Buy an export/overseas model
- Use a voltage transformer
- Buy at airport duty-free (often dual voltage)

**Check before buying!**

---

## Top Brands

> "Which brands are best?"

**Premium:**
- **Zojirushi** – Top quality
- **Tiger** – Excellent
- **Panasonic** – Great features

**Budget:**
- Cheaper models work well too
- Basic models still better than Western brands

## Where to Buy

> "Best places to shop?"

**For export models:**
- BIC Camera (export floor)
- Yodobashi Camera
- Airport duty-free
- Amazon Japan

**Ask for "kaigai shiyo" (overseas use) models!**
