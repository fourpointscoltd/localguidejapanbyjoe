> "Why does Japan have so many vending machines? What can I get?"

**Japan has over 5 million vending machines!** They're everywhere because they're convenient and safe.

---

## Why So Many?

> "What makes Japan different?"

**Reasons:**
- Low crime rate – machines don't get vandalized
- Land is expensive – machines use less space than stores
- 24/7 convenience – no staff needed
- Japanese love convenience
- Restocking is efficient

---

## What Can You Find?

> "It's not just drinks?"

**Common items:**
- Cold and hot drinks
- Coffee (canned and fresh)
- Green tea and water
- Energy drinks

**Interesting finds:**
- Hot soup (in winter)
- Ice cream
- Snacks and chips
- Instant noodles
- Umbrellas
- Cigarettes (need ID)

---

## How to Use Them

> "Are they hard to use?"

**Very easy:**
1. Insert coins or bills (¥10-¥1000)
2. Press button for your item
3. Take item from bottom
4. Get change from coin slot

**IC cards work too:**
- Tap Suica/Pasmo on the reader
- No coins needed!

---

## Hot vs Cold

> "How do I know if it's hot or cold?"

**Look at the label:**
- 🔵 Blue = Cold
- 🔴 Red = Hot

**Hot drinks are amazing in winter!**

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use vending machines daily
- Know which brands are best
- Use IC cards for convenience
- Love seasonal limited drinks

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Hot drinks in winter** – Try hot milk tea or corn soup

2. **Payment** – Most take coins, many take IC cards

3. **Rare machines** – Look for fun ones like hamburgers or ramen

4. **Pricing** – Usually ¥100-170 for drinks

5. **Regional drinks** – Different areas have local specialties
