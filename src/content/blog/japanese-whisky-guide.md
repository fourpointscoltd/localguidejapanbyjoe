> "I want to try Japanese whisky. Where do I start?"

**Japanese whisky is world-famous!** But be prepared – premium bottles are expensive and rare.

---

## Famous Brands

> "What should I know?"

**Major distilleries:**
- **Suntory** – Yamazaki, Hakushu, Hibiki
- **Nikka** – Yoichi, Miyagikyo, Taketsuru

**Premium bottles** are hard to find due to global demand!

---

## How to Drink

> "What's the Japanese way?"

**Popular styles:**
- **Highball** – Whisky + soda, very popular!
- **On the rocks** – With ice
- **Straight** – For tasting
- **Mizuwari** – With water

**Highball is huge** in Japan – try it at izakaya!

---

## Where to Try

> "Where can I taste it?"

**Options:**
- Bars with whisky selection
- Izakaya (highballs)
- Whisky bars in major cities
- Distillery tours (book ahead!)
- Hotel bars

**Convenience stores** – Have basic brands at good prices.

---

## Buying Whisky

> "Where to buy bottles?"

**Shops:**
- Department store liquor sections
- Specialty liquor stores
- Airport duty-free
- Don Quijote (limited selection)

**Warning:** Rare bottles are very expensive or unavailable!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Drink highballs at izakaya
- Know premium whisky is pricey
- Enjoy whisky year-round
- Understand the global demand

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Highball first** – Great introduction to Japanese whisky

2. **Don't expect rare bottles** – They're really hard to find

3. **Airport duty-free** – Sometimes has good selection

4. **Distillery tours** – Book months ahead

5. **Budget options** – Suntory Toki, Black Nikka are accessible
