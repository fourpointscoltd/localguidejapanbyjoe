> "Can you suggest one thing to do, see, eat, or stay in each major destination?"

Here's my quick-hit guide for Japan's top destinations — one standout recommendation for each:

## Tokyo
- **Do:** Walk from Shibuya → Harajuku → Omotesando on a Sunday morning
- **See:** Meiji Shrine at sunrise (arrive before 7am)
- **Eat:** Standing sushi at Uogashi Nihon-Ichi (Shibuya) — amazing quality for ¥2,000
- **Stay:** Asakusa area — affordable, great food, walking distance to Sensoji

## Kyoto
- **Do:** Rent a bicycle and ride the Philosopher's Path
- **See:** Fushimi Inari — go at 6am to have the gates to yourself
- **Eat:** Nishiki Market — walk through and eat everything
- **Stay:** Near Kyoto Station for convenience, or Gion for atmosphere

## Osaka
- **Do:** Street food walk through Dotonbori at night
- **Eat:** Takoyaki from a street stall (not a restaurant — the street ones are better)
- **See:** Osaka Castle at sunset
- **Stay:** Namba area — central to everything

## Hokkaido
- **Do:** Drive the scenic roads in summer or ski in winter
- **Eat:** Kaisendon (seafood rice bowl) at Nijo Market in Sapporo — ~¥2,000
- **See:** Otaru Canal — romantic port town 30 min from Sapporo
- **Stay:** Sapporo as your base

## Hiroshima
- **Do:** Take the ferry to Miyajima Island
- **Eat:** Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki (layered, not mixed)
- **See:** Itsukushima Shrine's floating torii gate
- **Stay:** Near Hiroshima Station for easy ferry access
