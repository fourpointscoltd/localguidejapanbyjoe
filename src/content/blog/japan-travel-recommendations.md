Planning a trip around Japan? Here are my personal recommendations for one standout experience in each major destination!

## Tokyo

**DO:** Walk through Yanaka - Old Tokyo charm
**SEE:** TeamLab Planets (immersive digital art)
**EAT:** Tsukemen at Fuunji in Shinjuku
**STAY:** Shibuya area for first-timers

## Kyoto

**DO:** Early morning at Fushimi Inari (before 8 AM)
**SEE:** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
**EAT:** Kaiseki ryori (traditional multi-course meal)
**STAY:** Gion area for atmosphere

## Osaka

**DO:** Bar hopping in Shinsekai
**SEE:** Osaka Castle at sunset
**EAT:** Takoyaki in Dotonbori
**STAY:** Namba for nightlife, Umeda for business

## Hiroshima

**DO:** Day trip to Miyajima Island
**SEE:** Peace Memorial Museum
**EAT:** Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki
**STAY:** Near Hiroshima Station for convenience

## Kyushu (Fukuoka/Nagasaki/Beppu)

**DO:** Sand bath in Ibusuki
**SEE:** Aso volcano crater
**EAT:** Hakata ramen (Fukuoka)
**STAY:** Beppu for onsen experience

## Hokkaido

**DO:** Blue Pond in Biei
**SEE:** Otaru canal at night
**EAT:** Fresh seafood don at Morning Market
**STAY:** Niseko for skiing, Sapporo for city life

## Quick Decision Guide

| Priority | Go To |
|----------|-------|
| Culture | Kyoto |
| Food | Osaka |
| Modern Japan | Tokyo |
| Nature | Hokkaido |
| History | Hiroshima |

These are the kinds of experiences locals treasure—and that's what makes visiting Japan so special!
