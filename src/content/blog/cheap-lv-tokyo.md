> "Hello! Where to get affordable second hand
 LV bags here in Tokyo?"

**In Tokyo, finding cheap Louis Vuitton bags is a bit tricky because new items are quite pricey, and even used ones often still cost a lot.** However, a good way to look for more affordable LV bags is to check out second-hand stores. These shops are famous for keeping items in good condition. Some popular second-hand store chains in Tokyo where you often find luxury brands include Komehyo, Brand Off, and Daikokuya. You can easily find these stores on Google Maps just by searching their names near your location in Tokyo. They usually have a variety of bags, but prices and availability can vary, so it might be a good idea to visit a few different shops to compare what's on offer. This way, you can find a good deal.
