> "Should I go to Tokyo or Kyoto first? Which is better for first-time visitors?"

**Both are amazing!** But they offer very different experiences. Here's my advice.

---

## Tokyo vs Kyoto

> "What's the difference?"

**Tokyo:**
- Modern, huge, exciting
- Shopping, technology, anime
- Fast-paced city life
- Many different neighborhoods
- International airport hub

**Kyoto:**
- Traditional, historic, peaceful
- Temples, shrines, geisha
- Slower pace
- Compact city center
- Traditional culture

---

## My Recommendation

> "Which one first?"

**Start in Tokyo if:**
- You fly into Narita/Haneda
- You want modern Japan first
- Shopping is important to you
- You like nightlife and entertainment

**Start in Kyoto if:**
- You fly into Kansai (Osaka)
- Traditional Japan is your priority
- You prefer slower pace
- Temples and history excite you

---

## How to Do Both

> "Can I see both?"

**Absolutely! Most people do:**
- Shinkansen (bullet train) connects them in 2 hours 15 minutes
- JR Pass covers this route
- Easy to visit both in one trip
- 3-4 days each city is ideal

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Live in one, visit the other
- See Tokyo as work, Kyoto as vacation
- Prefer Kyoto for traditional experiences
- Visit Tokyo for shopping and entertainment

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Fly into one, out of the other** – Saves backtracking

2. **Tokyo first is common** – Adjust to jet lag in the big city

3. **Kyoto needs reservations** – Popular temples book up

4. **Add Osaka** – Close to Kyoto, different vibe

5. **Don't rush** – Both deserve at least 3 full days
