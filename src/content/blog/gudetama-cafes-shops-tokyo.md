If you're hunting for Gudetama goodies in Tokyo, your best bet is to start at **Sanrio World in Ginza**. It's a store that carries various Sanrio products, including Gudetama merchandise.

## Where to Find Gudetama

### Sanrio Stores
- **Sanrio World Ginza** - Large selection of all Sanrio characters
- **Sanrio Vivitix** locations in various shopping malls

### Sanrio Puroland
For the ultimate Gudetama experience, visit **Sanrio Puroland** in Tama City. It's about 40 minutes from Shinjuku, and you'll find:
- Gudetama merchandise
- Themed attractions
- Character meet-and-greets
- Exclusive limited items

## Shopping Tips

1. **Character Street at Tokyo Station** - Has a Sanrio shop with rotating seasonal items
2. **Kiddy Land in Harajuku** - Multiple floors including Sanrio goods
3. **Don Quijote** - Discount stores often have Sanrio merchandise at lower prices

## Cafe Experiences

While dedicated Gudetama cafes come and go as pop-ups, **Sanrio Puroland** has permanent dining options with character-themed food. Check their website for current offerings!

The lazy egg has quite a following in Japan, and you'll likely spot Gudetama merchandise in convenience stores and department stores throughout Tokyo too.
