**Wow, good question about the restaurant hours in Yanaka Ginza!** This charming neighborhood is quite unique, and luckily, most shops and eateries here don't stick to the typical Tokyo schedule of closing on Sundays. So, you're in luck! You'll find that many delightful places are open, ready to welcome you even on a lazy Sunday. Isn't it great? Just stroll down the scenic streets, and you'll find tons of options to enjoy some local flavors. Yanaka Ginza really is like stepping into a storybook scene!
