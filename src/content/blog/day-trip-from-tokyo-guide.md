> "What day trips can I do from Tokyo?"

**So many great options!** Here are my top recommendations.

---

## Within 1 Hour

> "What's close to Tokyo?"

**Kamakura**
- Great Buddha statue
- Many temples and shrines
- Beach access
- About 1 hour from Tokyo

**Yokohama**
- Chinatown
- Ramen museum
- Waterfront area
- 30 minutes from Tokyo

**Kawagoe**
- "Little Edo" old town
- Traditional streets
- Sweet potato treats
- 45 minutes from Ikebukuro

---

## 1-2 Hours Away

> "What about slightly further?"

**Nikko**
- UNESCO World Heritage shrines
- Beautiful nature
- Waterfalls
- 2 hours from Tokyo

**Hakone**
- Hot springs
- Mt. Fuji views
- Open-air museum
- 1.5 hours from Tokyo

**Enoshima**
- Small island
- Shrine and caves
- Beach nearby
- 1 hour from Tokyo

---

## Mt. Fuji Day Trip

> "Can I see Mt. Fuji in a day?"

**Yes!**
- Kawaguchiko lake area
- Best views on clear days
- 2 hours by bus
- Check weather before going!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit these places on weekends
- Use train passes for savings
- Go early to avoid crowds
- Check weather forecasts

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Start early** – Leave by 8-9 AM for full day

2. **JR Pass** – Covers Kamakura, Nikko, and more

3. **Weekdays better** – Less crowded than weekends

4. **Return tickets** – Buy round-trip for convenience

5. **Check seasonal events** – Festivals, flowers change throughout year
