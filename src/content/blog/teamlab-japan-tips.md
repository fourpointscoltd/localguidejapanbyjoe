> "I have heard mixed reviews on I about teamlab, is it worth it? i've heard its too crowded"

**Absolutely, TeamLab is totally worth a visit!** It’s like stepping into another world with all the digital art and interactive installations. But I get it, crowds can be a bit of a downer. Here’s a tip: try to go first thing in the morning. It's usually quieter then, and you can really enjoy the magic without too much hustle and bustle around you. Plus, experiencing the artwork with fewer people adds so much to the charm. So, definitely go for it, and make it an early trip!
