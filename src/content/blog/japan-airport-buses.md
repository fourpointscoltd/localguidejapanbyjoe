> "How do airport limousines bus work? Do I need make reservations ahead of time or can I purchase a ticket at the airport?"

**Guess what?** You don't need to book the airport limousine buses in Japan in advance. Just head to the ticket counter when you land, grab your ticket, and you’re good to go. It's super easy and usually, there’s barely any wait time. Keep in mind though, after 10:00 p.m. the buses stop running until around 5:00 a.m., so if you land late, your best bet might be a taxi, but it can be pricey—about 50,000 yen from Narita to Tokyo. Alternatively, you could book a nearby hotel or hang out at the airport until the buses start running again.
