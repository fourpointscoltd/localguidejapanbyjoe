> "Is it safe to eat fugu in Tokyo? Can you recommend a good restaurant?"

**Yes, fugu is very safe in Japan!** Here's what you need to know.

---

## Why Fugu Is Safe

> "Isn't fugu poisonous?"

**Yes, but restaurants are strictly regulated:**
- Chefs need a special license
- Strict rules for removing poison
- Government inspections
- Only licensed restaurants can serve it

**In Japan, you can eat fugu at any restaurant without worry!**

---

## Recommended Restaurants in Ginza

> "Where should I go?"

**Famous fugu restaurants:**
- **Fugu Fukuji** – High quality, great service
- **Naruto** – Popular among locals
- **Genhin Fugu & Crab Cuisine Ginza Shinbashi** – Also serves crab

All are in the Ginza area!

---

## How to Eat Fugu

> "What dishes are popular?"

**Common fugu dishes:**
- **Fugu sashimi** – Thin slices, beautiful presentation
- **Fugu hot pot** – Warm and delicious
- **Fried fugu** – Crispy and tasty
- **Fugu fin sake** – Grilled fin in hot sake

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat fugu in winter season
- Trust licensed restaurants completely
- Consider it a special occasion food
- Know the best local spots

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Winter is best** – Fugu season is November to March

2. **Make reservations** – Popular restaurants fill up

3. **Not cheap** – Expect to pay more than regular meals

4. **Worth trying** – Unique texture and taste

5. **Don't worry** – Safety record is excellent
