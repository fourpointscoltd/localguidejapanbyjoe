Honestly? I don't think it's worth it for most travelers.

## What is the Green Car?

The Green Car is JR's "first class" seating on Shinkansen and some local trains. It offers:
- **Wider seats** (about 10% more space)
- **More legroom**
- **Quieter cars** (sometimes)
- **Footrests** on Shinkansen
- **Power outlets** at every seat

## Why I Say Skip It

I've ridden the Shinkansen's Green Car before, and while the seats are a bit wider and comfier, **that's about it**. Here's my honest take:

1. **Regular seats are already comfortable** - Japan's standard seats are better than most countries' first class
2. **Trains aren't usually packed** - Outside rush hour, you'll have plenty of space
3. **Short journey times** - Tokyo to Kyoto is only 2 hours
4. **Better use of money** - Save ¥13,000+ for food or experiences

## When Green Car MIGHT Be Worth It

- **Very long journeys** (5+ hours)
- **Traveling during peak holiday periods**
- **Physical needs** (more space for tall people or mobility issues)
- **Business travel** (need to work on laptop)

## The Numbers

| Route | Regular | Green | Difference |
|-------|---------|-------|------------|
| Tokyo-Kyoto | ¥13,320 | ¥18,720 | +¥5,400 |
| Tokyo-Osaka | ¥13,870 | ¥19,270 | +¥5,400 |

That extra ¥5,400 ($35+) per trip adds up quickly!

## My Verdict

Save that money for some amazing sushi or a few extra souvenirs! The regular Shinkansen experience is already world-class.
