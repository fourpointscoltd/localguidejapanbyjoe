> "Is it worthy to go to Okinawa for a day trip from Tokyo in July?
 Is it like a beach area?"

**Okinawa does indeed have some of the most beautiful beaches in Japan, and it's a fantastic beach destination.** However, considering it's quite far from Tokyo—a flight takes about 2.5 to 3 hours one way—a day trip might be really tiring. You might spend more time traveling than enjoying the beach! Also, Okinawa is not just about the beaches; it has a rich culture and history, delicious unique cuisine, and lovely locals. So, it's better to plan to stay there for at least a couple of nights if you can. This way, you can truly experience what Okinawa has to offer without rushing through your visit. Also, consider visiting in July when the weather is perfect for the beach!
