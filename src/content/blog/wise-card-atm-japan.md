> "Are there specific ATMs in Japan that accept Wise cards for withdrawals?"

Great question! Based on many travelers' experiences, **Seven Bank ATMs at 7-Eleven stores have the highest success rate** for foreign cards including Wise.

---

## Best ATMs for Foreign Cards

> "Which ATMs should I look for?"

**Most reliable (in order):**

1. **Seven Bank (7-Eleven)** – Best success rate
2. **Japan Post Bank** – At post offices
3. **AEON Bank** – In AEON shopping malls
4. **E-net** – At FamilyMart convenience stores

Seven-Eleven is your safest bet. There are many stores even in smaller cities.

---

## Why Seven Bank Works Best

> "What makes 7-Eleven ATMs special?"

Seven Bank ATMs are designed for:
- International cards
- Multiple languages (English available)
- 24-hour access (the store is always open)
- Easy-to-follow screens

They specifically support foreign Mastercard and Visa cards.

---

## Tips for Smooth Withdrawals

> "Any advice before I go?"

**Before your trip:**
- Make sure your card is activated for international use
- Check your daily withdrawal limit
- Let your bank know you're traveling to Japan

**When withdrawing:**
- Select "English" on the screen
- Choose "Withdrawal"
- Select "Savings" (even if it's a checking account)
- Try smaller amounts first if unsure

---

## What About Rural Areas?

> "I'm going to the countryside. Will ATMs work there?"

This is important! **Withdraw cash in cities before going rural.**

In countryside areas:
- Fewer 7-Eleven stores
- Some local ATMs don't accept foreign cards
- Post offices usually work but have shorter hours

Have enough cash before leaving major cities!

---

## How Much Cash to Carry

> "How much should I withdraw?"

Japan still uses a lot of cash. I recommend:
- **Daily budget:** Keep ¥10,000-20,000 for daily expenses
- **Backup:** Have extra in case ATMs don't work
- **Big cities:** Credit cards work at many places
- **Countryside:** Cash is king

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Carry cash for daily purchases
- Use IC cards (Suica) for trains and convenience stores
- Use credit cards at bigger stores
- Have a bank account with local banks (of course!)

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Check your card fees** – Wise usually has low fees, but check

2. **Withdraw larger amounts less often** – Saves on fees

3. **7-Eleven is everywhere** – Even at train stations

4. **Airport ATMs work** – Get cash when you land

5. **Some ATMs close at night** – But 7-Eleven is 24/7

6. **Keep some yen for emergencies** – Technology sometimes fails!
