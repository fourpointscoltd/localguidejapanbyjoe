> "Is it acceptable for women to wear shorts in Japan during the summer?"

**Absolutely yes.** Japanese women wear shorts in summer all the time. You'll see it everywhere — on the train, in shopping malls, at restaurants.

## The Reality

Japan in summer is **brutal** — 35°C+ with extreme humidity (July-September). Wearing shorts isn't just acceptable, it's practical. Nobody will give you a second look.

## What Japanese Women Actually Wear in Summer

- **Shorts** — denim, linen, or cotton. All common
- **Skirts** — mini to midi length
- **Lightweight dresses** — very popular
- **Tank tops** — completely normal in casual areas
- **Sandals** — everywhere you go

## When to Be More Covered

There are a few situations where you might want longer clothing:

| Situation | What to Wear | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Temples & shrines** | Knees covered preferred | Sign of respect (not always enforced) |
| **Fancy restaurants** | Smart casual | Dress code may apply |
| **Business meetings** | Professional attire | Cultural expectation |
| **Strong A/C indoors** | Bring a light cardigan | Trains and malls are freezing! |

## My Honest Tips

- **Bring a thin cardigan or scarf** — not for modesty, but because air conditioning in Japan is incredibly cold. The contrast between 35°C outside and 20°C inside is shocking
- **Sunscreen is essential** — Japanese women are serious about sun protection. Pick some up at any drugstore (Japanese sunscreen is world-famous for quality)
- **UV arm sleeves** — you'll see many Japanese women wearing these. Available at Uniqlo and 100 yen shops
- **Comfortable walking shoes** — you'll walk a lot. Sandals with good support are better than flip-flops
