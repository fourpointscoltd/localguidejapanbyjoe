> "Hi All, may i ask can 8yo kids enter Shinjuku Tokyu Kabukicho Tower?"

**Absolutely, 8-year-olds can enter Shinjuku Tokyu Kabukicho Tower without any issues!** Just remember, though, Kabukicho is known for its nightlife and there's a curfew for youngsters. Most places in Japan have regulations that say minors should be accompanied by an adult, especially after 10 or 11 pm. So, if your plans include being out late, just keep that timing in mind.
