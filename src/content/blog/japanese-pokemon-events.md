> "Tips for Pokemon-related experiences that aren't the cafe or centers..? As well as any tips for specific Pokémon card stores to visit."

**For a cool Pokemon experience outside cafes, check out the Pikachu Outbreak in Yokohama.** It's a big event where lots of Pikachus show up – super fun to watch! Another neat spot is Pokemon Wonder at Yomiuri Land, where you can explore outdoor areas with hidden Pokemon. Also, Universal Studios Japan (USJ) has a Pokemon Halloween event if you're around in the fall.

---

## Details

Those events change yearly, so always check what's new. Right now, I'm excited about the Pokemon Craftworks Exhibition.

If you're into Pokemon cards, Tokyo has some great shops to explore. Try Nakano Broadway or Akihabara - both have lots of stores with huge selections of Pokemon cards. Remember, some rare finds might be pricey but are super cool!
