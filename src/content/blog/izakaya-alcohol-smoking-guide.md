Great question! From my experience living in Japan and visiting izakayas all over the country, the short honest answer is: **yes, basically**.

## What is an Izakaya?

The word "izakaya" literally means a place to drink alcohol with food, so drinking is kind of the main event there. However, times are changing!

## Alcohol Expectations

- **Drinking is the norm** - Most people at izakayas are drinking
- **Non-alcoholic options exist** - Soft drinks, tea, and non-alcoholic beer are always available
- **No pressure** - Staff won't force you to drink; ordering food-only is fine

## Smoking Situation

As of recent years, Japan has tightened smoking regulations:
- **Many izakayas are now non-smoking** or have separated smoking areas
- **Smaller, traditional izakayas** may still allow smoking
- **Chain izakayas** tend to be non-smoking or have proper ventilation

## Tips for Non-Drinkers

1. Order "oolong-hai" without alcohol (just oolong tea)
2. Try "Dry Zero" or other non-alcoholic beers
3. Focus on the amazing food - that's half the experience!

## What to Expect

Even if you don't drink or smoke, izakayas offer incredible food experiences. Just be aware that:
- The atmosphere can get lively (loud!)
- You'll be sharing space with people who are drinking
- Late-night izakayas tend to be smokier in areas that allow it

Don't let concerns about alcohol or smoking stop you from experiencing izakaya culture - just choose your venue wisely!
