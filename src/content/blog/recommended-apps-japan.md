> "What apps should I download before visiting Japan?"

Here are the only apps you **actually need**. No bloated list — just the ones I personally use living here:

## Must-Have (Install These Before Your Flight)

### Google Maps
- **Why:** Navigation, train routes, walking directions, restaurant reviews
- **Tip:** Download offline maps for Tokyo/Kyoto before your trip (saves data)
- Works for **all transportation** — trains, buses, walking, driving

### Google Translate
- **Why:** Camera translation for menus and signs, conversation mode for speaking
- **Tip:** Download the **Japanese offline pack** before traveling. Camera translation works without internet!

### Suica (Apple Wallet) or Mobile PASMO
- **Why:** Tap to ride trains and pay at stores — no physical card needed
- **iPhone users:** Add Suica directly in Wallet app
- **Android users:** Get Mobile PASMO from Google Play

## Very Useful

### Navitime / Japan Travel by Navitime
- **Why:** More detailed than Google Maps for Japanese train schedules, platform numbers, and transfer info
- **Tip:** Shows which car to board for easiest transfers

### PayPay
- **Why:** Japan's most popular mobile payment. Some small restaurants and shops are **PayPay only** (no cash, no credit card)
- **Tip:** You can link a foreign credit card. More and more places accept it

## Nice to Have

| App | Purpose | When You Need It |
|---|---|---|
| **Tabelog** | Restaurant reviews (Japan's Yelp) | Finding local restaurants |
| **Klook/KKday** | Discount tickets for attractions | Theme parks, experiences |
| **GO (taxi app)** | Request taxis | Late night or rural areas |
| **Safety Tips** | Earthquake/disaster warnings | Emergency alerts in English |
| **Japan Official Travel App** | Tourist info | General trip planning |

## Apps You DON'T Need

- **Uber** — limited in Japan. Use GO instead
- **WhatsApp** — Japanese people use LINE
- **VPN** — Japan doesn't block any major websites
- **Currency converter** — just Google "100 USD to JPY"

## Pro Tip

Set up everything **before you land**. Airport Wi-Fi can be slow, and you don't want to be downloading apps while navigating a new country.
