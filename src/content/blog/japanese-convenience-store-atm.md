> "Which convenience store ATMs work with foreign cards?"

**7-Eleven is your best friend!** Their ATMs reliably work with international cards.

---

## Best ATMs for Tourists

> "Which ones should I use?"

**Recommended:**
- **7-Eleven** – Most reliable, 24/7
- **Japan Post Office** – Also works well
- **Lawson** – Many work now
- **Family Mart** – Hit or miss

**7-Eleven ATMs are the gold standard** for foreign cards!

---

## How to Use

> "What's the process?"

**Steps:**
1. Insert card
2. Select English language
3. Choose "Withdrawal"
4. Enter amount
5. Confirm
6. Take cash, card, and receipt

**Easy!** The machines have English options.

---

## Fees to Expect

> "How much are the fees?"

**Possible fees:**
- ATM fee: Usually ¥110-220
- Your bank's international fee
- Exchange rate margin

**Check with your bank** before traveling!

---

## Withdrawal Limits

> "How much can I take out?"

**Typical limits:**
- Per transaction: ¥30,000-100,000
- Daily limit: Set by your bank
- Some cards have lower limits

**Tip:** Withdraw larger amounts to reduce fees.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Use their bank ATMs
- Know convenience store ATMs charge fees
- Rarely carry large cash amounts
- Increasingly use cashless payment

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **7-Eleven first** – Always your safest bet

2. **Notify your bank** – Tell them you're traveling

3. **Card issues** – Try another ATM before panicking

4. **Rural areas** – May have fewer ATM options

5. **Backup card** – Bring more than one card
