> "Hi All, I'm in Kyoto and would like to buy women and kids reasonable price clothes, where can I buy?
 Also, I would like to buy kindergarten school bag and lunch boxes for my son, where can I find these things.
 I have already checked and bought some stuff from Uniqlo.
 Thanks I'm advance!"

**When looking for affordable women's and kids' clothes in Kyoto, you should check out Uniqlo.** It's a popular store with good prices and nice styles for both adults and children. Another good store is GU, which is like a cheaper version of Uniqlo. If you want a style a bit unique, you might also like to visit a store called Shimamura, which is also budget-friendly.

---

## Details

If you're interested in more Japanese-style clothing, you should look at Muji. It has simple and functional clothes.

Lastly, for buying bento boxes and other Japanese goods, Loft and Tokyu Hands are great stores. They have a wide range of products that are perfect for gifts. You can also find many specialty bento box shops around tourist areas in Kyoto.
