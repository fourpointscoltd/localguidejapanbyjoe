> "Is it worth visiting Kanazawa? Some people say to skip it."

I **really love Kanazawa**. If you like sushi, art, or Japanese sake, you should definitely go!

---

## Why Kanazawa Is Special

> "What makes it different from other cities?"

**Best sushi in Japan** – I think Kanazawa has even better sushi than Tokyo. The fish comes straight from the Sea of Japan, and it's incredibly fresh.

**Beautiful gardens** – Kenrokuen is one of Japan's top 3 gardens.

**Ninja Temple (Myouryuji)** – This is a must-see! The whole temple is full of traps, hidden rooms, and surprises. You need a reservation, but it's worth it.

**Art museums** – The 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art is amazing.

---

## Who Should Visit Kanazawa

> "Is it right for me?"

**Go to Kanazawa if you:**
- Love sushi and seafood
- Enjoy traditional Japanese culture
- Want to see beautiful gardens
- Like sake (Kanazawa has great local sake)
- Want fewer tourists than Kyoto

**Maybe skip if you:**
- Only have 1 week total in Japan
- Prefer big city excitement
- Don't like seafood

---

## How to Get There

> "Is it easy to reach?"

From Tokyo: **2.5 hours by Shinkansen** (Hokuriku Shinkansen)

Very easy! You can do it as a day trip, but I recommend staying 1-2 nights.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit Kanazawa for weekend trips
- Go specifically for sushi at Omicho Market
- Combine with Shirakawa-go trip
- Consider it a more relaxed alternative to Kyoto

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book Ninja Temple in advance** – Reservations required

2. **Visit Omicho Market** – Fresh seafood and sushi for breakfast

3. **Try the local sake** – Kanazawa is famous for it

4. **Rent a bike** – The city is flat and easy to cycle

5. **Combine with Shirakawa-go** – About 1.5 hours by bus
