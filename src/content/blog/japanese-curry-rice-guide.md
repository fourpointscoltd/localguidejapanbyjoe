> "What makes Japanese curry different?"

**Japanese curry is unique!** It's sweeter, milder, and a comfort food favorite.

---

## How It's Different

> "How is it different from other curries?"

**Japanese curry vs Indian curry:**
- Sweeter and milder
- Thicker sauce
- Served with rice
- Uses curry roux (blocks)
- British-influenced originally

**Flavor profile:**
- Slightly sweet
- Not very spicy (usually)
- Rich and thick
- Comfort food taste

---

## Popular Types

> "What kinds exist?"

**Common variations:**
- **Katsu curry** – With fried pork cutlet
- **Beef curry** – Most traditional
- **Chicken curry** – Lighter option
- **Vegetable curry** – With carrots, potatoes
- **Seafood curry** – Shrimp, squid

**Spice levels:**
- Mild (甘口 amakuchi)
- Medium (中辛 chukara)
- Hot (辛口 karakuchi)

## Where to Eat

> "Where can I try it?"

**Chain restaurants:**
- **CoCo Ichibanya** – Customize everything
- **Go! Go! Curry** – Kanazawa style
- **Matsuya** – Budget option

**Other places:**
- Family restaurants (Denny's, Gusto)
- Convenience stores (surprisingly good!)
- Specialty curry shops

## CoCo Ichibanya Tips

> "How does CoCo Ichibanya work?"

**Customization:**
1. Choose rice amount (200g-400g)
2. Choose spice level (1-10)
3. Choose sauce amount
4. Add toppings
5. Pay and enjoy!

Great for beginners – visual menu available.

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Consider curry comfort food
- Make it at home often
- Have a favorite curry shop
- Eat it weekly for some

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Start mild** – Japanese mild is pretty mild

2. **Katsu curry** – Most popular tourist choice

3. **Convenience store curry** – Great late-night option

4. **Fukujinzuke** – Red pickles served on the side, try them!

5. **Curry bread** – Fried bread with curry inside, find at bakeries
