> "I left my bag on the train! Can I get it back?"

**Good news!** Japan has an amazing lost and found system. You have a good chance of getting it back!

---

## What to Do First

> "What are the steps?"

**Immediately:**
1. Note the train line, direction, and time
2. Remember what car you were in
3. Go to the nearest station staff

**At the station:**
- Tell them what you lost
- Give details about the train
- Fill out a form if needed
- They'll contact other stations

---

## Where Items Go

> "Where does my stuff end up?"

**Timeline:**
- Same day: Usually at the terminal station of that line
- Next day: Moved to central lost and found
- After a few days: Police lost and found

**JR lines:** Each company has their own lost and found center.

---

## How to Search

> "Can I search online?"

**Yes!**
- JR East: [lost and found website]
- Tokyo Metro: [lost property center]
- Call the train company's lost and found

**Bring:**
- ID (passport for tourists)
- Description of item
- Proof of ownership if possible

---

## Success Rate

> "Will I actually get it back?"

**Japan is amazing for this!**
- Very high recovery rate
- People turn in items
- Staff are thorough
- Even cash often returned!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Report lost items immediately
- Know the system works well
- Turn in items they find
- Trust the process

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Act fast** – Report within hours for best chance

2. **Take photos** – Of your belongings for identification

3. **Station staff help** – They're very helpful

4. **Police station** – After a few days, check there too

5. **Apple AirTag** – Helpful for tracking bags
