> "When exactly do cherry blossoms bloom? How do I plan my trip?"

**It depends on the year and location!** Here's how to plan for sakura season.

---

## Typical Timing

> "When is cherry blossom season?"

**General timing:**
- **Okinawa:** January-February (earliest)
- **Tokyo/Osaka:** Late March - Early April
- **Kyoto:** Late March - Early April
- **Tohoku:** Mid to Late April
- **Hokkaido:** Late April - May (latest)

**Full bloom lasts** about 1 week!

---

## Understanding the Forecast

> "How do I check the forecast?"

**Key terms:**
- 開花 (Kaika) – First blooms open
- 満開 (Mankai) – Full bloom (best viewing!)
- 桜吹雪 (Sakura fubuki) – Petals falling

**Check forecasts at:**
- Japan Meteorological Corporation
- Weathernews sakura forecast
- Japan-Guide.com

Forecasts come out in January/February.

---

## Best Viewing Spots

> "Where should I see them?"

**Tokyo:**
- Ueno Park
- Meguro River
- Shinjuku Gyoen
- Chidorigafuchi

**Kyoto:**
- Maruyama Park
- Philosopher's Path
- Arashiyama

**Other famous spots:**
- Hirosaki Castle (Aomori)
- Yoshino (Nara)
- Himeji Castle

---

## Hanami Culture

> "What is hanami?"

**Cherry blossom viewing party:**
- Picnic under the trees
- Eat and drink with friends
- Blue tarps mark spots
- Day and night viewing

**You can join!** Buy food at convenience stores and find a spot.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Check forecasts obsessively
- Plan hanami with friends
- Know their favorite local spots
- Go multiple times during season

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book hotels early** – Sakura season is peak tourist time

2. **Flexible dates help** – Timing changes yearly

3. **Weekdays better** – Less crowded than weekends

4. **Night viewing** – Many spots have illumination

5. **Rain ends it fast** – Petals fall quickly after rain
