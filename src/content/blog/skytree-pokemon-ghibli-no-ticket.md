> "Do I need a Skytree ticket to visit the Pokémon Center and Ghibli Store?"

**No! Tokyo Solamachi is free to enter.** You only need a ticket for the observation deck.

---

## Tokyo Solamachi (Free!)

> "What's in the shopping complex?"

**Tokyo Solamachi** is the shopping mall at the base of Skytree. It's free to enter and has:

**Pokémon Center Skytree Town**
- Official Pokémon merchandise
- Skytree-exclusive items
- Pikachu goods everywhere
- Great for fans of all ages

**Donguri Republic (Ghibli Store)**
- Official Studio Ghibli merchandise
- Totoro, Spirited Away, Kiki's Delivery Service goods
- Plushies, accessories, stationery
- Beautifully designed store

**Other shops:**
- Sumida Aquarium (separate ticket needed)
- Japanese souvenirs and snacks
- Restaurants on multiple floors
- Ultraman shop, J-League shop, and more

---

## Should You Go Up Skytree?

> "Is the observation deck worth it?"

**Honestly?** It depends:
- **Clear day:** Yes, amazing views. You might see Mt. Fuji
- **Cloudy/rainy:** Skip it, you'll see nothing
- **Price:** ¥2,100-3,100 depending on how high you go
- **Evening:** City lights are beautiful

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit Solamachi for shopping without going up Skytree
- Know that the observation deck is mainly for tourists
- Go to the restaurants for the views (free with your meal)
- Visit on weekdays to avoid weekend crowds

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Solamachi is huge** - Budget 1-2 hours for shopping

2. **Pokémon Center has exclusive items** - Different from other locations

3. **Food court on 6F and 7F** - Great variety and views

4. **Combine with Asakusa** - It's a short walk across the river

5. **Go in the evening** - Skytree is beautifully lit up at night (free to see from outside)
