> "Do I need a Skytree ticket to visit the Pokémon Center and Ghibli Store?"

**No ticket needed!** Both stores are in Tokyo Solamachi, which is free to enter.

---

## How It Works

> "Where exactly are they?"

**Tokyo Solamachi** is the shopping mall at the base of Tokyo Skytree. It's open to the public – you only need a ticket if you want to go up the observation deck.

**Store locations (4th floor):**
- **Pokémon Center SkyTree Town**
- **Donguri Kyowakoku (Ghibli Store)**

Both are close to each other!

---

## What Else Is at Solamachi?

> "Is there more to see?"

**Yes! Lots of great shops:**
- Regional specialty stores from all over Japan
- **TORITON** – Famous conveyor belt sushi from Hokkaido
- Food court with many options
- Character goods stores
- Unique souvenirs

You can easily spend 2-3 hours just shopping and eating.

---

## Should I Go Up the Tower?

> "Is the observation deck worth it?"

**It depends:**
- Great views of Tokyo
- Can be crowded and expensive
- Shibuya Sky might be better value

If you just want shopping, skip the ticket!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit Solamachi without going up the tower
- Shop at the character stores
- Eat at the restaurants
- Go up the tower only on special occasions

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Combine with Asakusa** – Very close, easy to walk

2. **Evening is nice** – Tower is lit up beautifully

3. **Weekdays less crowded** – Especially for popular stores

4. **Food basement** – Great for snacks and souvenirs

5. **Sumida Aquarium** – Also in the building (separate ticket)
