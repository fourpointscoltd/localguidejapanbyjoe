> "I just saw an earthquake prediction for Tokyo. Should I be worried about my trip?"

I completely understand the concern. Let me give you a local's honest perspective — I've lived through countless earthquakes in Japan.

## The Truth: Earthquakes Happen Often, but Serious Damage Is Rare

Japan experiences small earthquakes **almost daily**. Most are so minor you won't even notice them. Thanks to some of the **strictest building codes in the world**, modern buildings in Japan are designed to flex and absorb shaking rather than collapse.

Hotels, apartment buildings, train stations, and shopping centers in cities like Tokyo are **built to withstand strong earthquakes**. The infrastructure here is genuinely world-class when it comes to seismic safety.

## What to Actually Watch Out For

### Tsunami Risk (Coastal Areas Only)
If you're near the coast and feel a **long, strong earthquake**, move to higher ground immediately. Follow official alerts and hotel staff instructions.

### Fires After Earthquakes
Gas leaks can cause fires after a quake. Stay alert, and evacuate calmly if necessary.

## Japan's Early Warning System

This is amazing — **your phone will automatically receive earthquake alerts**, even with a foreign SIM card. The alert comes seconds before the shaking reaches you, giving you time to take cover.

### Apps to Download Before Your Trip:
- **Safety Tips** — by Japan Tourism Agency (multilingual)
- **Yurekuru Call** — earthquake early warning
- **NHK World** — live updates in English

## What to Do During an Earthquake

1. **Stay indoors** — don't run outside
2. **Get under a sturdy table** or desk
3. **Stay away from windows** and tall shelves
4. **Hold on** until the shaking stops
5. After it stops, **check for exits** and listen for announcements

## The Local Perspective

Japanese people live with earthquake risk every single day — and life goes on completely normally. Kids go to school, people commute to work, tourists enjoy their vacations. It's something we're prepared for, not something we fear.

**Don't let earthquake anxiety ruin your trip.** Be informed, download the apps, know what to do — and then enjoy Japan.
