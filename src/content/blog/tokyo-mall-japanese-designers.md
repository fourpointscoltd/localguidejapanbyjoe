> "Hello! Which is the best mall in Tokyo that has a lot of Japanese designers? I want to take my 13 yr old daughter shopping. Thank you!"

**If you're exploring Tokyo with a 13-year-old and want to check out some cool Japanese designers, KITTE near Tokyo Station is a fantastic choice.** It's got a great mix of shops that showcase local talent. Plus, it's really convenient because you can just walk over from Tokyo Station. After KITTE, you could also stroll to Midtown Hibiya; it’s nearby and has a neat collection of shops that both of you might find interesting. It’s a fun way to spend the day hopping from one spot to another while discovering unique Japanese fashion!
