> "Let's talk about FREE hotel amenities!
 I understand that hotels provide \"free toiletries\" and pajamas. What does that usually include and are they normal brands? Can someone post a pic of the PJ's or the free loot? Do you get to keep the PJ's or do they re-use them? Are they one size fits all?"

**In Japan, most hotels offer free amenities like slippers, pajamas, toothbrushes, razors, and towels.** Whether you can keep these items depends on the item itself. Usually, you can take disposable items like toothbrushes, razors, and sometimes even slippers. However, pajamas and towels are meant for use only during your stay and should be left in the room when you check out.

---

## Details

It's a good idea to check with the hotel if you are unsure about what you can take home. Some items might have a small fee if you want to keep them, especially if they are reusable. Generally, anything that cannot be cleaned or reused for hygienic reasons is okay for you to take. This ensures everything stays nice and easy for everyone!
