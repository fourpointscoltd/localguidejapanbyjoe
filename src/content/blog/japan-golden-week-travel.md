> "Is it advisable to visit Japan during Golden Week due to higher costs and crowds?"

Japan is an amazing travel destination, but timing your visit can make a huge difference in your experience. One of the most debated periods for travel is **Golden Week**, a national holiday stretch from **April 29 to May 5**. If you're wondering whether it's a good time to visit, here’s everything you need to know.

## **What Is Golden Week?**

Golden Week is one of Japan’s busiest holiday seasons, consisting of four national holidays within a single week. Since most Japanese people get time off, it’s a peak travel season for domestic tourists. This means that **transportation, accommodations, and attractions are significantly more crowded** than usual.

### **The Downsides of Visiting During Golden Week**

While Japan is always a fantastic place to visit, **Golden Week presents several challenges for tourists**:

- **Higher Costs** – Hotels, flights, and even some tourist attractions increase their prices due to high demand. Budget travel during this period is difficult.
- **Crowded Transportation** – Bullet trains (Shinkansen) sell out quickly, and flights to and from Japan are pricier. Highways are also jammed with road trips.
- **Packed Tourist Spots** – Major destinations like Kyoto, Tokyo, and Osaka become **extremely congested**, making sightseeing less enjoyable.

### **When Is a Better Time to Visit Japan?**

If you can, it’s **better to avoid Golden Week** and visit during these alternative periods instead:

- **Late March to early April** – For cherry blossoms with **manageable crowds** (except in famous spots like Kyoto).
- **Mid-May to June** – Right after Golden Week, the weather is pleasant, and crowds thin out.
- **September to November** – For beautiful autumn foliage and comfortable temperatures.

### **Should You Visit Japan During Golden Week?**

If you have **no choice but to visit during Golden Week**, **plan ahead**:
✔ **Book hotels and transportation early** – Ideally **months in advance**.
✔ **Avoid major cities** – Lesser-known places like Tohoku or Kyushu might be less crowded.
✔ **Be flexible with your itinerary** – Expect delays and packed attractions.

If you have the freedom to choose your travel dates, **visiting Japan outside of Golden Week is a much better experience**. The country is just as beautiful—without the stress of crowds and high prices.
