> "What is going on with the suica card? I'm so confused."

**the Suica card situation has gotten a bit tricky recently!** Usually, you'd grab one from the ticket machines at the stations, but now, because of a semiconductor shortage, they've moved sales to the service counters. It's a good idea to pick one up the moment you land—either at Haneda or Narita Airport—to avoid the hassle later. Plus, you can actually download the Suica card directly to your smartphone if you use services like Apple Pay. It's super convenient and means one less card to worry about!
