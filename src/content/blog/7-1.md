> "I have the dumbest question ever. I’ve looked on YouTube and in various groups and can’t seem to find the answer (hence this anonymous post).
When buying one of the fruit smoothies at 7-11, do you pay before or after you put it in the fancy blender machine?"

**At 7-11 in Japan, paying for a fruit smoothie is very straightforward.** First, choose your favorite smoothie from the freezer section. These are usually pre-packaged and ready to blend. After you've picked out your smoothie, carry it to the cashier. Here, you'll pay for your item. The prices are visible on the packaging or on a price tag on the shelf, so you can check before you head to the counter. Once you've paid, you can go to the designated blending machines that are typically located nearby. Just open the lid of your smoothie pack, place it in the machine, and let it do its magic. In just a few minutes, you'll have a freshly blended smoothie to enjoy as you continue your adventures in Japan!
