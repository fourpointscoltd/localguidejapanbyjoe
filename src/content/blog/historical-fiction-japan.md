> "What movies or dramas do you recommend reading and seeing before visiting
 Japan?"

**Before visiting Japan, watching some movies and dramas can really help you understand the culture better.** On Netflix, "Sanctuary" is a great show that gives a glimpse into the world of sumo wrestling. Another good watch is "First Love"; it's set in Japan and offers a good feel of the country's vibe. "Asakusa Kid" is also a great choice to see Japan's entertainment scene in the past.

---

## Details

If you're interested in a bit of historical perspective, "A Man Called Pirate" is a film based on post-war Japan. It's a fantastic way to get a taste of Japanese history through a personal story.

Also, consider following some YouTube channels that focus on Japanese culture and history; they can be very informative and fun to watch!
