> "What's a traditional Japanese breakfast like?"

**Very different from Western breakfast!** It's savory, healthy, and satisfying.

---

## Traditional Components

> "What's included?"

**Typical items:**
- **Rice** – The base of every meal
- **Miso soup** – Warm and comforting
- **Grilled fish** – Usually salmon or mackerel
- **Tamagoyaki** – Sweet rolled omelet
- **Pickles** – Tsukemono varieties
- **Natto** – Fermented soybeans (try it!)
- **Nori** – Dried seaweed sheets
- **Tofu** – Sometimes included

---

## Where to Try It

> "Where can I get traditional breakfast?"

**Best places:**
- Ryokan (traditional inn) – Often included
- Business hotels with Japanese option
- Chains like Yayoi-ken or Ootoya
- Some cafes serve morning sets

---

## Hotel Breakfast Buffets

> "What about hotel buffets?"

**Japanese hotels often have both:**
- Western section (eggs, bread, bacon)
- Japanese section (rice, fish, miso)
- You can mix and match!

---

## Natto – Should You Try It?

> "What's the deal with natto?"

**Fermented soybeans:**
- Very sticky and stringy
- Strong smell and taste
- Very healthy
- Many foreigners don't like it
- But some love it!

**Try it once** – You might be surprised!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Eat simpler breakfast at home (toast, rice)
- Full traditional breakfast is special
- Know natto is an acquired taste
- Appreciate ryokan breakfasts

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Ryokan breakfast** – Book a ryokan for the experience

2. **Convenience store** – Onigiri makes quick breakfast

3. **Coffee shops** – Morning sets are Western-style

4. **Don't skip rice** – It's filling and satisfying

5. **Green tea** – Often served with breakfast
