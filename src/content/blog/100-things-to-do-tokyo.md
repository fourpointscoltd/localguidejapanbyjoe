Planning a trip to Tokyo? With so much to see and do, it can be overwhelming to decide where to start. I've put together the ultimate bucket list of **100 things to do in Tokyo** based on my years of living in and exploring this incredible city. Let's dive in!

## 🏙️ Iconic Tokyo Experiences (1-15)

1. **Visit the Shibuya Crossing** - Stand at the world's busiest pedestrian crossing and feel the energy of Tokyo
2. **Have a beer at a bar in Shibuya** - Experience the nightlife in one of Tokyo's most vibrant districts
3. **Eat Ramen in Takadanobaba** - This student area has some of the best affordable ramen shops
4. **Go shopping in Shinjuku** - From department stores to underground malls, shopping heaven awaits
5. **Enjoy Tokyo's view from the Metropolitan Government Building** - It's FREE and offers stunning panoramic views
6. **Explore Harajuku** - Witness unique fashion, cute cafes, and vibrant street culture
7. **Walk through Kabukicho** - Experience Tokyo's famous entertainment district (safely!)
8. **Shop at Don Quijote** - The ultimate discount store for souvenirs and everything else
9. **Try convenience store food** - Konbini food is surprisingly delicious and affordable
10. **Explore Akihabara** - The mecca for anime, manga, and electronics lovers
11. **Buy a manga or anime figurine** - Even if you're not a fan, it's a unique souvenir
12. **Visit Tokyo Tower** - The classic symbol of Tokyo
13. **Go up Tokyo Skytree** - Japan's tallest structure with incredible views
14. **Stroll through Ueno Market** - Discover local flavors and hidden gems
15. **See pandas at Ueno Zoo** - One of Japan's most beloved attractions

## 🍜 Food Adventures (16-35)

16. **Visit the Ghibli Museum** - A must for Studio Ghibli fans (book tickets in advance!)
17. **Visit the Fujiko F. Fujio Museum** - Doraemon fans will love this
18. **Pay respects at Fujiko F. Fujio's grave** - For the dedicated fans
19. **Have a matcha ice cream cone** - The perfect Japanese treat
20. **Drink a bottle of Ramune** - Experience this iconic Japanese soda

22. **Try all-you-can-eat yakiniku (grilled meat)** - A carnivore's paradise
23. **Eat at 100 yen conveyor belt sushi** - Affordable and fun!
24. **Explore Tokyo Bay area** - Beautiful waterfront views and attractions
25. **Admire the Rainbow Bridge from Odaiba** - Especially beautiful at night
26. **Take the Yurikamome line** - An automated train with stunning views
27. **Take a walk around Odaiba** - Shopping, entertainment, and seaside views
28. **Photo with the Statue of Liberty replica** - A quirky Tokyo landmark
29. **Visit teamLab Borderless or Planets** - Immersive digital art experience

32. **Have a drink at a themed bar** - Tokyo has bars for every interest

34. **Try your luck at UFO catcher machines** - Addictively fun!
35. **Take a scenic train ride on Seibu Shinjuku line** - Beautiful on sunny days

---

## Final Tips

- **Get a Suica/Pasmo card** for easy transportation
- **Download offline maps** - You'll need them
- **Bring comfortable shoes** - You'll walk A LOT
- **Learn basic Japanese phrases** - Locals appreciate the effort
- **Carry cash** - Many places still don't accept cards

Tokyo is a city that never runs out of surprises. Whether you complete 10 or all 100 of these experiences, you're guaranteed to create unforgettable memories.
