> "Do I need an international driver's license to rent a car in Japan?"

**Yes!** You'll need an International Driving Permit (IDP) and your passport.

---

## Important Warning

> "Does my IDP work in Japan?"

**IDPs aren't valid for every country!**

Double-check if yours is recognized in Japan before your trip. Some countries' permits are not accepted.

---

## Three Things to Watch Out For

> "What should I be careful about?"

**I've driven abroad, so let me share important warnings:**

**1. Left-Hand Traffic**
- Everything is reversed!
- Wipers and turn signals are swapped
- Easy to drive on the wrong side when leaving parking lots
- This causes many accidents

**2. Police Are Everywhere**
- Many rules about stopping at "stop" signs
- No-entry zones to avoid
- Speed limits are lower than many countries (40-60 km/h on regular roads)
- Watch your speed to avoid fines!

**3. Don't Drive in Downtown Kyoto**
- Streets are old and narrow
- Packed with pedestrians, cyclists, buses, taxis
- Very stressful driving
- Not worth it!

---

## Why Renting a Car Is Great

> "Should I still rent a car?"

**Yes! Despite the warnings, it's amazing!**

Benefits:
- Perfect for hot summers, cold winters, sudden rain
- Rural buses are infrequent
- Freedom to explore at your own pace
- Best way to see countryside Japan

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Know the strict traffic rules
- Always stop completely at stop signs
- Avoid driving in Kyoto
- Love road trips in the countryside

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Get IDP before you travel** – Can't get it in Japan

2. **Rent from major companies** – Toyota, Nissan, etc.

3. **GPS is included** – Usually in English

4. **Rural areas are easy** – Less traffic

5. **Avoid big cities** – Use trains instead
