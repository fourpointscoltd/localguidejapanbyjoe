> "Everyone says get to Fushimi Inari Gates early to avoid crowds. What time do you consider early? When do the big crowds usually arrive? Going first week of November."

**Absolutely, early mornings are golden at Fushimi Inari Taisha!** Try to be there around sunrise, which is about 6:30 or 7 AM in November. It's quieter, and you can soak up the serene vibe without much crowd. The morning light also creates a beautiful scene, perfect for photos. If you're not an early riser, arriving by 8 AM is still pretty good. But once it hits 10 AM, the tour buses roll in and it can get pretty busy until the afternoon. Early morning is definitely your best bet for a peaceful visit!
