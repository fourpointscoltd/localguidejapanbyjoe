> "Are Japanese drugstores worth visiting?"

**Yes!** Japanese drugstores have amazing beauty and health products. Many tourists stock up here!

---

## Popular Chains

> "Which stores should I visit?"

**Major drugstores:**
- **Matsumoto Kiyoshi** – Most famous, yellow signs
- **Sugi Drug** – Good selection
- **Welcia** – Open late
- **Cocokara Fine** – Common in cities
- **Sundrug** – Budget-friendly

---

## Best Products to Buy

> "What should I get?"

**Skincare:**
- Hada Labo lotions
- Biore sunscreen (the best!)
- Sheet masks
- DHC lip cream
- Kose products

**Medicine:**
- EVE (pain relief)
- Salonpas patches
- Eye drops (Rohto)
- Stomach medicine

**Beauty:**
- False eyelashes
- Makeup removers
- Hair treatments
- Japanese cosmetics

---

## Tax-Free Shopping

> "Can I get tax free?"

**Yes!**
- Spend ¥5,500+
- Show passport
- Items in sealed bag
- Don't open until you leave Japan

---

## Prices

> "Is it expensive?"

**Generally affordable:**
- Sunscreen: ¥500-1,500
- Face masks: ¥300-1,000
- Medicine: ¥500-2,000
- Cosmetics: Varies

**Compare:** Prices differ between stores!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Shop at drugstores regularly
- Know which products are best
- Stock up during sales
- Trust Japanese quality

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Biore sunscreen** – Number one tourist purchase

2. **Don Quijote vs Drugstore** – Compare prices

3. **Staff recommendations** – Ask for help

4. **Point cards** – Available but not worth it for tourists

5. **Multiple stores** – Visit a few to compare
