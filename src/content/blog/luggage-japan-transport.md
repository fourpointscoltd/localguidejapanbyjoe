> "is that ok if I bring my luggage with me in the public transport? I am not planning to hire a transportation company to my luggage."

**Absolutely, you can bring your luggage onto trains, buses, and taxis here in Japan.** Most public transports are quite accommodating. Just a heads-up, some busy trains and buses might have specific rules about luggage during peak hours, so checking these in advance can be handy. and guess what? You can even bring along items like bicycles or large musical instruments, as long as they're properly covered. So, don't stress too much about moving around with your belongings—it's pretty straightforward!
