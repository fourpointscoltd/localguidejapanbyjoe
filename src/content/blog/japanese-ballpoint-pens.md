**If you're into pens, you definitely want to check out Itoya in Ginza; it's pretty much the go-to spot for the best stationery in Japan.** But if you're pressed for time, you won't go wrong popping into a bookstore or stores like Loft and Tokyu Hands—they've got a great selection too. Japanese stationery is top-notch, really. And a little secret spot—KAKIMORI in Kuramae, close to Asakusa. They have an amazing collection of notebooks and fountain pens. It’s totally worth a visit!
