> "Does Disney allow visitors to bring own refillable water bottles or do we have to buy all food and drinks inside?"

**Absolutely, you can bring your refillable water bottles to Japan—super handy and eco-friendly, right?** Just remember, cans, glass bottles, and alcoholic drinks are a no-go. But your plastic bottles or thermoses are totally fine. and about snacks, keep it light, like some nuts or a granola bar. For anything more specific, just take a quick peek at the official website of the places you plan to visit. They'll have all the details you need!
