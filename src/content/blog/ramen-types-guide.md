> "What are the different types of ramen? Which should I try?"

Japan has many ramen styles! Here's your guide to the main types.

---

## Main Ramen Types

> "What are the basic categories?"

**By broth type:**

**Tonkotsu (Pork bone)**
- Creamy, white, rich
- From Fukuoka (Hakata)
- Popular: Ichiran, Ippudo

**Shoyu (Soy sauce)**
- Clear brown broth
- Classic Tokyo style
- Lighter than tonkotsu

**Miso**
- Originally from Hokkaido
- Thick, savory flavor
- Warming in winter

**Shio (Salt)**
- Clear, light broth
- Delicate flavor
- Good for beginners

---

## Regional Specialties

> "What about local styles?"

**Famous regional ramen:**
- **Hakata** (Fukuoka) – Thin noodles, tonkotsu broth
- **Sapporo** (Hokkaido) – Miso with butter and corn
- **Kitakata** – Flat, curly noodles, shoyu broth
- **Tokyo** – Shoyu-based, medium noodles

Each region is proud of their style!

---

## How to Order

> "What do I need to know?"

**Customization options:**
- Noodle firmness (soft/normal/firm)
- Broth richness (light/normal/rich)
- Garlic amount
- Extra toppings (egg, chashu, nori)

**Ticket machines:** Many ramen shops use them – buy your ticket first, then sit down.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Have a favorite ramen style
- Slurp noodles (it's polite!)
- Eat quickly – ramen is fast food
- Try different shops to compare

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Slurp loudly** – It's expected and cools the noodles

2. **Don't let it sit** – Eat while hot, noodles get soggy

3. **Free toppings** – Garlic, ginger, spice often available

4. **Kaedama** – Extra noodles for your remaining broth

5. **Lunch is best** – Less crowded, same quality
