> "What's the best aquarium in Tokyo for kids?"

**I have several recommendations!** Each has different features.

---

## My Top Picks

> "Which should I visit?"

**For a big aquarium with lots of time:**
- **Aqua Park Shinagawa** – Spacious and fun

**For a quick, casual visit:**
- **SUMIDA Aquarium** – At Tokyo Skytree

**For the best overall:**
- **Hakkeijima Sea Paradise** – Biggest in Kanto area!

---

## Hakkeijima Sea Paradise

> "Why is this the best?"

**Located in Kanagawa (next to Tokyo):**
- Biggest aquarium in the Kanto area
- High-level performance shows
- Great for spending a full day
- Worth the trip!

---

## Other Options

> "What about other aquariums?"

**SUMIDA Aquarium:**
- Inside Tokyo Skytree
- Convenient location
- Good for short visit

**Aqua Park Shinagawa:**
- Near Shinagawa Station
- Modern and spacious
- Nice shows

---

## Special Mention: Okinawa

> "What about outside Tokyo?"

**If you go to Okinawa:**
- **DMM Kariyushi Aquarium** – Very unique experience!
- **Churaumi Aquarium** – Big but I found it less enjoyable

I really love Okinawa for aquariums!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Take kids to aquariums on weekends
- Know the best local spots
- Combine with other activities
- Visit during off-peak times

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Hakkeijima is worth the trip** – Best shows and size

2. **Weekdays are less crowded** – Better for kids

3. **SUMIDA is convenient** – Combine with Skytree visit

4. **Check show times** – Plan around performances

5. **Bring snacks** – Kids get hungry!
