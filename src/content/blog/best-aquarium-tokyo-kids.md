> "Which aquarium is best for kids in Tokyo?"

**Maxell Aqua Park Shinagawa is my top pick** for families. But it depends on your kids' ages.

---

## Top Aquariums for Kids

> "What are the best options?"

**Maxell Aqua Park Shinagawa**
- Best dolphin show in Tokyo
- Interactive light displays kids love
- Easy to reach (Shinagawa Station)
- Good for ages 3+
- Takes about 2 hours

**Sumida Aquarium (Tokyo Skytree)**
- Penguins are the star attraction
- Compact and easy to navigate with strollers
- Combine with Skytree visit
- Good for all ages
- Takes about 1-1.5 hours

**Sunshine Aquarium (Ikebukuro)**
- Rooftop penguin exhibit is unique
- Good variety of marine life
- Located in Sunshine City mall (food and shopping nearby)
- Good for ages 2+
- Takes about 1.5-2 hours

---

## For a Day Trip

> "What about outside central Tokyo?"

**Enoshima Aquarium (Fujisawa)**
- Larger than Tokyo aquariums
- Jellyfish exhibit is stunning
- Can combine with Enoshima Island and beach
- About 1 hour from Shinjuku
- Great for a full day out

**Hakkeijima Sea Paradise (Yokohama)**
- Theme park + aquarium combo
- Rides and animal shows
- Best for kids who want more than just fish
- About 1.5 hours from central Tokyo

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Visit Sunshine Aquarium because it's easy to combine with shopping
- Go to Shinagawa Aqua Park for date nights (beautiful lighting)
- Take kids to Hakkeijima for a full day of fun
- Buy annual passes if they live nearby

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Weekdays are much less crowded** - Avoid weekends if possible

2. **Dolphin shows fill up** - Arrive 15-20 minutes early for seats

3. **Strollers are OK** - All Tokyo aquariums are stroller-friendly

4. **Gift shops are tempting** - Budget for stuffed animals your kids will want

5. **Combine with nearby attractions** - Each aquarium is near other fun spots
