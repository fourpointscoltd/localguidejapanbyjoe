> "Hi! I am a huge perfume fan, currently in Japan. Was wondering you know the perfume/ body spray that most of the ladies use here day to day ( there is this amazing soft smell I feel most of the time when one pass by ©) . TIA!"

**In Japan, you'll find that a lot of young folks and beauty enthusiasts go for brand-name perfumes, and lately, Aesop from Australia is all the rage.** It's not Japanese, but worth checking out FUEGUIA 1833 Ginza—located in Ginza Six. If you're into something a bit more personalized, there's this cool shop in Nakameguro where you can get custom-made perfumes. and if you're leaning more towards home fragrances, definitely don't miss Kako in Kuramae. It's a great spot to explore unique scents!
