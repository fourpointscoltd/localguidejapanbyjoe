> "Is it worth to have a super expensive sushi dinner ($150-250pp USD) vs just having sushi at any normal sushi place?"

**Absolutely, if it fits your budget, experiencing both high-end and regular sushi places can be quite an adventure in Japan.** High-end sushi offers an exceptional experience with premium ingredients like luxurious sea urchin, which can be a real treat. On the other hand, places like Sushiro or Kura Sushi are wallet-friendly and also super fun with their conveyor belts! You should definitely try to book a spot at both types of places, it's like a mini sushi tour! It’s a great way to taste the spectrum of what sushi in Japan has to offer.
