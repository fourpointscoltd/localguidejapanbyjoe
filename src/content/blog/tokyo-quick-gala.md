> "What’s the best ski resort close to Tokyo? Looking for one with easy access to the ski resort using public transportation and one that has hotels/accommodations close by. Thank you."

**Gala Yuzawa is a fantastic choice if you're short on time.** Just hop on the Shinkansen from Tokyo and you’ll be hitting the slopes in about an hour. It’s super convenient. And guess what? If you have a few days and want a different experience, consider Hokkaido. The snow there is like powdered sugar—so soft and perfect for skiing! Plus, it’s known for being a bit safer due to its fluffy texture. Both spots offer unique experiences depending on your travel plans!
