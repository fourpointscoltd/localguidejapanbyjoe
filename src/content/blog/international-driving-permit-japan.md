> "Do I need an international driver's license to rent a car in Japan?"

Yes! When you rent a car in Japan, you need:

- **International Driving Permit (IDP)** from your country
- **Your passport**
- **Your regular driver's license**

But wait – not all IDPs work in Japan. Check if yours is accepted before your trip!

---

## Important Things to Know

> "What should I watch out for when driving?"

I've driven abroad, and there are three big things you need to know about driving in Japan:

### 1. Left-Side Traffic

Japan drives on the **left side** of the road. Everything is reversed:
- The steering wheel is on the right
- Wipers and turn signals are swapped
- When leaving a parking lot, it's easy to accidentally drive on the wrong side

**Be extra careful** when pulling out from convenience stores or parking lots!

### 2. Lots of Rules (and Police)

In cities like Tokyo, police are everywhere. Watch out for:
- **Stop signs** – You must completely stop
- **Speed limits** – 40-60 km/h on regular roads, 80-100 km/h on highways
- **No-entry zones** – These are easy to miss

Speed limits are **lower than many countries**. Check your speed often!

### 3. Don't Drive in Downtown Kyoto

Seriously. The streets are:
- Old and narrow
- Full of pedestrians, cyclists, buses, and taxis
- Not designed for modern cars
- Very stressful to navigate

Use public transport in Kyoto. Trust me.

## Where Renting a Car Makes Sense

> "Is driving worth it at all?"

**Yes!** Driving is amazing in:

- **Rural areas** – Buses are rare, driving gives you freedom
- **Hokkaido** – Huge distances, beautiful scenery
- **Countryside** – Small towns, hidden gems
- **Mountain areas** – Hot springs, nature spots

The car protects you from Japan's hot summers, cold winters, and sudden rain. It's the best way to explore off the beaten path!

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Don't drive in big cities (parking is expensive, trains are better)
- Rent cars for countryside trips
- Use rental companies at train stations (very convenient!)
- Book through Toyota, Nissan, or Times Car Rental

## Where to Rent

Rental car companies are easy to find:
- **Toyota Rent a Car**
- **Nissan Rent a Car**
- **Times Car Rental**
- **Orix Rent a Car**

Many have locations right at train stations!

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Get your IDP before leaving home** – You can't get one in Japan

2. **Book in advance** – Especially during holidays

3. **GPS is included** – Most rental cars have navigation (set it to English)

4. **Highways have tolls** – They can add up quickly

5. **Gas stations are full-service** – Staff will fill your tank for you

6. **Return with a full tank** – Otherwise you'll pay extra
