> "What should I check out at a Japanese supermarket?"

**Japanese supermarkets are amazing!** Here's what to explore.

---

## Must-See Sections

> "What sections are unique?"

**Bento section:**
- Fresh made daily
- Great value (¥300-600)
- Variety of options
- Discounted in evening!

**Sashimi/Sushi:**
- Fresh and affordable
- Much cheaper than restaurants
- Great for hotel room dinners

**Onigiri:**
- Rice balls with fillings
- Perfect snack
- ¥100-150 each

---

## Evening Discounts

> "When are things on sale?"

**Look for discount stickers:**
- 20% off (2割引)
- 30% off (3割引)
- 50% off (半額 hangaku)

**Best time:** 7-9 PM for biggest discounts on prepared foods!

---

## Interesting Items to Try

> "What unique things should I buy?"

**Snacks:**
- Japanese Kit Kats (regional flavors)
- Rice crackers (senbei)
- Mochi snacks

**Drinks:**
- Japanese tea varieties
- Canned coffee
- Fruit drinks

**Ingredients:**
- Instant dashi
- Japanese mayonnaise
- Furikake (rice seasoning)

---

## Major Supermarket Chains

> "Which supermarkets exist?"

**Common chains:**
- **Aeon** – Large, everywhere
- **Ito-Yokado** – Good quality
- **Life** – Urban locations
- **Maruetsu** – Tokyo area
- **Summit** – Good selection

**Don Quijote** is not a supermarket but has grocery sections!

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Shop in the evening for discounts
- Buy bento for quick dinners
- Know weekly sale patterns
- Bring reusable bags

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Bring a bag** – Plastic bags cost money (¥3-5)

2. **Self-checkout** – Common, easy to use

3. **Tax included** – Prices shown include tax

4. **Evening shopping** – Best discounts

5. **Try the deli** – Hot food section is great
