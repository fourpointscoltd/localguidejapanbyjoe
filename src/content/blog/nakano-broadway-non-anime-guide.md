Absolutely! While Nakano Broadway is famous for anime and manga shops, there's actually a surprising variety of non-anime stores that make it worth visiting.

## Beyond Anime

### Vintage Watches
Nakano Broadway has several excellent vintage watch shops with Rolex, Omega, and Japanese brands at competitive prices.

### Rare Sneakers
Sneakerheads will find specialty shops selling limited edition and vintage sneakers.

### Unique Food
- **Daily Chico** - Famous for their ice cream tower with flavors like wasabi and natto!
- **Vintage candy shops** - Nostalgic Japanese sweets from decades past

### Antiques & Collectibles
- Vintage toys (not just anime)
- Retro electronics
- Old cameras and equipment
- Vinyl records

## The Building Itself

Nakano Broadway is a fascinating 4-floor shopping complex that feels like a time capsule. Even if you're not buying, the atmosphere is unique and very "old Japan."

## Getting There

Just 5 minutes from Shinjuku on the JR Chuo Line. Exit at Nakano Station and walk through the covered shopping arcade.

## My Recommendation

Even if anime isn't your thing, spend 1-2 hours exploring. You'll find:
- Quirky local shops
- Great people-watching
- Affordable lunch spots
- Unique souvenirs you won't find elsewhere

It's a genuine slice of Japanese subculture that goes way beyond anime!
