> "Which is better for 1 night after Shirakawa-go: Kanazawa or Takayama?"

This is a tough question! Both are amazing. Here's my breakdown.

---

## Quick Answer

> "Just tell me which one!"

**For 1 night → Takayama is the easy win**

**Staying longer → Kanazawa has more to offer**

---

## Takayama

> "Why choose Takayama?"

**Pros:**
- Much closer to Shirakawa-go (about 1 hour by bus)
- Small, quiet, traditional atmosphere
- Beautiful Edo-period streets
- Famous for Hida beef
- Perfect for a short stay

**Best for:**
- 1-night trips
- People who want a relaxed, traditional vibe
- Hida beef lovers

---

## Kanazawa

> "Why choose Kanazawa?"

**Pros:**
- Bigger city with more to do
- Kenrokuen Garden (one of Japan's top 3)
- Kanazawa Castle
- Omicho Market (amazing seafood!)
- 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art
- Better for 2-3 nights

**Best for:**
- Longer stays
- Sushi and seafood lovers
- Art and garden enthusiasts

---

## Distance from Shirakawa-go

| Destination | Travel Time |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Takayama** | ~1 hour by bus |
| **Kanazawa** | ~1.5 hours by bus |

Both are easy to reach, but Takayama is closer.

---

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Stay in Takayama for quick Shirakawa-go trips
- Visit Kanazawa as a separate destination
- Combine all three over 3-4 days if possible

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **Book buses early** – Especially in autumn and winter

2. **Takayama morning markets** – Don't miss them!

3. **Kanazawa sushi** – Among the best in Japan

4. **Hida beef** – Must-try in Takayama

5. **If you have time** – Do both!
