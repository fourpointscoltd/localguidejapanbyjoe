> "Where can I buy a Katana? Sword?"

**If you're looking to buy a katana, SEIYUDO is a famous spot definitely worth checking out.** They have a variety of swords, from ornamental to the real deal. Before you get too excited, make sure to peek at their website to see what catches your eye. Just a heads-up, remember to look into Japan's regulations on transporting swords, particularly if you plan to bring it back home. Carrying a katana around without proper cause can lead to trouble with the police here. So, a little bit of homework before you buy could save you a lot of hassle!
