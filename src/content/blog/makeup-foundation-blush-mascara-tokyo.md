### 💡 **Wait, What Is @cosme?**

@cosme (pronounced “at-cosme”) is **Japan’s biggest beauty review platform**. It’s like Japan’s version of Sephora meets Reddit—users rank products based on real reviews.
There’s even a **physical store (@cosme TOKYO)** where they only stock **top-rated, award-winning items** from the site. If you’re unsure what to buy, this place is your best bet.
