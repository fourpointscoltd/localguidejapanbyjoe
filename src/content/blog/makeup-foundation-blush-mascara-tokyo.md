> "Where can I find good foundation, blush, and mascara in Tokyo?"

**@cosme TOKYO in Harajuku is your one-stop destination!** It's Japan's biggest beauty review site turned into a real store.

---

## @cosme TOKYO (Harajuku)

> "What makes this store special?"

**Japan's #1 beauty destination:**
- Products ranked by real user reviews
- Testers available for almost everything
- Staff can help match your skin tone
- Tax-free shopping for tourists
- Located in Harajuku (easy to reach)

**Best-selling categories:**
- Japanese foundation (known for natural, dewy finish)
- Blush (Japanese brands excel at subtle color)
- Mascara (Japanese mascaras are famous for hold and volume)

---

## Top Japanese Beauty Brands to Try

> "Which brands should I look for?"

**Foundation:**
- KATE - Affordable, great coverage
- SUQQU - Premium, beautiful finish
- RMK - Natural, lightweight

**Blush:**
- CANMAKE - Adorable packaging, great colors, very affordable
- JILL STUART - Beautiful, giftable
- NARS (Japan exclusive colors)

**Mascara:**
- Heroine Make - Legendary for waterproof hold
- Dejavu - "Film type" that won't smudge
- Majolica Majorca - Fun packaging, great performance

---

## Other Great Stores

> "Where else can I shop?"

**Drugstores (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Sundrug)**
- Cheapest prices for Japanese cosmetics
- Tax-free available
- Great for CANMAKE, KATE, Majolica Majorca

**Department store counters**
- For SUQQU, THREE, Clé de Peau
- Get a professional color match
- Samples available

## What Japanese People Actually Do

Most locals:
- Check @cosme rankings before buying any beauty product
- Stock up on CANMAKE and KATE at drugstores
- Consider Heroine Make mascara a holy grail product
- Buy limited edition seasonal colors

---

## Extra Travel Tips

1. **@cosme rankings change monthly** - Check current #1 products

2. **Testers are everywhere** - Don't be shy about trying products

3. **Japanese skin tones run light** - Some foundations may not match darker skin

4. **Drugstore quality is amazing** - ¥500-1,500 products rival luxury brands

5. **Tax-free minimum** - Usually ¥5,000+ in one store for tax exemption
